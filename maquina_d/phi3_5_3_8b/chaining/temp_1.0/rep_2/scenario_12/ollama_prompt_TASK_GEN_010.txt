The mission objective for our Earth-observation satellite over Colombia is to carry out a non-essential background filler task with flexible scheduling. Specifically targeting the city of San Alberto, this request involves an opportunistic capture using near-infrared reflection capabilities primarily aimed at water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. We seek to discriminate gradients in these critical environmental components without exerting pressure on our standard operational resources or deadlines. The required task must be conducted the day after tomorrow, in the morning—this upper limit is crucial for us to manage data freshness and availability efficiently. Given its low priority importance, this request may drop or reschedule at convenience; however, it serves as a valuable supplementary activity within our broader environmental monitoring efforts.

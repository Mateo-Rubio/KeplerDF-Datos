This is a standard operational tasking for an NIR (near-infrared reflection) multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance. The operation focuses on Mulya Bakti in Indonesia, where we seek high-quality data within three days overnight deadline. This routine monitoring is essential for establishing baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters, contributing to our usual workflow updates and vegetation health assessments across the region.

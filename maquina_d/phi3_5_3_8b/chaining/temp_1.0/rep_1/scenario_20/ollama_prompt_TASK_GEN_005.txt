This is a standard operational tasking for Pavullo nel Frignano in Italy to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis at around mid-day within three days. The objective involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance measurements, using near-infrared reflection capabilities of our satellite assets. This task is an integral part of our baseline pipeline tracking for monitoring vegetative health over this specific locale within the region.

As part of our routine monitoring to ensure continuous coverage over key areas, assign a commercial task for multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection. This is aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance measurements in Rheinwald, Switzerland, specifically around mid-day within four days from today's date. The findings will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking setup, helping us maintain an accurate understanding of regional vegetation health as part of environmental monitoring efforts.

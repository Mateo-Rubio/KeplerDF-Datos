As part of our routine monitoring to maintain a comprehensive baseline pipeline tracking for environmental changes, we are tasking an automated satellite constellation planner with the following highly realistic operational request: Collect near-infrared reflection data during tomorrow afternoon in Norte. This mission is scheduled as a standard operational task, aimed at water body boundary mapping and assessing soil moisture to discriminate gradients within this region of interest. The resulting dataset will enhance our understanding of the local ecosystem's dynamics and contribute valuable insights into climate-related changes that impact both natural resources and human activities in Norte.

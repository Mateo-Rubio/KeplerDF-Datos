Request Submission for Low Priority Opportunistic NIR Reflection Task in La Chapa, Colombia

As the operator of this Earth-observation mission, I seek to conduct a non-essential background survey focused on near-infrared reflection tasks. Specifically, our objective is to assess water body boundaries and soil moisture gradients within La Chapa's surroundings. This task carries low operational importance and therefore allows for flexibility in scheduling; however, we aim to execute this mission by the afternoon of three days from today. The data gathered will not be time-critical but can offer valuable insights into regional environmental conditions, particularly concerning aquatic systems and vegetative water uptake areas that may present gradients indicative of ecological health or agricultural practices in play.

We are tasked with executing a standard operational SAR scan of Medio Baudó city in Colombia. This active radar imaging is essential for capturing the ground's geometry and penetrating through any heavy cloud cover expected tomorrow during the afternoon, as part of our routine monitoring to maintain an up-to-date baseline pipeline tracking system. The high-resolution microwave surface imagery obtained will be pivotal in understanding terrain features that are not visible with optical sensors due to weather conditions or vegetation density.

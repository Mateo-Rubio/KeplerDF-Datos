This is an absolute institutional priority mission requiring immediate action to capture a high-resolution thermal mapping of Norte in Italia. The goal is to document surface temperature profiles and detect any potential heat anomalies within the region, utilizing passive radar scanning with microwave technology for penetrating heavy cloud cover and providing clear insight into hidden ground structures. Given the critical nature of this task, it is bound by a mandatory directive that must be executed flawlessly to ensure operational security and scientific integrity. The data acquisition window tightly adheres to three days in the evening, which aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking for continuous monitoring under natural light conditions when thermal contrasts are most pronounced.

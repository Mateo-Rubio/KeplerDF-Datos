In three days in the morning, we have a standard operational tasking to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis over Bangka Selatan, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and will involve capturing near-infrared reflection data for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels as well as calculating the NDVI index and surface reflectance. The objective is to discriminate gradients effectively within this region's agricultural landscape, ensuring we maintain a comprehensive understanding of vegetation health and soil moisture content under normal operational statuses.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasked with submitting a request for data acquisition from our automated satellite constellation. The objective is to conduct an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging over the city of Kuala Enok in Indonesia. This operation will penetrate through any cloud cover and provide detailed ground geometry, which is essential for understanding the local topography and urban development patterns. Given its low operational priority, this task can be scheduled flexibly; however, it must be completed within four days in the morning to meet our data collection cycle requirements. The information gathered will serve as a supplementary dataset that complements higher-priority missions but remains valuable for comprehensive environmental monitoring and urban planning efforts.

In accordance with our routine monitoring schedule for Indonesia's Kepulauan Meranti region, we are tasked to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance measurements using near-infrared reflection capabilities. The objective is set for execution within four days, specifically in the morning hours to ensure optimal light conditions for our high-resolution optical snapshot requirements. This standard operational tasking aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking and will contribute valuable data towards understanding vegetation health and dynamics in this area of interest.

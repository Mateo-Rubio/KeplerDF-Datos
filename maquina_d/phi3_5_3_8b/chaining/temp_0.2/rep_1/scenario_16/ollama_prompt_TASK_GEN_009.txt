As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasking a routine monitoring operation for multispectral vegetation analysis in Bukit Mabong, Malaysia. This standard operational tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and requires the satellite constellation to capture near-infrared reflection data during the afternoon period the day after tomorrow. The objective here is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance analysis, which will contribute significantly to our understanding of local ecosystem dynamics under normal operational conditions.

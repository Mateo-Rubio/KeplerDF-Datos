As part of our routine monitoring to ensure comprehensive Earth observation coverage, we are initiating a mission with an SAR sensor for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging. The objective is to penetrate cloud cover and accurately capture the ground geometry in Poggi del Sasso, Italy. This task has been designated as low priority importance; hence, it can be adjusted flexibly within our operational schedule. We aim for this operation to contribute valuable data for environmental studies and landscape mapping. The deliverables are expected no later than the day after tomorrow in the morning, ensuring timely integration into our ongoing analysis without disrupting higher-priority missions.

In accordance with our standard operational tasking procedures, we are initiating a routine monitoring mission for multispectral vegetation analysis in the city of Banyuasin, located within Indonesia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) alongside surface reflectance measurements. This tasking falls under a standard operational status with fixed parameters as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The data acquisition window is set for three days from now, specifically around mid-day to ensure optimal light conditions for the high-resolution optical snapshot required for true-color visual documentation and vegetation health assessment.

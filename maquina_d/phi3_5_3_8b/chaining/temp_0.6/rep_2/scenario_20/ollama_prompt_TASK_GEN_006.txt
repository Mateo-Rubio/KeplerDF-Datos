Request for Thermal Mapping in Sekadau, Indonesia with a Standard Operational Tasking. The objective is to capture high-resolution thermal images of the region during afternoon hours on the day after tomorrow, using active radar microwave surface imaging technology to delineate heat signatures and assess temperature variations across different terrains and structures within Sekadau. This task is essential for monitoring environmental changes and potential anomalies in surface temperatures that could indicate underlying issues requiring further investigation. Compliance with the baseline pipeline tracking setup will ensure a continuous flow of data, allowing us to maintain an updated understanding of thermal dynamics in this sector.

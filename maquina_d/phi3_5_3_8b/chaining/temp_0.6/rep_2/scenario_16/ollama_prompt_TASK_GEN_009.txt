This is to formally task an automated satellite constellation planner with conducting a standard operational tasking focused on multispectral vegetation analysis in Bukit Mabong, Malaysia. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), alongside surface reflectance measurements using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This routine monitoring aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup for agricultural health assessment, scheduled precisely for the day after tomorrow during the afternoon hours to ensure data collection within optimal light conditions and minimal atmospheric interference.

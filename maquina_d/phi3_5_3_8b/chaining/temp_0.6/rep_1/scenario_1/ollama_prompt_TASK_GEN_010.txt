This is an urgent operational execution to secure a high-resolution optical snapshot for true-color visual documentation of multispectral vegetation in El Castillo, Colombia. This routine monitoring task requires the collection of data within three days from precisely during the afternoon hours and should be considered as non-essential background filler work that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The primary objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), which will also aid in soil moisture gradient discrimination through near-infrared reflection analysis.

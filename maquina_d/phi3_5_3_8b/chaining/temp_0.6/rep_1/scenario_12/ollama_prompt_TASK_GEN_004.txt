In accordance with standard operational tasking requirements for routine monitoring, we are initiating a mission to capture thermal mapping of the urban heat island effect in La Paz, Colombia. The data collected is essential for our baseline pipeline tracking setup, which requires a steady workflow update with fixed parameters representing the normal operational status of urban thermal dynamics monitoring in this region.

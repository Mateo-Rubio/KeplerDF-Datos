As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am submitting a task to be integrated into our automated satellite constellation planner. The request is for the collection of near-infrared reflection data over Indonesia's equatorial region with specific operational objectives: assessing soil moisture and discriminating gradients in water bodies. This constitutes a non-essential background filler task, which can be rescheduled at our convenience due to its low priority importance.

This is an absolute institutional priority to conduct a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography in Murung Raya, Indonesia. The task involves multispectral vegetation analysis with near-infrared reflection techniques aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI). This mandatory directive is critical for evaluating surface reflectance and requires urgent operational execution within three days overnight. The data collected will be instrumental in monitoring environmental conditions, providing insights into plant health and water body boundaries essential for our ecological studies.

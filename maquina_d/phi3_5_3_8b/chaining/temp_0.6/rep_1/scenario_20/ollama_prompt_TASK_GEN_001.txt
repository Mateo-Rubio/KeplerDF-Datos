As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am formally directing our automated satellite constellation planner to execute a standard operational tasking. The objective is multispectral vegetation analysis in Colombia's city of San Carlos de Guaroa. Our aim is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index, as well as surface reflectance during an overnight three-day window. This routine monitoring will serve as a key component within our baseline pipeline tracking system, ensuring normal operational status with fixed parameters aligned for comprehensive environmental insight collection.

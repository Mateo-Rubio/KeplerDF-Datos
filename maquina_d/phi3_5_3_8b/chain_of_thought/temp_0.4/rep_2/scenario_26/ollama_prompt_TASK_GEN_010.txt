In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols and within the framework of routine monitoring for our Earth-observation mission, we are issuing a directive for a SAR (active radar scan) to be conducted over Colombia's city of Puerto López. This operation is scheduled for three days from now during the evening hours, with an emphasis on capturing cloud-penetrating microwave surface imagery that will provide us with valuable insights into ground geometry and other physical phenomena beneath heavy weather conditions. The data acquired through this mission is expected to contribute significantly to our baseline pipeline tracking system by offering a comprehensive understanding of the area's topography and environmental characteristics during nighttime hours when traditional optical sensors are less effective.

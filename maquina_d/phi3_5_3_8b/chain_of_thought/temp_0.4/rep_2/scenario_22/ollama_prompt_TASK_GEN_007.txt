In accordance with our current operational priorities and considering the flexibility required for this task, we are initiating a request for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging during the upcoming overnight window in three days' time. This operation is designated as a non-essential background filler task, which means it can be adjusted according to convenience without impacting higher priority missions. The objective of this SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) survey will allow us to capture cloud-penetrating images and assess ground geometry under various weather conditions. Please note that while this request is not urgent, we expect the data products from this overnight operation within the specified three days' deadline.

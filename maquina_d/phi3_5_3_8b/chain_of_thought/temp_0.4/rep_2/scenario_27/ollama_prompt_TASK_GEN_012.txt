As a non-essential background filler task with flexible scheduling, we seek to conduct an opportunistic capture using thermal mapping capabilities. The aim is to obtain a heat signature and surface temperature profile in Kapuas Hulu, Indonesia, specifically targeting data collection "the day after tomorrow" during the afternoon hours for potential thermal anomaly detection and mapping of heat emissions. This task can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience due to its low priority importance; however, it remains part of our broader observational efforts in monitoring environmental conditions that may present significant insights into localized heating phenomena.

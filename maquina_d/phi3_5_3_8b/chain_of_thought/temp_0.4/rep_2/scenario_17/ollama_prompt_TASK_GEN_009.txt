In accordance with our current objectives for routine monitoring and baseline pipeline tracking, we are scheduling a SAR mission over Indonesia's Equatorial region within three days during the morning hours. This tasking is designed to provide an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging capabilities of our satellite constellation. The primary goal here is to capture cloud-penetrating images and assess ground geometry, which will contribute valuable data for environmental studies and natural resource management in this region. Given the nature of SAR technology, we anticipate that this operation can proceed even under adverse weather conditions. However, please note that due to its low operational priority, this request is flexible and may be rescheduled at your convenience without impacting higher-priority missions.

As a standard operational tasking, we are requesting today's high-resolution optical snapshot during the nighttime period to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis in Setiris, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), as well as surface reflectance using near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping or soil moisture assessment, discriminating gradients effectively.

As a matter of absolute institutional priority with a binding contract requirement for critical payload capture, we are tasking an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to be executed in Remedios, Colombia. This operation is scheduled for four days hence and specifically targets the morning window. It is imperative that this mission lock remains un-preemptable due to its high importance. The data acquired will significantly contribute to our understanding of ground geometry under various weather conditions, providing essential insights into environmental monitoring efforts in the region.

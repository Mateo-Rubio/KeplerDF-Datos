In accordance with our ongoing efforts to maintain a comprehensive environmental monitoring system, we are initiating a routine tracking mission that requires standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis in Indonesia. Specifically, this operation will focus on assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the NDVI index as well as surface reflectance within Simabur city during the morning hours of three days from now. This task is set to align with our baseline pipeline tracking schedule and maintain normal operational status parameters. The data collected will contribute significantly to our understanding of regional vegetation health and dynamics, supporting both ecological studies and agricultural planning.

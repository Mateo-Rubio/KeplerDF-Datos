As a low priority importance task within our Earth-observation mission, we seek to conduct an overnight VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis in San Martín, Colombia, with an absolute institutional priority for this observational opportunity occurring three days hence at nighttime. This initiative will involve capturing high-resolution optical snapshots under true color visual documentation during the nocturnal hours to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index, as well as surface reflectance. The objective is an opportunistic capture that can be dropped or rescheduled at our convenience, serving as background survey tracking.

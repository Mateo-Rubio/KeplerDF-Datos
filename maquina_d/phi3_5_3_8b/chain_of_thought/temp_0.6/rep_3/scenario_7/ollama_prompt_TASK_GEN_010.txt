In accordance with operational guidelines for a standard operational tasking, this request seeks to initiate a routine monitoring process using multispectral vegetation analysis in Colombia's city of Pantanillo. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) alongside surface reflectance measurements within three days, specifically scheduled for morning operations. This tasking aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup and maintains a fixed parameter representing normal operational status. The data products are expected to be delivered by the specified deadline without alterations or extensions.

Extralateralism in international relations can take various forms such as diplomatic engagements beyond borders to tackle global challenges like climate change or terrorism, humanitarian aid efforts during natural disasters affecting citizens abroad regardless of national affiliation, international trade agreements that involve multiple countries to facilitate economic growth and resource sharing for mutual benefit, peacekeeping operations deployed in conflict zones outside a nation's territory aimed at maintaining or restoring regional stability when local conflicts threaten security or violate human rights, and cultural and educational exchanges which promote understanding between different nations. These efforts underscore the recognition of shared responsibilities and the importance of cooperative endeavors for common goals beyond immediate national interests.

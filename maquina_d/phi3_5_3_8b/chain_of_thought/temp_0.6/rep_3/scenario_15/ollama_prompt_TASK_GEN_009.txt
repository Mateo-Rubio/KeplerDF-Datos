In accordance with our standard operational tasking for routine monitoring and baseline pipeline tracking, we are initiating a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance measurements over Ecuatorial City in Indonesia during evening hours, specifically targeting "the day after tomorrow". This task aligns with our established schedule without altering or extending the deadline. The resulting data will contribute to a comprehensive understanding of vegetative health and dynamics within this region's unique environmental conditions.

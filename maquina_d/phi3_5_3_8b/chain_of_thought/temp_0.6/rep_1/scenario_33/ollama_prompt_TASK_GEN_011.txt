In accordance with our Earth-observation mission scheduling, we are organizing a task for an automated satellite constellation planner to conduct an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging over the city of Tesis located within Italy. This operation is aimed at penetrating heavy weather conditions and capturing ground geometry with high precision. The data collection window has been set for "the day after tomorrow" during a period identified as around mid-day, ensuring optimal environmental conditions for our radar's capabilities. Given the nature of this task which does not fall within critical operational parameters or time-sensitive research mandates, it is designated with low priority importance. Consequently, while we recognize its contribution to our comprehensive monitoring efforts, operators are advised that due to its non-essential status, they may choose to drop or reschedule this task at their convenience without prejudice to more pressing missions. This will serve as a background survey and testbed tracking task, complementing the broader spectrum of activities underway within our satellite network's observational framework.

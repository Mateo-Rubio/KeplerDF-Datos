In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols for routine monitoring within Italy's agricultural regions, we request a VNIR sensor-based multispectral vegetation analysis at Prati di Tivo in four days during the morning hours. This initiative is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating surface reflectance to monitor plant health effectively. The data collected will contribute significantly to our understanding of local agricultural practices and environmental conditions, ensuring that we maintain a comprehensive overview for future reference and analysis.

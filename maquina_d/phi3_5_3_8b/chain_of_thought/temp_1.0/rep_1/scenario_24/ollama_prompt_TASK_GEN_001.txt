In accordance with operational directives for a routine monitoring tasking scheduled in Sabana de Torres, Colombia, which falls under standard operational parameters set forth by our Earth-observation satellite network planner, we seek to capture multispectral vegetation analysis using high-resolution optical snapshots. The objective is aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance measurements during an overnight timeframe within three days from now, in alignment with our regular monitoring pipeline tracking setup.

In accordance with the scheduled institutional priorities for our Earth-observation missions, we are preparing a standard operational tasking set within our established baseline pipeline tracking framework. The objective is to execute a routine monitoring mission using multispectral vegetation analysis, which will specifically assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance characterization in Venaco, France, during the morning timeframe over the span of three days. This tasking is aligned with our medium importance category, ensuring consistent workflow updates that maintain our normal operational status while contributing valuable data to environmental research efforts.

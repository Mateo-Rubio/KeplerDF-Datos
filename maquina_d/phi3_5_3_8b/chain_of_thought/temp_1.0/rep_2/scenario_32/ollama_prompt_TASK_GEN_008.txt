As a non-essential background filler task with flexible scheduling considerations due to its low priority importance, we aim to conduct an SAR (active radar scan using microwave surface imaging) operation on tomorrow during the evening hours to capture ground geometry and penetrate cloud cover. This opportunistic capture will serve as a testbed tracking activity for our satellite constellation planner, allowing us to observe features that are typically obscured by weather conditions or lack of daylight photography capability. Given the non-critical nature of this mission objective within our broader observation goals, it can be rescheduled at operator convenience without impacting higher priority data acquisitions and institutional priorities.

In accordance with institutional requirements for high importance operations involving radar technology capable of cloud penetration and detailed ground geometry analysis, we hereby issue a binding contract requirement for an active radar scan over Kapuas Hulu in Indonesia starting from the day after tomorrow during the designated overnight period.

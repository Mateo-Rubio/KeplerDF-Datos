In three days' time, we are conducting an active radar scan mission using microwave surface imaging in Uribe, Colombia. This operation is aimed at cloud-penetrating capture and mapping ground geometry to provide valuable insights into the area's topography and land cover during nighttime conditions. Due to the low operational priority of this task, it can be scheduled flexibly; however, given its role in enhancing our understanding of nocturnal environmental dynamics, we will aim to execute within the specified overnight period without altering or extending the deadline.

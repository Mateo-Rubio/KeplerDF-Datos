As a next step in our Earth-observation mission sequence, we have designated Diano Borello, located in Italy, for a low priority importance task requiring us to capture high-resolution optical snapshots during overnight periods. The objective is to execute this as an opportunistic capture endeavor with the utmost flexibility concerning scheduling. Operators are explicitly instructed to consider these tasks as background survey or testbed tracking assignments and are free to drop or reschedule them according to their convenience, without affecting our primary mission objectives.

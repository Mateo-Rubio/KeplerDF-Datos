In accordance with the standard operational tasking requirements for our Earth-observation mission, we request a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities on "the day after tomorrow" in the morning to assess surface reflectance and calculate the NDVI index. This routine monitoring is essential for baseline pipeline tracking within Indonesia's agricultural sector, specifically targeting the city of Malinau. The data products are expected as part of a regular update that aligns with our ongoing efforts to monitor vegetation health and changes in surface reflectance due to environmental factors or human activities.

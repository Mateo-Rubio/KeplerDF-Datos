As a critical component of our ongoing environmental monitoring operations, we are requesting the collection and analysis of near-infrared reflection data over Indonesia's equatorial region. Specifically, the task is set for "the night after tomorrow", during early hours in the morning when conditions tend to be most stable for assessing water body boundaries and soil moisture levels. This standard operational tasking forms a part of our baseline pipeline tracking aimed at providing consistent multispectral vegetation analysis, including surface reflectance studies essential for effective environmental stewardship and agricultural management in the region. The data gathered will contribute to discriminating between different gradients within these critical ecosystems.

In light of my recent return to a place I used to frequent, which had witnessed considerable construction in the intervening period marked by "the day after tomorrow", during evening hours, I am compelled to share observations about this accelerated urban transformation. The once familiar ground beneath our feet has given way to an ever-expanding concrete jungle, leaving little trace of its erstwhile natural state. This rapid alteration speaks volumes not only to the changes in infrastructure but also raises concerns regarding environmental and communal impacts that may accompany such drastic urban development.

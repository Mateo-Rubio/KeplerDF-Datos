As a mission operator for our automated satellite constellation, this task request is directed towards obtaining high-resolution optical snapshots with VIS sensors to achieve clear optical imagery of Blera, located in Italy. Given its low priority status and considering the flexible nature of opportunistic capture tasks, we are aiming for a morning acquisition four days from now. This aligns with our standard operational procedures where such data collection is not urgent but remains an opportunity for additional insight into the region's daylight photography potential.

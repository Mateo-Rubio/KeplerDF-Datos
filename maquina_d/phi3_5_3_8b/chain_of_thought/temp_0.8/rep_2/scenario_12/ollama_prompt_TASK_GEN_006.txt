In line with today's scheduled operations for Kutai Kartanegara situated within Indonesia, we seek to conduct a standard operational tasking focused on obtaining multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This initiative is designed to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate surface reflectance indices accurately. As this constitutes an essential component of our routine monitoring, it falls under a non-essential background filler task which can be conveniently rescheduled or dropped at operator convenience. The objective remains clear optical imagery documentation to provide true-color visual records during the overnight period when environmental conditions are expected to optimize for such assessments.

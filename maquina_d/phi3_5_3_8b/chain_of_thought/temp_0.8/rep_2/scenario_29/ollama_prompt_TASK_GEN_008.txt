In accordance with institutional guidelines, we seek to conduct a low priority importance task by capturing near-infrared reflections during overnight periods for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. This initiative serves as an opportunistic filler task that can be dropped or rescheduled at our convenience. The location of interest is Monte Romano, situated in the picturesque country of Italia. We aim to observe these specific features using a high-resolution optical snapshot for true-color visual documentation under natural daylight conditions when possible, despite the emphasis on an overnight operation due to its low operational urgency and flexible scheduling nature.

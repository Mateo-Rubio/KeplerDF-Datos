This is an absolute institutional priority due to its critical nature for environmental surveillance and assessment.

This task has been allocated a priority level of 3, emphasizing its high importance and requiring an un-preemptable mission lock.

The operator notes that this request is part of a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. However, due to its elevated priority, it carries the weight of a binding contract requirement and urgent operational execution.

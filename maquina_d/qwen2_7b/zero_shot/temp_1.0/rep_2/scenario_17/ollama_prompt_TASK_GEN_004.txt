As part of our flexible background survey activities, we aim to capture a multispectral vegetation analysis for an area within Colombia. This is not an essential task with immediate importance but rather a testbed tracking task that involves observing chlorophyll absorption levels or calculating the vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The goal is to acquire these data in high-resolution detail with our VNIR sensor before we head into the evening on the day following tomorrow, ensuring the products are ingested into our pipeline well before that specific time slot. This will aid in our ongoing research efforts to better understand and manage the ecosystems in the region, providing valuable insights for environmental conservation and agricultural productivity.

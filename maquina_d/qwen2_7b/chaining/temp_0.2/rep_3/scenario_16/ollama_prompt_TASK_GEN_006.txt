In response to a medium importance tasking directive, our automated satellite constellation planner is tasked with capturing high-resolution optical imagery of Mulya Bakti, Indonesia, using near-infrared sensors. This standard operational task aims to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries for agricultural monitoring purposes. The imagery collection will occur overnight in three days, as part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This request is not subject to any urgent deadlines or contractual obligations; instead, it is a routine monitoring activity that contributes to our ongoing assessment of environmental conditions and supports decision-making processes for resource management. With this task, we aim to gather essential data without compromising the integrity of more critical payload captures or contractual requirements, ensuring a balanced approach to satellite mission allocation.

In anticipation of capturing a comprehensive thermal map of Kapuas Hulu, Indonesia, our Earth-observation mission operator hereby submits a low priority importance task for execution on the day after tomorrow during the afternoon. This request is framed as a flexible schedule background survey, with no expectation of urgent operational execution or contractual binding. The primary sensor to be utilized is the TIR, which will enable us to assess surface temperature profiles and detect thermal anomalies across the region. We aim to gather data that will provide insights into heat emissions without the constraints of daylight photography or clear optical imagery, allowing for a more nuanced understanding of Kapuas Hulu's thermal dynamics during this specific diurnal period.

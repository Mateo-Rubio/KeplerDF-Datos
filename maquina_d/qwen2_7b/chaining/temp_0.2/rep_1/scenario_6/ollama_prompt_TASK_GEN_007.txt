This will enable us to assess the city's infrastructure, environmental conditions, and urban development status accurately.

This will allow for the detection of heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across various areas within Zipaquirá, which is crucial for identifying potential hotspots or anomalies that may require further investigation.

This will help us assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) and surface reflectance, providing valuable insights into the health and productivity of local vegetation.

Lastly, our NIR (Near-Infrared) sensor will be used for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. This data is essential for understanding the hydrological dynamics within the region, which can inform agricultural practices, water resource management, and environmental conservation efforts.

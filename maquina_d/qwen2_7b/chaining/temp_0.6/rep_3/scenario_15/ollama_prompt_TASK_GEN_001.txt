On the day after tomorrow during the afternoon, an opportunistic background survey task is requested for a high-resolution optical snapshot of Colombia's picturesque city of Remedios. This "non-essential background filler task" aims to capture clear daylight photography and true-color visual documentation of its landscape. The flexible schedule allows for this "testbed tracking task" to be dropped or rescheduled at our convenience, prioritizing lower importance requests with normal operational status or as part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup. With the primary sensor set to VIS, we seek to document the city's beauty without the constraints of a strict deadline, treating it as a flexible opportunity for background surveying rather than an urgent operational execution.

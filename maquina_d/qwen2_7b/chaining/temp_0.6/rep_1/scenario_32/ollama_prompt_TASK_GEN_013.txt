In preparation for a comprehensive environmental assessment tomorrow in the evening, we seek to capture a low priority importance VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis in Belén de Umbría, Colombia. This "opportunistic capture" aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index while evaluating surface reflectance. The goal is to obtain high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography, clear optical imagery, and true-color visual documentation that will serve as a background survey or testbed tracking task. With this "non-essential background filler task", we ensure that our observations capture the essence of the region's vegetation health with minimal disruption to our baseline pipeline tracking setup, maintaining normal operational status.

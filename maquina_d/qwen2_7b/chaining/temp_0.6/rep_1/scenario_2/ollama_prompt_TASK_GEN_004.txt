Requesting a standard operational tasking for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging with Colombia's Nóvita region as the target location. This mission aims to capture cloud-penetrating imagery, providing critical insights into ground geometry and topography for normal operational status and baseline pipeline tracking requirements. The high-resolution SAR data will be collected in four days around mid-day, as part of a routine monitoring effort. This task is categorized under fixed parameters representing normal operational status and is not an essential background filler task; it can be flexibly scheduled or dropped at convenience if needed. The data acquisition process will ensure comprehensive coverage of the Nóvita region without preemption from other missions, contributing to standard workflow updates and maintaining high-quality asset contractual value.

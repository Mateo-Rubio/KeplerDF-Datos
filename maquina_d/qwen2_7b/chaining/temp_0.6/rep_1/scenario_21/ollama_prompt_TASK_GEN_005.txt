In response to an absolute institutional priority, we request the satellite constellation to capture a high-resolution near-infrared reflection image of the Norte region in Brazil. This essential task is part of our standard operational pipeline tracking and involves assessing soil moisture levels for agricultural planning purposes. We aim to gather this data in three days, in the morning, to ensure optimal conditions for accurate soil moisture assessment without interference from atmospheric conditions. As a binding contract requirement, this critical payload capture is mandated to provide indispensable insights into local farming ecosystems, enabling timely decision-making and resource management. This mission falls under our baseline priority level 3, which signifies an urgent operational execution with no room for preemption or rescheduling; it is a non-essential background filler task that we may drop or reschedule at convenience if circumstances require. The image will facilitate water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment, contributing to our understanding of environmental conditions and supporting agricultural strategies in Norte.

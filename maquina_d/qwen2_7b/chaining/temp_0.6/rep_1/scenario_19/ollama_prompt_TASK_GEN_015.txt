In the heart of Indonesia, focusing on Desa Muaro Sentajo, our satellite constellation is tasked with capturing a comprehensive thermal mapping overview. Operating under the principle of 'standard operational tasking', we aim to capture critical heat signatures and surface temperature profiles during the target day, which falls 'tomorrow during the afternoon'. Utilizing the sophisticated TIR sensor, this mission involves an active radar scan that pierces through any cloud cover or heavy weather conditions for a cloud-penetrating capture. The objective is to meticulously monitor thermal anomalies and map heat emissions across the region, ensuring a detailed assessment of thermal dynamics. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status. It is a routine monitoring effort that supports our comprehensive surveillance capabilities and contributes to maintaining the integrity of our data collection processes.

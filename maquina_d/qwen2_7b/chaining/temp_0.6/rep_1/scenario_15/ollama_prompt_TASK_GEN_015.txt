With a nod towards Indonesia's rich biodiversity and considering the urgent need for environmental monitoring, our Earth-observation mission seeks to embark on a low priority importance task focusing on thermal mapping for the day after tomorrow overnight. Our primary sensor of choice is the TIR (Thermal Infrared) instrument, which will enable us to capture heat signatures across an area in Basa Ampek Balai Tapan, Indonesia. The mission aims to achieve a comprehensive thermal anomaly detection and mapping of heat emissions without compromising on quality, as it aligns with our flexible schedule for opportunistic captures. This non-essential background filler task will allow us to assess surface temperature profiles and contribute to background survey data, treating it as a testbed tracking task that complements our broader operational activities.

In three days, in the evening, an Earth-observation mission operator requests the automated satellite constellation planner to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography over Murindó, Colombia. This low priority importance task aims at obtaining clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation of the region. The operator explicitly notes that this request is part of a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, considering it as a non-essential background survey with no strict deadline requirements. As this mission involves a VIS sensor, the primary objective is to observe the area with visual sensors that can provide high-quality daytime images without cloud obstruction, enabling detailed documentation of the landscape and urban features in Murindó.

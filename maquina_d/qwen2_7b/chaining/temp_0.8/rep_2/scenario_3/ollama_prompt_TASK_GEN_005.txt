Our Earth-observation mission operator is tasked with capturing a low priority importance view of Porto Recanati, Italia, on a clear day three days from now in the morning. Specifically, utilizing a VNIR sensor, we aim to conduct a low priority importance multispectral vegetation analysis focusing on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating a NDVI index to understand surface reflectance. This is part of an opportunistic background survey task with a flexible schedule allowing for rescheduling or dropping it as needed at our convenience. The ultimate goal is to gather this valuable information without any preemption concerns, treating it as a non-essential background filler task in our routine monitoring activities.

Logging a standard operational tasking over Tagliole in Italia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim for a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index, along with surface reflectance for the area. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the delivery deadline is set for three days hence, when we can safely conduct the overnight scan due to minimal disturbance. The products should be ingested into the pipeline by then.

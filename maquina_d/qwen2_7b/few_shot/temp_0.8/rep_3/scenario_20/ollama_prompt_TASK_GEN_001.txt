Logging a standard operational tasking over San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim to capture a multispectral vegetation analysis over the area to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the delivery deadline for the data products is set for three days from now, when we can expect an overnight observation to ensure optimal conditions for imaging.

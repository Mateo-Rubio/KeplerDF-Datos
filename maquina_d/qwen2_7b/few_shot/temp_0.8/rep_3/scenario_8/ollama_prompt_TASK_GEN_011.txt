Logging a standard operational tasking over Arsiero in Italia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We want to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the area around the town using a multispectral vegetation analysis with a high-resolution optical snapshot under daylight photography conditions. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and we aim to have the products ingested into our systems in four days in the morning.

Logging a standard operational tasking for Venaco in France, which is part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance to understand the ecological conditions of the region. This commercial tasking represents a routine monitoring activity at normal operational status, and we need to capture this data in three days during the morning hours to capture the most accurate information possible.

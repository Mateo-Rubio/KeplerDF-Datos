Logging a standard operational tasking over Kutai Kartanegara in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. Treating it as routine monitoring at normal operational status, we aim to perform a near-infrared reflection survey for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment under standard conditions in the city. The goal is to discriminate gradients using the tools provided by our satellite constellation. With this task, we are seeking to map water bodies and assess soil moisture content in Kutai Kartanegara for tomorrow morning at the latest.

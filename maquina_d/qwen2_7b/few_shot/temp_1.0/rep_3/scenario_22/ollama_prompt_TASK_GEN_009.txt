Logging a standard operational tasking for Banyuasin in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We are to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over this area in order to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance. This is an 'around mid-day' observation planned three days ahead as routine monitoring at normal operational status. The data ingestion deadline is set for around mid-day on that specific day, according to our usual schedule.

Logging a low priority importance task in Slovenia, specifically over the city of Norte. We aim for a non-essential background filler task that will help us assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance through multispectral vegetation analysis over the region. Given its status as a flexible schedule task, it can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. Yet, should an opportunity arise on the day after tomorrow in the morning, this opportunistic survey will provide valuable insights into the environmental conditions of Norte. All the gathered data must meet the deadline set for then and no sooner.

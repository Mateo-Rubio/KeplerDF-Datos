Over at Mulya Bakti in Indonesia, we have a standard operational tasking to capture an area of interest with our near-infrared (NIR) sensor on our satellite constellation. It's part of the commercial pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. The goal is to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels, providing crucial insights into aquatic management and agricultural conditions. Considering it's a routine monitoring operation, there's no rush for this task, with the products due in three days overnight at the latest.

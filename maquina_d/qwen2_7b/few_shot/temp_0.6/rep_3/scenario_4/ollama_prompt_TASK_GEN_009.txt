Logging a standard operational tasking over Rokan Hilir in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. Aiming for a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the area. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the delivery deadline is set for in three days in the morning. Cloud cover has been heavy lately, so this will help us gather critical data about the land use and health of vegetation under varying conditions.

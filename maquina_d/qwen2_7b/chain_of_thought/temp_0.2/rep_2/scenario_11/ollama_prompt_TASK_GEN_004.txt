In three days, at the start of the overnight period, we request a low priority importance tasking for a near-infrared (NIR) sensor capture. The objective involves a flexible schedule for opportunistic data collection in Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Sácama. This mission aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels using the unique capabilities of the NIR sensor during this time frame. The imagery will serve as a background survey, providing valuable insights into environmental conditions without imposing any urgent operational constraints.

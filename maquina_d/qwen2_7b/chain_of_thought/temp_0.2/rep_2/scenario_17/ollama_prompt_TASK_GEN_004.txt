In three days, at the onset of the evening, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Caramanta, Colombia. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels through near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment, using a multispectral vegetation analysis with our VNIR sensor. The goal is to provide thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions through thermal mapping, ensuring we capture the essence of the landscape under the clear optical imagery of daylight photography as the sun begins to set.

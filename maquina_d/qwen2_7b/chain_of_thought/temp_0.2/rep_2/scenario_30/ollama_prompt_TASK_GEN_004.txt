In three days, at the time of overnight, we are tasked with capturing a critical SAR scan over Uribe, Colombia. This mission allocation carries a low priority importance, allowing for a flexible schedule where the data collection can be dropped or rescheduled as convenient. The goal is to perform an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology, which will pierce through any heavy weather conditions to capture ground geometry. This "cloud-penetrating capture" is essential for our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

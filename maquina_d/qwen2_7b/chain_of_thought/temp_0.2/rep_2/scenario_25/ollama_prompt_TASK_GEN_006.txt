In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we request a low priority importance task focusing on the Indonesian region, specifically Singkep Barat. This mission involves capturing a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to understand surface reflectance. The objective is to gather high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography, clear optical imagery, and true-color visual documentation of the area. This standard operational tasking aims to provide valuable insights into the vegetation health and environmental conditions without imposing any urgent operational execution requirements.

In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our satellite constellation will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Colombia's picturesque town of Cachira. This daylight photography will serve as a true-color visual documentation, providing us with clear optical imagery that captures the essence of this charming location after dark. As part of a low priority importance request, we aim to deliver this non-essential background survey without altering the deadline, treating it as a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. Our mission is to assess the heat signature and surface temperature profile of Cachira overnight, contributing to our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

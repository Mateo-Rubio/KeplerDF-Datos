In three days, at the request's specified time of evening, we aim to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors over the region of Corteno Golgi in Italia. This task is considered a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The imagery will serve as a critical tool for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index, providing essential insights into the vegetation health and environmental conditions.

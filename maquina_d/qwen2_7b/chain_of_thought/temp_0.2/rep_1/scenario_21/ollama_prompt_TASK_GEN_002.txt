In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle above Santa Croce sull'Arno, Italia, our satellite constellation will be tasked with capturing thermal imagery overnight. This mission is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling to accommodate any necessary adjustments or rescheduling at convenience. The primary sensor involved will perform thermal mapping, seeking to capture the heat signature and surface temperature profile of the region during this period. As the night unfolds, our satellite will diligently monitor and collect data, contributing to our understanding of thermal anomalies and heat emissions in the area.

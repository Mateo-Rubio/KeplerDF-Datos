In three days, at the beginning of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology over the equatorial region of Indonesia. This cloud-penetrating capture aims to provide us with detailed ground geometry data without being obscured by weather conditions. The primary objective is to gather essential information for our baseline pipeline tracking setup, which involves fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This task is classified as a standard operational tasking and will be part of our routine monitoring activities. As it pertains to our low priority importance request, we understand that this mission can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if necessary, treating it as a background survey or testbed tracking task.

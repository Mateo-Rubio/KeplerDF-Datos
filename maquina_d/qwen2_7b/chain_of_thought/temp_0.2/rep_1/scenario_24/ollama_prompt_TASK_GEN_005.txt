In three days, during the evening period, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data for a low priority importance task in Norte, a region without a specific country designation. This operational request aims to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries using the specified time frame as a strict deadline for delivering thermal mapping data products. The evening period is chosen for its potential to provide optimal conditions for near-infrared reflection analysis, ensuring accurate soil moisture assessment and water body boundary mapping without altering the deadline.

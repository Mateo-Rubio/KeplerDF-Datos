In four days, we are tasked with capturing a routine monitoring mission for the Kepulauan Meranti region in Indonesia. This standard operational tasking involves a VNIR sensor scan scheduled overnight. The primary objective is to conduct a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data collection will focus on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the vegetation NDVI index, as well as surface reflectance for comprehensive multispectral vegetation analysis.

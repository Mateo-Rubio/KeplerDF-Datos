In three days, around mid-day, we request the deployment of our satellite constellation to capture near-infrared (NIR) data over the city of Sarolangun in Indonesia. This task is of medium importance and will be executed as a standard operational tasking. The primary objective is to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels using the high-resolution optical snapshot provided by the NIR sensor at this specific time. We aim for clear imagery that captures the essence of daylight photography, ensuring that the data products are delivered on the specified deadline without any adjustments to the target day or time period.

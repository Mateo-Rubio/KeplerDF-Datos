In four days, at the onset of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey using our VNIR sensor over the picturesque region of Italia, specifically focusing on the city of Moncalieri. This "non-essential background filler task" aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and surface reflectance through a "multispectral vegetation analysis", providing us with valuable insights into the health and composition of the local flora without compromising our operational workflow or deadlines.

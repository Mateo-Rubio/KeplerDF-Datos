In three days, at the break of dawn, we are to capture a detailed active radar scan over the city of Dolores in Colombia. This mission is designated as a low priority importance task with flexible scheduling requirements. We aim to use this opportunity for cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry using microwave surface imaging techniques. The primary objective is to gather data that will serve as a baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status and contributing to our standard workflow updates without any urgency for immediate execution.

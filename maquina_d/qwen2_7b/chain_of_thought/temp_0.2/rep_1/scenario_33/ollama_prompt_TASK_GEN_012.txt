In three days, at the time when the sun begins its descent and paints the sky with hues of orange and pink, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a near-infrared (NIR) reflection snapshot of Kuantan Singingi, Indonesia. This request, designated as a low priority importance task, aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels without altering the deadline set for delivery. The imagery will serve as a background survey, allowing for opportunistic capture that can be conveniently scheduled or rescheduled as per operational convenience.

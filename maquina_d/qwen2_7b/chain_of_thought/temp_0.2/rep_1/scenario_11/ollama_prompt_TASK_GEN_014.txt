In three days, during the morning hours, we request a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission using the VNIR sensor. This will involve capturing high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and clear optical imagery across Norte's landscape. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index, as well as surface reflectance data, providing valuable insights into the health and composition of the local flora. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

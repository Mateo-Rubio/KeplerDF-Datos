On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun their nightly dance, our satellite constellation will be tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of Diano Borello, nestled in the beautiful country of Italia. This "overnight" mission is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates our operational needs without imposing any urgent constraints. The primary sensor to be deployed will be our VIS system, enabling us to gather clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation of this picturesque location under the cover of darkness. This "background survey" aims to provide valuable insights into the landscape and its features without disrupting the natural nocturnal environment.

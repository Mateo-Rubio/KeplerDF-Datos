In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance visual snapshot of Singkep Barat, Indonesia. This high-resolution optical imagery will serve as daylight photography, providing clear optical details for our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The VNIR sensor will be used to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for vegetation analysis, contributing to our non-essential background survey without compromising more critical operational tasks.

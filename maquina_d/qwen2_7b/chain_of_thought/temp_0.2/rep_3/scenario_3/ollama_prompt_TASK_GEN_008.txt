In three days, at the time of overnight, we request the capture of a low priority importance task involving VNIR sensors for a nighttime multispectral vegetation analysis. This will serve as a background survey to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index across the region in Gazzola, Italia. The operational objective is to gather detailed information on surface reflectance under the cover of darkness, providing valuable insights into the health and composition of the local vegetation without interference from daylight conditions.

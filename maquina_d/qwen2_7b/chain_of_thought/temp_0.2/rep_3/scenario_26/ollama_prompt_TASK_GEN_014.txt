In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun to twinkle in the sky, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data over the region known as "Italia Norte". This task is designated with a low priority importance, allowing for flexible scheduling. We aim to gather information on soil moisture levels and water body boundaries using this spectral band, which will provide invaluable insights into environmental conditions without the interference of daylight. The deadline for delivering these data products is strictly set for overnight hours, ensuring that we capture the desired information accurately and efficiently.

In four days, at the break of dawn, our satellite constellation is tasked with capturing a thermal mapping of Victoria, Colombia. This critical payload capture involves assessing surface temperature profiles and detecting thermal anomalies across the region. As this request carries a low priority importance, it is designated as a flexible schedule task, allowing for opportunistic capture without compromising more urgent operational execution. The data collected will serve as a background survey to monitor heat emissions and support decision-making processes in environmental management and urban planning.

In four days, during the morning hours, we are tasked with capturing a low-priority importance VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis for the region of Moncalieri, Italy. This involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to provide surface reflectance data. The objective is to obtain clear optical imagery that will serve as a true-color visual documentation of the area's vegetation health without altering the deadline or shifting it to another part of the day.

In four days, at the start of the morning, we need to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Bangka Selatan, Indonesia. This daylight photography mission aims to provide clear visual documentation and true-color imagery for our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We are focusing on capturing the region's landscape and urban areas using our visible light sensor, ensuring that we adhere to our standard operational tasks without altering the deadline or shifting it to any other part of the day.

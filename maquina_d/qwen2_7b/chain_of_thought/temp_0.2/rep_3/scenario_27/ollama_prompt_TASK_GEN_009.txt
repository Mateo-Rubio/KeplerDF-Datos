In three days, around mid-day, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey using the VNIR sensor over Indonesia's equatorial region. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index for vegetation analysis, as well as mapping surface reflectance. The goal is to provide high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography, clear optical imagery, and true-color visual documentation of the area, ensuring we capture the heat signature and surface temperature profile for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions.

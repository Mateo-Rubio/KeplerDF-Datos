In three days, at the time when the world sleeps and the stars shine brightest, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a thermal mapping snapshot of Norte, Slovenia. This "in-the-dark" mission, designated as a non-essential background filler task with a flexible schedule, aims to assess surface temperature profiles and detect any thermal anomalies that may indicate heat emissions from various sources. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, this "overnight" capture will contribute to our understanding of the region's thermal dynamics without disrupting daytime operations or affecting the urgency of other critical payloads.

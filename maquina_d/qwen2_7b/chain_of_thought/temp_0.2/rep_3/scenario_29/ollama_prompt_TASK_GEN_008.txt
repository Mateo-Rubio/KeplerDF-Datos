In four days, at the time when the world sleeps and the stars are most visible, our Earth-observation mission will capture a near-infrared reflection snapshot of Monte Romano, Italy, during its overnight period. This low-priority importance task is designated as a non-essential background filler, meant to be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. It aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels, providing us with critical data on surface reflectance under the cover of darkness.

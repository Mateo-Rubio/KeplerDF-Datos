In four days, at the time when the sky is dark and the world is quiet, our satellite constellation will embark on a mission of medium importance. This task involves capturing a near-infrared reflection overnight over the city of Murung Raya in Indonesia. The goal is to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels using this technique, which allows for discrimination of gradients that are crucial for agricultural and environmental monitoring. This standard operational tasking requires us to deliver high-quality data products by the specified deadline, ensuring our baseline pipeline tracking remains on course with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

In three days, at the time of overnight, we request the execution of a standard operational tasking involving a VNIR sensor for a nighttime multispectral vegetation analysis. This involves capturing high-resolution optical snapshots as daylight photography or true-color visual documentation of the region in Colombia, specifically focusing on San Carlos de Guaroa. The purpose is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance, providing critical data for environmental monitoring and agricultural assessment without interference from daytime sunlight.

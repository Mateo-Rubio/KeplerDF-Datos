In three days, around mid-day, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery over Pavullo nel Frignano, Italia. This mission is designated as a standard operational tasking with a focus on mapping water body boundaries and assessing soil moisture levels. The imagery will provide critical data for environmental monitoring and agricultural management, offering insights into chlorophyll absorption levels and NDVI indices to ensure optimal crop health and water resource allocation. The deadline for delivering this data product is strictly adhered to, ensuring timely and accurate information for decision-making processes.

In four days, around mid-day, we aim to capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of the region known as Schweiz/Suisse/Svizzera/Svizra, with a particular focus on the city of Rheinwald. This standard operational tasking involves assessing soil moisture levels and delineating water body boundaries using the unique properties of near-infrared reflection. The imagery will serve as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status and contributing to our routine monitoring efforts.

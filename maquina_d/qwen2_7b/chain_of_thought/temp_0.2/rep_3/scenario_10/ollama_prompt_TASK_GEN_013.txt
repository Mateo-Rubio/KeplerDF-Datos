In four days, at the beginning of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. This cloud-penetrating capture aims to provide detailed ground geometry information for the region of Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Baraya. The primary objective is to gather data that will aid in understanding the landscape and potential environmental conditions without being hindered by weather conditions or time of day. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling and opportunistic capture at our convenience. It serves as a background survey to support ongoing monitoring efforts without impacting more critical operational tasks.

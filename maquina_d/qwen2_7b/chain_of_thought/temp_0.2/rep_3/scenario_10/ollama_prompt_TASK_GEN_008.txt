In four days, at the time when the world sleeps and the stars shine bright, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the region known as Italia Norte. This mission, designated as a standard operational tasking with medium importance, aims to provide clear optical imagery for this area overnight. The primary sensor used will be the VIS (visual imaging system), which will deliver daylight photography and true-color visual documentation essential for understanding the landscape under the cover of darkness. This data collection is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status with fixed parameters that ensure the integrity of our monitoring efforts.

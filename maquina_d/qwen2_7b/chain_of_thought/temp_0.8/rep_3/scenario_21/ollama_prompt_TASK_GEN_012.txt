In four days, at the beginning of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner is tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot over the equatorial region of Indonesia. Specifically, we aim for a daylight photography or clear optical imagery that highlights the landscapes and urban environments. This "low priority importance" task requires us to monitor and gather data for an opportunistic capture schedule, treating it as a background survey or testbed tracking task since it does not have a preeminent deadline urgency. The primary sensor involved will conduct this observational mission using its VIS capabilities to provide us with the necessary visual documentation for our Earth-observation mission's database. We aim to adhere strictly to the operational parameters set without altering, extending, shifting or interpreting the deadline for data delivery.

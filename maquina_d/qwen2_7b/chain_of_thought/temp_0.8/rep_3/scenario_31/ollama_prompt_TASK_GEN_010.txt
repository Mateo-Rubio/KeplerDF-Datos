Given the stringent deadline of acquiring data by 'the day after tomorrow' in the morning, our satellite constellation is tasked with conducting an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. This operation aims to capture a cloud-penetrating image across the region of interest, Tierralta in Colombia, focusing on early-morning hours for optimal visibility conditions. The objective is to acquire high-resolution data, essential for understanding ground geometry and navigating through heavy weather, without any modifications or extensions to this operational timeframe. This mission holds a 'priority level 3' status, reflecting its critical importance in our institutional priorities.

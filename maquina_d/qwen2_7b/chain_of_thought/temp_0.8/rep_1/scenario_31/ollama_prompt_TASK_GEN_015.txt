In observance of today's operational priorities and considering the mission's absolute importance, our Earth-observation mission will focus on conducting a critical overnight task in Indonesia. Specifically, we aim for Tanjung Jabung Timur, a region rich with diverse land use and vegetation. Using the Vegetation Near-Infrared (NIR) sensor, our objective is to assess water body boundaries and soil moisture levels. This task serves as a non-essential background filler task, flexible in scheduling, to support ongoing environmental monitoring and research efforts without compromising urgency or operational integrity.

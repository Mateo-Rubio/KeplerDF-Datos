In preparation for a comprehensive assessment of soil moisture conditions in the captivating landscape of Kepulauan Meranti, Indonesia, our mission is allocated with medium importance. Scheduled to capture the essential data within four days, specifically capturing the essence of the morning light, we are undertaking this task as part of our standard operational pipeline tracking setup. The utilization of our near-infrared sensor will enable us to meticulously map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels, providing critical insights for environmental management and agricultural planning.

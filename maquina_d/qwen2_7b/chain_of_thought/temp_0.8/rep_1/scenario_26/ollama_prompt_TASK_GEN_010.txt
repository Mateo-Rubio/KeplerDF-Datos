In three days, at the onset of the evening, we aim to capture a comprehensive SAR image of Puerto López, Colombia. This high-priority request for information on its surface geometry and structure has been formulated considering the urgency of the mission allocation. Our operational focus will be on cloud-penetrating radar scans that can deliver critical data without the interference of daytime weather conditions. Expectant of delivering detailed microwave surface imaging by dusk, we anticipate this task to contribute significantly to our baseline pipeline tracking efforts.

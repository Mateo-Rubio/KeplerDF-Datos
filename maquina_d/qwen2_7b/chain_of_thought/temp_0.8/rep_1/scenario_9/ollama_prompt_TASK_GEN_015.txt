In response to the request with a priority level of Low Importance and a target set for Tomorrow Overnight, we are planning to capture high-resolution optical imagery through multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors in Ecuatorial Indonesia. This "background survey" task aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index across the region, documenting surface reflectance patterns under conditions that permit unobstructed viewing at night. This will serve as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, allowing for opportunistic capture without affecting immediate deadlines or critical payload acquisition priorities.

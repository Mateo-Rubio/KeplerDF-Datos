In light of the stringent operational deadline set for "tomorrow", our Earth observation mission requests high-resolution optical imaging during the "overnight" period. We aim to capture a true-color visual documentation of an area under litigation in Colombia, leveraging daylight photography capabilities through our visible spectrums. This captures both the essential details of land use and potential disputes in their natural state, without the distortions introduced by sunlight. The mission is marked as a low priority importance task, allowing for flexible scheduling to accommodate other operational commitments should the need arise for rescheduling or dropping this task at convenience.

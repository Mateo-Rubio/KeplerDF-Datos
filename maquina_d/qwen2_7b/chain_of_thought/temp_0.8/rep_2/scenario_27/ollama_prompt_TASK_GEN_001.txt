Taking into account today's operational context and prioritizing our flexible schedule, we are to embark on a low priority importance task which involves capturing a snapshot of Colombia's verdant landscape using the VNIR sensor. This observational effort will focus on the unique opportunity presented by the evening hours when we can obtain high-resolution optical imagery with clear daylight conditions for our day photography, true-color visual documentation, and multispectral vegetation analysis. Specifically, we aim to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index as well as surface reflectance without being constrained by the day's changing light and atmospheric conditions. We are to execute this request in the evening timeframe, which is when nature will provide optimal conditions for our mission.

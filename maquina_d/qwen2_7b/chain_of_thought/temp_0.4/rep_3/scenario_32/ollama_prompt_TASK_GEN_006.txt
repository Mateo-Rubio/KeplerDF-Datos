On the day after tomorrow, at the early hours of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner has been tasked with capturing a low-priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Indonesia's equatorial region. The primary sensor requirement is for multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR technology. This mission aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index, providing critical insights into surface reflectance without altering the operational deadline or shifting it to any other part of the day.

In three days, at the time when darkness envelops Tre Fasci, Italia, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot. This daylight photography aims to provide clear visual documentation of the region, illuminating its landscapes and structures in true color under the cover of the overnight period. As part of our standard operational tasking, this mission is scheduled for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging capabilities, piercing through any cloud cover to capture ground geometry. This will ensure a comprehensive view of the area's topography, essential for baseline pipeline tracking and normal operational status updates.

In approximately four days, our satellite constellation will capture a near-infrared (NIR) observation of San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia, focusing on an overnight period. This mission is designated as standard operational tasking, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The primary objective involves assessing soil moisture levels through the near-infrared reflection, which will aid in delineating water body boundaries and evaluating soil health. The imagery collected during this time will provide critical insights into environmental conditions without interference from daylight or other atmospheric conditions that may affect accuracy.

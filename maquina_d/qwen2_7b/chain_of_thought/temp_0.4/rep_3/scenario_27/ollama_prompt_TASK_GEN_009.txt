In three days, around mid-day, we request the capture of a low priority importance task involving the use of a VNIR sensor for multispectral vegetation analysis. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index, providing us with surface reflectance data. This task aims to document the state of vegetation in Indonesia, specifically focusing on an area known as Ecuatorial. We emphasize that this request is part of a flexible schedule, designed for opportunistic capture, and can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if necessary.

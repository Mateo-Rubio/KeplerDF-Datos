In three days, around mid-day, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. This critical payload capture involves cloud-penetrating capabilities to ensure that our observation of the city of San Vicente del Caguán in Colombia is not hindered by adverse weather conditions or obstructions. The operational execution of this mission is an absolute institutional priority, reflecting a binding contract requirement for a detailed and comprehensive view of the urban landscape and its surrounding environment.

To capture a critical thermal signature for an overnight analysis, we are tasked with obtaining near-infrared (NIR) data from a location in Indonesia, specifically the Ecuatorial city. The mission allocation is of low importance, allowing for flexible scheduling and treating it as a non-essential background filler task. We aim to collect this data on the target day, which is four days ahead, focusing on the overnight period for optimal conditions. This high-resolution optical snapshot will be used for thermal mapping, capturing heat emissions and surface temperature profiles without interference from daylight or cloud cover.

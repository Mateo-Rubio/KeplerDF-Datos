In three days, at the time of overnight when the sky is dark and the world is quiet, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data for Arnoldstein, located in the picturesque country of Österreich. This low priority importance task aims to document the unique thermal patterns of the area during this specific period. We seek to understand the heat signatures and surface temperatures that might be indicative of various phenomena, from agricultural health to environmental conditions. The deadline is strictly set for this exact overnight period to ensure we capture the essence of the night-time environment when disturbances are minimal, providing us with a clear and undisturbed view of Arnoldstein's thermal landscape.

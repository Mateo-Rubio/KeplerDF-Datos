In four days, at the time when the world sleeps and the night sky unfolds its mysteries, our mission will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Pandan Sembuat, Indonesia. This daylight photography will serve as a clear visual documentation of the region's landscape under the veil of darkness. The purpose of this 'overnight' tasking is to provide us with an opportunity to observe and document the unique characteristics of the area that might be obscured during the day. This low priority importance request allows for a flexible schedule, treating it as a background survey or testbed tracking task, where we can drop or reschedule at convenience as long as it aligns with our operational workflow updates.

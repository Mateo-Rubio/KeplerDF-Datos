In three days, during the afternoon, we aim to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis of the region in Italia, specifically focusing on the area around Norte. This task is part of our standard operational pipeline tracking with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for this area, providing valuable insights into the health and productivity of the vegetation.

We are looking forward to receiving clear optical imagery that will serve as a true-color visual documentation of the region, offering detailed information on the surface reflectance patterns. This data collection exercise is essential for our baseline monitoring efforts and will contribute to our ongoing research in understanding environmental changes and agricultural health within the specified geographical context.

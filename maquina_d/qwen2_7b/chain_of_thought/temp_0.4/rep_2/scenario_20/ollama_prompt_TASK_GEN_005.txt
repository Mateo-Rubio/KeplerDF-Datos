In three days, around mid-day, we request a routine monitoring task for near-infrared (NIR) observations over Pavullo nel Frignano, Italia. This standard operational tasking involves capturing the heat signature and surface temperature profile of the region using active microwave surface imaging. The imagery will serve as a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to provide thermal anomaly detection and mapping of heat emissions without altering the deadline or shifting the time window.

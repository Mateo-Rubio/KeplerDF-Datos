On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the world is enveloped in the peaceful calm of overnight, our mission requires a low priority importance task. This involves capturing a snapshot of the landscape in Borgo San Lorenzo, Italy, using a VNIR sensor for multispectral vegetation analysis. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index to understand surface reflectance. This "background survey" aims to provide insights into the health and dynamics of the vegetation without altering the natural day-to-day operations.

In four days, at the time of overnight, our satellite constellation will capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis for the region of Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Cumaral. This critical payload capture is part of an urgent operational execution under the binding contract requirement involving a 'thermal mapping' task to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the area's vegetation. The imagery collected will provide essential data for monitoring environmental changes and supporting agricultural management decisions, ensuring compliance with our institutional priorities.

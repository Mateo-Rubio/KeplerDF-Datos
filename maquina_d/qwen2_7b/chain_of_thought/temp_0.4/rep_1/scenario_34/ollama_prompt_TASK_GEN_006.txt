In four days, our satellite constellation will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot overnight over Indonesia's equatorial region. This task involves a clear optical imagery requirement for daylight photography and true-color visual documentation aimed at providing detailed views of the landscape under the cover of darkness. Given its nature as a low priority importance request, this "non-essential background filler task" is scheduled with a flexible schedule, allowing for opportunistic capture without compromising other critical operational tasks. We aim to deliver thermal mapping capabilities through our VIS sensor, focusing on capturing heat signatures and surface temperature profiles essential for understanding the unique environmental conditions of Indonesia's equatorial area during the overnight period.

In three days, at the time when the sun dips below the horizon and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our satellite constellation will be dispatched to capture a near-infrared reflection snapshot of Arnoldstein, located in the picturesque country of Österreich. This low priority importance task involves an overnight mission allocation, which is a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. As an essential background survey for our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, we aim to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries without the interference of daylight or cloud cover. This "non-essential background filler task" serves as a testbed tracking task for normal operational status, ensuring that our satellite constellation remains ready to provide critical data products on demand.

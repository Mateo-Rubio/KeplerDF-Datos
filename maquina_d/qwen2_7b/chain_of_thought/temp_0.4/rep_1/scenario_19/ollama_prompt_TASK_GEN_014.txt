In three days, during the afternoon, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis data for the region encompassing the country of Italia and the city of Norte. This standard operational tasking aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels across various landscapes and calculate the vegetation NDVI index as well as surface reflectance. The data collection is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status with fixed parameters that ensure the integrity of our commercial tasking and routine monitoring activities.

This operational tasking will adhere to a standard baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and contributing to our routine monitoring efforts. As part of this mission, we aim to capture essential data for understanding vegetation health and distribution, ensuring that these insights are delivered in time for informed decision-making processes without any alteration to the specified deadline.

In three days, in the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner is tasked with capturing an active radar scan of Indonesia's ecuatorial region. This involves penetrating through any cloud cover for a detailed microwave surface imaging mission. We aim to deliver thermal mapping insights that help us detect heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, crucial for identifying potential anomalies or patterns related to climate change, agriculture, or natural resource management. This task holds low priority importance, allowing for flexible scheduling and opportunistic capture, fitting well as a background survey or testbed tracking task in our pipeline tracking setup.

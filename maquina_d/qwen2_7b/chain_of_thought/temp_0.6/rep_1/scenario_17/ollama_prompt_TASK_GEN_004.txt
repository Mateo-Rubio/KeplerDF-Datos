In three days, with the sun setting on the horizon, we aim to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis using our VNIR sensor over the lush landscapes of Colombia. As we focus our gaze on Caramanta, a city nestled within the verdant hills, we seek thermal mapping capabilities to uncover the subtle nuances of temperature and heat signatures across its fertile soil and thriving ecosystems. This operation embodies a low-priority importance task, allowing for flexible scheduling while still adhering to the deadline set for the evening hours. The imagery collected will serve as a valuable addition to our baseline pipeline tracking for environmental monitoring, providing insights on chlorophyll absorption levels and vegetation health without the constraints of daylight or cloud cover.

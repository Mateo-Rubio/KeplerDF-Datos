In response to the task request, our Earth-observation mission operator aims to capture high-resolution optical imagery of Foraquira, Colombia, through a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors. The mission is designated as a low priority importance request, with the operational parameters set for an "overnight" timeframe, meaning "in three days". This tasking will serve as a flexible schedule "opportunistic capture", fitting into our baseline pipeline tracking setup with normal operational status. We will deliver thermal mapping data and assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance without altering the deadline to an adjacent part of the day.

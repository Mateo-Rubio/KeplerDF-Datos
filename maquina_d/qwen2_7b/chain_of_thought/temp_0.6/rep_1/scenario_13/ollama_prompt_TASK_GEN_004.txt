In three days, during the overnight period, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis in San Martín, Colombia. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level for each pixel, contributing to an overall vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance mapping. The goal is to provide high-resolution optical snapshots to document the nighttime conditions, ensuring that we capture clear optical imagery of the region without interference from sunlight, as part of a background survey or testbed tracking task.

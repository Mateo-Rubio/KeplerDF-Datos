To capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis for Porto Recanati, Italia, focusing on the morning of the day that falls exactly three days ahead, our satellite constellation will execute an opportunistic capture. This mission, with its low priority importance, is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance. The deadline for delivering the data products is strictly adhered to as specified: in the morning on the day that arrives three days from now.

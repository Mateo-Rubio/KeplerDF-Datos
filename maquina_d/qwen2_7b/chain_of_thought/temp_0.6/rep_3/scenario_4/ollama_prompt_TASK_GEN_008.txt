In three days, at the time when the sun sets and the stars begin to twinkle across the Austrian sky, a critical task awaits the observant eyes of our satellite constellation. This absolute institutional priority involves capturing a near-infrared reflection overnight in Arnoldstein, a charming village nestled among the hills of Österreich. The deadline is set precisely for this moment, not to be extended or shifted. We aim to deliver thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions through this 'NIR' sensor, ensuring that each pixel of data carries the essence of the Austrian landscape under the cover of night.

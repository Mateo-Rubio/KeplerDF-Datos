In four days, during the overnight period, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance visual representation of Betulia, Colombia. The primary sensor used will be for multispectral vegetation analysis, aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index along with surface reflectance to understand the health and distribution of the vegetation in this region. This is part of a flexible schedule, acting as a non-essential background filler task that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience, serving as an opportunistic survey within our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

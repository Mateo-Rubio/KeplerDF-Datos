The Earth-observation mission operator requests that the satellite constellation prioritize a standard operational tasking for capturing high-resolution optical imagery from the region of Suárez, Colombia. This mission is scheduled for delivery "in three days" around mid-day, representing a critical payload capture that must be treated as part of the baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. The imagery aims to provide clear daylight photography and true-color visual documentation to assess the local landscape and environment under normal operational status.

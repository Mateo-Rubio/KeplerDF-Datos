In preparation for a flexible background survey operation, our Earth-observation mission will commence its task on the horizon exactly three days from now, focusing on the overnight period when the sky is at its darkest. This endeavor, designated as a low priority importance request with a 'priority_level' of 1, aims to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis using a VNIR sensor over the region in Colombia, specifically targeting the city of Foraquira. This mission is crafted for opportunistic capture, allowing for scheduling adjustments as needed without compromising operational integrity. The primary objective here is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index across the landscape, providing essential insights into vegetation health and distribution under the veil of darkness.

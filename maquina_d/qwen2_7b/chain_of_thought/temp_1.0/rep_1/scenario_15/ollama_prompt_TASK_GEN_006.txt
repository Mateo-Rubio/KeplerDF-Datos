For our operational request, envisioning the day after tomorrow during the serene and illuminating hours of morning, we are seeking to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot using VNIR technology for daylight photography in Sintang, Indonesia. This critical task is prioritized as a low-importance, background survey mission with the stipulated deadline to deliver the data products firmly set for the morning on the day after tomorrow. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index without altering the stringent deadline or shifting it to any other part of the day.

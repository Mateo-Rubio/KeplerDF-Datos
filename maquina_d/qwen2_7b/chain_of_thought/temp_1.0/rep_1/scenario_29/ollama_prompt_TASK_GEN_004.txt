In light of Colombia's vibrant landscapes and diverse ecosystems, our Earth observation mission aims to capture crucial data on mid-day conditions in the Nunchía region for the day after tomorrow. Leveraging a VNIR sensor for multispectral vegetation analysis, we'll assess chlorophyll absorption levels across various agricultural and forested areas. This 'low priority importance' task holds the flexibility to be either dropped or rescheduled according to our current operational needs, while ensuring it contributes to an opportunistic background survey of environmental health in this region.

The priority allocation for this task reflects an absolute institutional importance, necessitating the execution of a critical payload capture for our commercial contract obligation. This specific mission is characterized by an urgent operational requirement for obtaining detailed data within the strict deadline of "in three days", which includes capturing the essence of the Colombian landscape at its most vibrant time of day—the afternoon period. A specialized request is made for high-resolution optical imagery through a VNIR sensor, aimed at providing an in-depth assessment of daylight photography and true-color visual documentation. We anticipate this acquisition will contribute to the comprehensive understanding and analysis of geographical features, urban and natural landscapes within Chivatá, without compromising the quality or timeliness of the data products.

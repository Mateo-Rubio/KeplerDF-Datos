Operating on a high-level urgency and acknowledging its position as a non-essential background filler task, this Earth observation mission aims for a true-color visual documentation through a low-resolution optical snapshot taken tomorrow in the evening. The target area lies within the beautiful country of Colombia, precisely at Vista Hermosa city. This task involves an opportunistic capture of vegetational health status by assessing chlorophyll absorption levels across the region using near-infrared reflection capabilities. Therefore, ensuring compliance with the operational deadline, we will provide water body boundaries and soil moisture assessments crucial for land management and agricultural planning by capturing the heat signature and surface temperature profile of the region in the evening hours.

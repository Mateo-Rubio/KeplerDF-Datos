STAGE 1 - REASONING

a) The exact string in 'target_day' is "tomorrow".

b) The exact string in 'target_diurnal_period' is "in the morning".

c) The single word contained in that string is "morning".

d) Confirming that the deadline is not being shifted to an adjacent part of the day, as "tomorrow" implies a specific date ahead, rather than a flexible time slot. The temporal parameters are intended to represent a strict operational deadline to deliver the data products, not the orbital capture window.

The target_day parameter does not imply any alteration, extension, shift, or reinterpretation of the deadline.

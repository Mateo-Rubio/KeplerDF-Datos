Tasking Request: Opportunistic SAR Scan of Sizzano Region in Italy

We are requesting an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to capture a cloud-penetrating snapshot of the ground geometry in the region of Sizzano, Italy. This low-priority importance task has a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture and is intended as a non-essential background filler task. The operator notes that this can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience.

The goal of this mission is to conduct a routine monitoring task as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography, clear optical imagery, or true-color visual documentation in the region of Sizzano, Italy.

The data must be ready by around mid-day in three days at the latest.

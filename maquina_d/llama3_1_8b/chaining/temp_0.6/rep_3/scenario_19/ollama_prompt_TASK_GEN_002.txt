We require a standard operational tasking for high-resolution optical snapshot imagery of the Italian countryside. Specifically, we are interested in capturing clear optical imagery of the region surrounding Valle Castellana during daylight hours. This will involve an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to pierce through any heavy weather and capture the ground geometry. The mission allocation authority has designated this task as a binding contract requirement, involving a critical payload capture and mandatory directive for baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We require the data to be delivered in three days during the afternoon.

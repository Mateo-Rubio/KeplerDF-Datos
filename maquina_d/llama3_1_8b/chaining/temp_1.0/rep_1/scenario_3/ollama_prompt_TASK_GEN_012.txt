Obtain high-resolution near-infrared reflection data of Malinau in Indonesia for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment to discriminate gradients. This is an absolute institutional priority, mandating a critical payload capture under a binding contract requirement due to its critical nature and un-preemptable mission lock. The objective is to achieve clear optical imagery with true-color visual documentation of the target area's near-infrared reflection signature by in three days in the evening.

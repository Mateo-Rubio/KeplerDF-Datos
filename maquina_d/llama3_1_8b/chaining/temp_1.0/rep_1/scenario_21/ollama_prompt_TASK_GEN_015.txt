Obtain high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography over the region of Sumatra, Indonesia, focusing on the city of Solok and its surrounding areas, which are characterized by lush vegetation. This standard operational tasking is part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and requires multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The mission allocation authority has designated this task as a commercial tasking, and we are working within a routine monitoring framework with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. Please complete the data collection in four days in the morning.

Task Request: Opportunistic Background Survey in Italy

We require an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to pierce heavy weather or capture ground geometry at Burzanella, Italy, which is part of the region of Italia. This task is a low priority importance request with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, to be treated as a non-essential background filler task where we can drop or reschedule at convenience. The objective of this mission is to conduct an active radar scan during the afternoon in three days.

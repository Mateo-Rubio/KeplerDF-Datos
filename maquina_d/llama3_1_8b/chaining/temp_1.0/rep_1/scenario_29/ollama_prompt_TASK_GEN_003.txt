Obtain high-resolution thermal mapping data for the Ketapang region in Indonesia, which is a standard operational tasking part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The goal is to capture a heat signature and surface temperature profile to perform thermal anomaly detection and mapping of heat emissions using active microwave surface imaging, ideally around mid-day in three days. This is a commercial tasking and a routine monitoring exercise, treating this as a standard workflow update.

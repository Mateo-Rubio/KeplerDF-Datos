We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This involves a high-resolution optical snapshot using multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over the region of France, specifically in the city of Venaco. This task is considered a standard operational tasking and should be treated as a commercial tasking with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. The data should be delivered in three days in the morning.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves a high-resolution optical snapshot using multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over the region of Sabana de Torres in Colombia. This is a commercial tasking with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, and we treat it as a non-essential background filler task where the operator explicitly notes to drop or reschedule at convenience. The data must be delivered in three days overnight.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography and clear optical imagery using the multispectral vegetation analysis capabilities of our VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The task location is in the Prati di Tivo area of Italia, and we aim to capture this data during the morning hours in four days.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This is a standard operational tasking with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, and we treat it as a background survey. The data should be captured the day after tomorrow in the morning.

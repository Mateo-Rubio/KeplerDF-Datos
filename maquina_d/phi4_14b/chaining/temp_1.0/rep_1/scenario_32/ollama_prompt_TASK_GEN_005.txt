We are requesting a low-priority satellite task for a flexible schedule to opportunistically capture imagery of Norte. As this is a non-essential background filler task, we acknowledge that this mission may be dropped or rescheduled at the operator's convenience. The objective is to perform an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging capabilities to achieve a cloud-penetrating capture of the terrain in and around Norte. This will allow for detailed ground geometry mapping regardless of heavy weather conditions. The data from this task would serve as background survey data and is considered a testbed tracking task within our current operational parameters. We look forward to the delivery of this data, which should be available "in three days overnight."

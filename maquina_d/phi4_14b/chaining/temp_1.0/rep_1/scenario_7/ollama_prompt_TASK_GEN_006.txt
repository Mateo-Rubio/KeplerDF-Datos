This mission involves a high-resolution optical snapshot targeting Indonesia's Ecuatorial region. As an absolute institutional priority and a binding contract requirement, it is essential that we secure a critical payload capture for this daylight photography operation within three days in the evening. The task must achieve clear optical imagery with true-color visual documentation of the target area. Due to its significant mission allocation priority, there will be no room for preemption; urgent operational execution and an un-preemptable mission lock are mandatory.

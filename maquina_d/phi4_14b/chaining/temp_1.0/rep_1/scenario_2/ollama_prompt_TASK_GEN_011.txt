We have an absolute institutional priority to conduct a critical payload capture using VNIR capabilities over Fobello, Italy. This task is a binding contract requirement aimed at performing multispectral vegetation analysis in the area. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance, crucial for understanding local vegetation health. This mission lock is un-preemptable, demanding urgent operational execution within our set constraints. The data must be captured in high-resolution daylight photography conditions and be ready in three days in the morning for immediate downstream use.

We have a standard operational tasking for monitoring activities over the Northern region of Italy. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters and represents normal operational status. We are specifically interested in conducting a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance using VNIR technology. The data collected should help provide insights into the current vegetative health across this area. The delivery of this data is crucial for tomorrow's overnight operations, ensuring we meet our schedule without any deviation.

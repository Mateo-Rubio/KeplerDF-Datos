We have a standard operational tasking that involves monitoring vegetation health in the picturesque region of Venaco, France. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, we require a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance studies, which will be critical for understanding local agricultural conditions. This task forms an integral part of our routine monitoring workflow, requiring fixed parameters to maintain normal operational status. The imagery must be captured in the morning, in three days in the morning, aligning with the planned schedule for consistent data collection.

We are requesting a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over the Prati di Tivo region in Italia. This mission involves capturing data to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index, which is crucial for understanding vegetation health and surface reflectance in this area. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking, this task carries fixed parameters indicative of normal operational status. The satellite imagery capture should be completed by in four days in the morning, ensuring timely data availability for our routine monitoring processes.

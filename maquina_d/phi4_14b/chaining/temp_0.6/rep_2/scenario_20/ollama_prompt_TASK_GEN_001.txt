We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing a normal operational status and a standard workflow update. Our objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance in this region. The data should be delivered in three days overnight, ensuring we have the necessary information for our ongoing environmental studies.

We have a standard operational tasking for a thermal mapping mission over the city of Norte in France. This request is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, reflecting normal operational status. The primary objective is to capture a detailed heat signature and surface temperature profile using thermal infrared (TIR) sensors. This will help us in detecting any thermal anomalies and mapping heat emissions across the region. We aim for this data collection to be completed in three days during the afternoon, ensuring timely delivery for analysis.

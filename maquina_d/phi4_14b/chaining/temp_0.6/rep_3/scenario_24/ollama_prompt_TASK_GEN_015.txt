We have a low-priority importance task for capturing active radar scans in Kuala Enok, Indonesia, using microwave surface imaging. This is intended to be an opportunistic capture, with a flexible schedule allowing for dropping or rescheduling at convenience. Please treat this as a non-essential background filler task, part of a background survey or testbed tracking task. We require the data delivery to be completed in four days in the morning.

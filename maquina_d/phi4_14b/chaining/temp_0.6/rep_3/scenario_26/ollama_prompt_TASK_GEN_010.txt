We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial tracking mission over the region of Puerto López in Colombia. This involves conducting an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to achieve cloud-penetrating capture and capture ground geometry, providing essential data even under heavy weather conditions. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The deadline for this task execution is set for "in three days in the evening," ensuring timely data availability within our workflow update framework.

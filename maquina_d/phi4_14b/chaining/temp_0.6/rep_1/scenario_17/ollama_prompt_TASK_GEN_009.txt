We have a low-priority importance task for an opportunistic capture in the Ecuatorial region of Indonesia. This flexible schedule allows us to drop or reschedule at convenience, treating it as a non-essential background filler task. The mission involves an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to achieve cloud-penetrating capture, which will be particularly useful in piercing heavy weather and capturing ground geometry. We aim for this data collection to occur in three days in the morning, providing us with the opportunity to integrate it into our broader observational framework as part of our background survey efforts.

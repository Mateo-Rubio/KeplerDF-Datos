We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner. The mission involves conducting a multispectral vegetation analysis in Corteno Golgi, Italy, using VNIR sensor technology. This routine monitoring activity is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters that reflect normal operational status. Our objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for this region. We aim to gather data "in three days in the evening" to ensure timely updates to our workflow.

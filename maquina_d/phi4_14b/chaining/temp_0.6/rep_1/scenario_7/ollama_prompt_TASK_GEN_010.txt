We have a standard operational tasking to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over Pantanillo in Colombia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, with fixed parameters for routine monitoring of the region's vegetation health. We aim to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate an NDVI index to evaluate surface reflectance. Please ensure this mission is scheduled as a commercial tasking within the normal operational workflow. The data must be delivered in three days in the morning.

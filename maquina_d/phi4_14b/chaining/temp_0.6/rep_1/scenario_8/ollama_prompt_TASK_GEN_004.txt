We have a low-priority importance request for an opportunistic capture in the Ituango region of Colombia. This task is a non-essential background filler where we can afford to drop or reschedule at convenience, treating it as a background survey. The goal is to utilize the multispectral vegetation analysis capabilities of our VNIR sensor to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance in this area. Given its flexible schedule, we aim for data capture specifically in three days in the morning.

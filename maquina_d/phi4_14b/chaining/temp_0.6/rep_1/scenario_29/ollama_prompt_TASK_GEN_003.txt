We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial mission focused on thermal mapping in the Ketapang region of Indonesia. The goal is to capture a detailed surface temperature profile, allowing us to detect any thermal anomalies and map heat emissions effectively. This routine monitoring activity is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. The data acquisition must be completed in three days around mid-day to ensure timely analysis and integration into our ongoing environmental assessment projects.

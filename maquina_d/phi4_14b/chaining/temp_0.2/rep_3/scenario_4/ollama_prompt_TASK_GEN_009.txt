We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial mission involving Rokan Hilir in Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. This task is scheduled for completion in three days in the morning, ensuring timely data availability for our ongoing workflow updates.

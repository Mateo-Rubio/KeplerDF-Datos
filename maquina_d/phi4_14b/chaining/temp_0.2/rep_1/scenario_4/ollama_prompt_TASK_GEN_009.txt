We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner. The mission involves capturing multispectral vegetation analysis over Rokan Hilir, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. We aim to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance using VNIR sensors. The data should be delivered in three days in the morning.

We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial mission that involves monitoring vegetation health in the region of Arsiero, Italy. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance analysis. We need this high-resolution optical snapshot to be captured in four days in the morning, ensuring we maintain our routine monitoring schedule without any deviations.

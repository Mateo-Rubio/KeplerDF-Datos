This mission involves capturing critical multispectral vegetation analysis data over Fobello, Italia, using a VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. This task is an absolute institutional priority and a binding contract requirement, necessitating an urgent operational execution with an un-preemptable mission lock. The satellite constellation planner must ensure the acquisition of this high-priority data specifically "in three days" and "in the morning." This critical payload capture is essential for maintaining our environmental monitoring standards.

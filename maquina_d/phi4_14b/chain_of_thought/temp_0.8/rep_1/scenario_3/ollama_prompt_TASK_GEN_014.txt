This is an absolute institutional priority request for a critical payload capture mission over the Borgo Valsugana region in Italia. The task involves conducting a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR capabilities to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index. Given the high importance of this mission, it is classified as a binding contract requirement with an urgent operational execution mandate. The data capture must be completed by the day after tomorrow during the overnight period. This is a non-negotiable deadline, and the mission lock is un-preemptable.

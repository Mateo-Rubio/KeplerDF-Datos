This mission involves an absolute institutional priority request for an active radar scan over Indonesia, focusing specifically on the Gilirang area. The primary objective is to obtain a cloud-penetrating capture of the ground geometry using microwave surface imaging capabilities. Given its high importance, this task falls under a binding contract requirement, necessitating urgent operational execution with an un-preemptable mission lock. The data products are critically needed for analysis in three days, during an overnight observation period. This ensures that we gather the most accurate and relevant information possible under these specific conditions.

We have an exciting opportunity to conduct an opportunistic and flexible satellite scan over the Tame region in Colombia. This task involves capturing a cloud-penetrating image of the ground geometry using microwave surface imaging. As a low priority importance request, we're open to adjusting the schedule to fit other mission needs. However, it's important that the data is ready by overnight in three days at the latest. Let's ensure this background survey doesn't clash with higher-priority missions while still achieving our observational goals.

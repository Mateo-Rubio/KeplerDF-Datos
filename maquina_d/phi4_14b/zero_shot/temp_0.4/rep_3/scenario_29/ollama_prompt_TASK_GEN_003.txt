We have a standard operational tasking for a thermal mapping mission over Ketapang, Indonesia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and involves capturing a heat signature or surface temperature profile using thermal infrared sensors. The mission aims to provide thermal anomaly detection and map heat emissions in the area. As this is a medium importance task, it follows our routine monitoring protocols with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Please ensure that the data products are ingested into the pipeline before mid-day in three days at the latest.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves a high-resolution optical snapshot using multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance in the region of Colombia, specifically targeting the city of San Carlos de Guaroa. This is a commercial tasking with an absolute deadline for data availability: in three days overnight.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring of a region in Italia, specifically targeting the city of Norte. This mission allocation has been designated as a binding contract requirement due to its medium importance level, involving commercial tasking as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot utilizing multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The data should be delivered in three days during the afternoon.

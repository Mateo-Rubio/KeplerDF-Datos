We require a high-resolution thermal mapping of Indonesia's Ketapang region to capture a surface temperature profile and identify any thermal anomalies, particularly around major water bodies, as part of our standard operational tasking and commercial monitoring requirements. This is a standard operational tasking part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission allocation for this task has been identified as a standard operational priority, and we require the data to be delivered in three days around mid-day.

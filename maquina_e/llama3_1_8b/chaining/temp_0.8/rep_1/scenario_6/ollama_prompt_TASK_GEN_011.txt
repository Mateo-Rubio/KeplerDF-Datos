Tasking Request for Routine Monitoring: Rheinwald, Switzerland Region

We require a standard operational tasking for a commercial tasking, part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This is a routine monitoring task involving multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level or calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance in the Rheinwald region of Switzerland. The target location is situated in the country of Switzerland, specifically in the city of Rheinwald. We aim to capture near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping or soil moisture assessment to discriminate gradients in this region.

Data delivery is required "in four days around mid-day".

We require a low priority importance task for opportunistic capture with a flexible schedule, to be treated as a non-essential background filler task where we can drop or reschedule at convenience. This task involves conducting an active thermal mapping using microwave surface imaging to capture a heat signature and surface temperature profile in the evening of the day after tomorrow. The location is Correggioli, Italy, and we aim to obtain high-resolution thermal data for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions.

Tasking request for a SAR-based active radar scan of Burzanella in Italia. This is a low priority importance request with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, and can be treated as a background survey or testbed tracking task to be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The objective is to perform microwave surface imaging for cloud-penetrating capture, piercing heavy weather or capturing ground geometry, taking advantage of daylight conditions. Delivery of the data is required in three days during the afternoon.

We are requesting a low priority importance task for an opportunistic thermal mapping mission to capture a heat signature and surface temperature profile over the region of Primorsko-goranska županija in Hrvatska. This will involve an active thermal scan using microwave surface imaging for cloud-penetrating capture, allowing us to pierce heavy weather and obtain a true-color thermal map. The mission allocation is flexible, with a fixed schedule for opportunistic capture, and the operator has explicitly noted that it can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if needed. The goal is to conduct this task as part of a background survey within in three days during the afternoon.

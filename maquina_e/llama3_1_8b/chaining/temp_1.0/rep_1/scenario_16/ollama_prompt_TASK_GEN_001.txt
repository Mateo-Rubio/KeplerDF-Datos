We require a standard operational tasking for commercial satellite imaging over Colombia, focusing on the city of Santander de Quilichao. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and involves a routine monitoring requirement. The objective is to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot using multispectral vegetation analysis for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating a vegetation NDVI index, along with surface reflectance. The task must be completed in three days in the evening.

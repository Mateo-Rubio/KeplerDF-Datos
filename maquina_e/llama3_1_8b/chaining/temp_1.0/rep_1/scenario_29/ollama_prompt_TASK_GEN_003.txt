We are assigning a standard operational tasking to capture thermal mapping data for the region of Ketapang, Indonesia, using microwave surface imaging to penetrate cloud cover and detect heat signatures. This commercial tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and routine monitoring. The mission allocation authority for this tasking is classified as medium importance and is not subject to any deadline changes or flexible scheduling requirements. Data must be delivered in three days around mid-day.

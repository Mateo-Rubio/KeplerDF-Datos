Task Request for Earth-Observation Mission: Vegetation Analysis in Ibagué, Colombia
We require a low-priority importance request with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance in the region of Ibagué, Colombia. Specifically, we are interested in capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of the area using daylight photography for true-color visual documentation. Given its low priority importance, this task can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if other tasks take precedence. The data must be ready by the day after tomorrow overnight at the latest.

Capture high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over Italia, specifically around Greve in Chianti, which is a key location for this baseline pipeline tracking setup. This task has been designated as an absolute institutional priority and a binding contract requirement involving a critical payload capture, given its high importance and urgency. The data products are required to be delivered by overnight in four days, allowing us to integrate the results into our standard operational tasking and commercial tasking schedules with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

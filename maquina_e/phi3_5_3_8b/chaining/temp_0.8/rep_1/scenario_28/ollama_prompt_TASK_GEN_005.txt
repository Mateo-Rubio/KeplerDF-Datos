As part of our routine monitoring within the Earth-observation mission framework, we are setting forth a non-essential background filler task for thermographic surveillsion over Cerasuolo, Italia. We aim to procure thermal mapping that will enable us to capture a heat signature and construct a surface temperature profile. This initiative is scheduled flexibly with the availability of data anticipated "the day after tomorrow around mid-day". Operators are advised that this task has been assigned low priority importance, hence it can be adjusted or rescheduled at their discretion without impacting primary mission objectives.

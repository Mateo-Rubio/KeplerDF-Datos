As part of our routine monitoring for thermal anomalies within Indonesia's archipelago, we have a scheduled TIR sensor pass over Ketapang in three days around mid-day. This task falls under standard operational procedure and is critical to maintaining our continuous surveillance objectives. It involves capturing surface temperature profiles and heat signatures that are essential for detecting environmental changes and potential risks within this region. The data collected will be used solely for monitoring purposes, ensuring we keep track of any significant thermal developments in the area.

In accordance with our ongoing efforts to understand regional environmental changes, we are initiating an SAR operation for active radar scanning over the city of Dolores in Colombia. This task has been designated as a low priority importance activity within our operational spectrum and is intended to provide cloud-penetrating capabilities and detailed ground geometry analysis that complements our baseline data collection efforts. Given its non-essential nature, this operation can be accommodated with flexibility; however, it must align with the established workflow updates as we have a strict deadline of in three days in the morning for task completion to maintain coherence within our dataset scheduling.

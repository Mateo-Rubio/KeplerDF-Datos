In three days around mid-day, we require a standard operational tasking to execute multispectral vegetation analysis in Pavullo nel Frignano, Italy. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance evaluation. This routine monitoring will contribute significantly to our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, enssuring a comprehensive understanding of vegetative health in this region.

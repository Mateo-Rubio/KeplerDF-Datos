As part of our routine monitoring to maintain a comprehensive environmental dataset, we have identified Corteno Golgi in Italy as the target location for multispectral vegetation analysis. This standard operational tasking is scheduled to occur three days from now in the evening and will involve capturing true-color visual documentation alongside assessments of chlorophyll absorption levels using near-infrared reflection techniques. The collected data will assist us in calculating a Vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) index, as well as surface reflectance measurements to help discriminate between different vegetative health states and boundaries effectively.

This is a standard operational tasking to perform thermal mapping in La Vega, Colombia. The objective is to capture a heat signature for surface temperature profiling using passive microwave scanning techniques that will pierce through any heavy weather conditions and provide detailed insight into the local geothermal activity or urban heating patterns as they develop during evening hours. This task forms an integral part of our baseline pipeline tracking, enssuring continuous monitoring for anomaly detection within a normal operational schedule. The data must be delivered by the day after tomorrow in the evening to maintain synchronicity with our reporting cycles and analytical workflow updates.

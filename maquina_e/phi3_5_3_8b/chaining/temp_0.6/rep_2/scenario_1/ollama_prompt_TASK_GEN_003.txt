Request for Thermal Mapping Task in Mamuju, Indonesia

The objective is to conduct a thermal mapping mission using passive microwave surface imaging technology. Our target area of interest lies within the city of Mamuju in Indonesia, where we aim to capture detailed heat signatures and establish comprehensive surface temperature profiles. This task, categorized as low priority importance, offers flexibility in scheduling with an opportunistic approach for data acquisition. Please be advised that this operation is intended for thermal anomaly detection and mapping purposes within the specified locale. The deliverables are expected to be available by the day after tomorrow around mid-day, ensuring timely processing of these critical datasets into our environmental observation repository.

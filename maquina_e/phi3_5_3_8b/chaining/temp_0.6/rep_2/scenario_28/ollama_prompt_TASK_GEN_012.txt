We are setting up a standard operational tasking for an Earth-observation mission over Ecuatorial city in Indonesia. The aim is to acquire high-resolution optical snapshots during daylight photography, capturing clear and true-color visual documentation of the region around mid-day within three days from today's date. This routine monitoring aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup and requires a microwave surface imaging scan using active radar to ensure cloud-penetrating capture alongside the optical observations for comprehensive analysis.

Today in the evening, we have a standard operational tasking to deploy an active radar scan for microwave surface imaging over Tuluá, Colombia. This mission is part of our routine monitoring efforts and will be carried out with the objective of capturing thermal mapping data. We aim to document heat signatures across various terrains in the region to create a comprehensive surface temperature profile. The results are expected to contribute significantly to our ongoing research into environmental changes, particularly focusing on detecting any unusual thermal anomalies that may indicate underlying geological or ecological shifts.

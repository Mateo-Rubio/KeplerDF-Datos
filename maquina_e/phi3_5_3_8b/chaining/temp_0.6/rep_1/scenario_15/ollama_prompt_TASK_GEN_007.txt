Initiate an absolute institutional priority directive for a mandatory TIR thermal mapping mission over Bolívar in Colombia. The objective is to capture a heat signature within the target area by high-resolution optical snapshot techniques, enssuring true-color visual documentation of surface temperature profiles and detecting any potential thermal anomalies that may indicate critical environmental or infrastructural concerns. This operation must be completed as part of an un-preemptable mission lock with a fixed schedule within three days in the evening deadline. The data obtained will play a pivotal role in our institution's capacity to respond promptly and effectively to any urgent issues that may arise, thereby maintaining operational readiness and environmental monitoring standards at their peak.

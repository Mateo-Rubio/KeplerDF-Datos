As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am issuing a task to our automated satellite constellation planner for the upcoming SAR (active radar scan) operation. The focus is on Uribe in Colombia, where we aim to penetrate heavy cloud cover and accurately map ground geometry during overnight conditions within three days from now. Given its low operational priority, this task can be scheduled flexibly; however, it remains a valuable opportunity for continuous monitoring of the region's surface features under varying weather patterns. The data collected will contribute to our understanding of terrain dynamics in cloud-prone areas and assist with environmental studies where optical imagery is not feasible due to persistent overcast conditions.

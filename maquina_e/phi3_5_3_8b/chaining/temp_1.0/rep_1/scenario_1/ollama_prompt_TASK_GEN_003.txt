The mission planner is instructed to organize an opportunity for a groundbresure thermal mapping operation over Mamuju in Indonesia. This task requires capturing surface temperature profiles and heat signatures using high-resolution active radar scanning via microwave imaging, suitable for cloud-penetrating capture and detailed terrain analysis. As the priority is low importance, the schedule remains flexible with the capability to be adjusted or rescheduled at convenience due to its opportunistic nature. The collected data should ideally culminate by the day after tomorrow around mid-day without imposing immediate deadlines or fixed timestamps.

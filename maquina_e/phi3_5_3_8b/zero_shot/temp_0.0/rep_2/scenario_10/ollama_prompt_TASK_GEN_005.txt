We have a standard operational tasking for Norte, where we aim to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis in the morning following daylight hours. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and requires us to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance evaluation. The results are needed by the day after tomorrow morning at the latest, ensuring they are ingested into our data processing pipeline in a timely manner.

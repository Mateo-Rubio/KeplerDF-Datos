This is part of our regular monitoring routine to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI). The required data products are needed by in four days overnight at the latest, with a strict delivery deadline set for ensuring timely ingestion into our baseline pipeline tracking system.

In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols for routine monitoring and commercial data collection, we are scheduling a SAR-based active radar scan mission over Belaga in Malaysia. The objective of this mission is to achieve cloud-penetrating capture capabilities for microwave surface imaging, which will provide us with critical insights into ground geometry and environmental conditions that are not visible through optical means alone. This task aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup and contributes essential data towards maintaining the continuity of our Earth observation efforts in this region.

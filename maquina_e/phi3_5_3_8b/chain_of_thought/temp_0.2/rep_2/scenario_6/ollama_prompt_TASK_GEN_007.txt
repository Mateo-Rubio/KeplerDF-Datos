In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols and within the framework of routine monitoring, we are scheduling a high-resolution optical snapshot aimed at capturing true-color visual documentation over Zipaquirá in Colombia. This mission is set to occur "in four days" during the morning hours, ensuring that environmental conditions are optimal for daylight photography. The data acquired will contribute significantly to our baseline pipeline tracking and multispectral analysis of urban landscapes within this region.

In accordance with our Earth-observation objectives and considering the natural phenomena we aim to document, this request calls for a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities. Specifically, we seek to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance measurements. The targeted execution of this mission is scheduled for three days from now during the evening hours, which will provide optimal lighting conditions conducive to accurate data collection and analysis. Given its low operational priority status, this task can be rescheduled at our convenience; however, it remains an essential part of our environmental monitoring efforts.

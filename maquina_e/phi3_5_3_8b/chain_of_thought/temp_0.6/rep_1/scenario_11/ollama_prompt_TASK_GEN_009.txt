In accordance with our routine monitoring schedule, we are tasked with conducting a thermal mapping operation using infrared technology within three days, specifically in the evening hours, without altering or extending this strict operational window. This initiative is set as a standard operational tasking to capture a heat signature and surface temperature profile of Kutai Timur, Indonesia, which will contribute valuable data for our environmental studies on thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions. The targeted area's clear optical imagery during the evening time enhances the potential for accurate measurements when atmospheric conditions are typically more stable, thus providing a reliable dataset for further analysis.

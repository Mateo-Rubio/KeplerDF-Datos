As a mandatory directive, we are tasking our satellite constellation with a standard operational objective in four days' time, specifically during the nighttime hours. The aim is to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities to assess soil moisture and discriminate gradients, which aligns with routine monitoring within our baseline pipeline tracking system. This operation forms a critical part of our environmental observation efforts in Colombia and will enhance our understanding of the agricultural landscape at San Carlos de Guaroa under different nocturnal conditions. The deliverables from this mission lock are essential for maintaining up-to-date records within our operational parameters, ensuring continuity and accuracy in our data collection processes.

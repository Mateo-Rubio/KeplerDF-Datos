The task at hand involves obtaining near-infrared reflection data from water bodies and soil to assess moisture gradients. This effort requires a high-resolution optical snapshot for true-color visual documentation during the afternoon on "the day after tomorrow". While this mission holds importance, it is classified as low priority, thus allowing some flexibility in scheduling without impacting our orbital capture window. Please note that due to its non-essential nature, this background survey can be easily dropped or rescheduled at the operator's convenience.

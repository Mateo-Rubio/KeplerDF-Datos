As a request for our Earth-observation mission, we are scheduling a standard operational tasking with medium importance to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR technology in Sabana de Torres, Colombia. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index alongside surface reflectance, which will contribute significantly to our environmental monitoring efforts. This tasking has been set for three days from now during the nighttime hours to ensure optimal conditions for data collection and analysis.

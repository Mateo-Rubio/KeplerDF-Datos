In accordance with our institutional procedures for routine monitoring and baseline pipeline tracking, we are tasking a VNIR sensor-based operation aimed at multispectral vegetation analysis. This mission is scheduled to occur on the day following tomorrow during early hours in the morning. The goal herein is not only to assess chlorophyll absorption levels but also to calculate a precise NDVI index and surface reflectance for effective environmental monitoring. Given its low operational priority, this task can be adjusted or postponed as necessary based on convenience; however, it remains an essential component of our comprehensive Earth observation strategy.

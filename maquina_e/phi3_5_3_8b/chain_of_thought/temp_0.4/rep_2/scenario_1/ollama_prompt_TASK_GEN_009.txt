In accordance with our current priorities and the flexibility afforded by a low-importance mission directive, we seek to schedule an opportunistic capture session focused on active radar scan using microwave surface imaging capabilities. This initiative is aimed at Kubu Raya in Indonesia, where cloud penetration and ground geometry assessment are of interest. The operation is planned for three days from now during the evening hours, with a clear understanding that this task can be adjusted or rescheduled based on operational convenience.

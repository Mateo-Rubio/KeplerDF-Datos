In accordance with our current Earth-observation mission requirements, we seek to conduct a thermal mapping operation using high-resolution infrared technology. The objective is to capture a heat signature and surface temperature profile of an area in Colombia that has been identified for its potential environmental significance. Specifically, the focus will be on Paya city during tomorrow afternoon hours. This task carries low operational importance at this time, thus it can be scheduled flexibly. Operators are advised to consider rescheduling if necessary and are not under strict institutional pressure to execute immediately. The data collected from these observations is expected to contribute valuable insights into the thermal dynamics of the region without preempting more critical missions currently in our pipeline.

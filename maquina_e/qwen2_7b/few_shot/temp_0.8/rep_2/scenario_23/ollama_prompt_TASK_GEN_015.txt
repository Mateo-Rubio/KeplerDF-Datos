Logging a standard operational tasking over Kepulauan Meranti in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim for a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance, which will provide valuable insights into agricultural health and environmental status. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and we need the products by four days' time at the latest during the overnight period.

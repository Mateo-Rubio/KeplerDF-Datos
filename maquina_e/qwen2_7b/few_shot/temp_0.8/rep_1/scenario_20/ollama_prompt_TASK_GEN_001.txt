Logging a standard operational tasking over San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim for a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance, focusing on the agricultural developments in the area. This routine monitoring is at normal operational status, with a deadline set for three days hence overnight. The products should be included in our data flow at that time.

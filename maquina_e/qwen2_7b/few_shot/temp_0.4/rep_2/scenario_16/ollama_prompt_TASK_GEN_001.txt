Logging a standard operational tasking for Santander de Quilichao in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over the area, assessing chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the NDVI index along with surface reflectance to provide insights on the health of vegetation. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the deadline for delivering the data products is set for three days from now in the evening.

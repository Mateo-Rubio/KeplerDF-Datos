Logging a low priority importance task, this request functions as a flexible background survey over Puerto Wilches in Colombia. Our aim is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance using a VNIR multispectral analysis. It's part of our opportunistic capture tasks for normal operational status where we can drop or reschedule at convenience if necessary. Yet, should conditions open up in three days for an early morning, delivering this valuable data is crucial by the target deadline.

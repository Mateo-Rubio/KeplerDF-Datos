Tackling a non-essential background filler task with low priority importance, we aim to opportunistically capture multispectral vegetation data over Ecuatorial in Indonesia as part of our flexible schedule. Our objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the areas south of town. This background survey operates at normal operational status, allowing for a convenient drop or rescheduling should priorities shift. The deadline for product delivery is set in three days around mid-day, with no substitutions for a different time period.

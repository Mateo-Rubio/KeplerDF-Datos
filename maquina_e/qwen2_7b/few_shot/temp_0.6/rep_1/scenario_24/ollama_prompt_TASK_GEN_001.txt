Logging a standard operational tasking over Sabana de Torres in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over the area to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance, which will help us understand the health status of various vegetation types. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the deadline for delivering the products is set for three days from now, overnight.

Given the operational guidelines, our Earth-observation mission request for a thermal mapping task in Colombia involves capturing heat signatures overnight in three days' time. Specifically, we aim to observe and document the thermal anomalies across Santa Helena del Opón with a clear focus on the overnight period when the atmosphere is at its most stable. This high-resolution optical snapshot will provide invaluable insights into surface temperature profiles without interference from daylight conditions or atmospheric disturbances. As this request holds low priority importance, it's framed as a flexible task that can be accommodated based on operational convenience while maintaining the deadline for data delivery in three days during the overnight hours.

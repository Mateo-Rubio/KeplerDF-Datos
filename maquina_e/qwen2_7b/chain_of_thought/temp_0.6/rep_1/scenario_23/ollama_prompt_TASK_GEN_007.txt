Given the stringent deadline of capturing imagery around mid-day in four days, our mission allocation prioritizes this task as a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation. Specifically, we are to conduct an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to capture cloud-penetrating imagery over La Cumbre, Colombia. This high-resolution optical snapshot will serve critical applications requiring clear visibility through atmospheric conditions, offering unparalleled insight into the region's topography and surface characteristics at the optimal time for optimal lighting conditions.

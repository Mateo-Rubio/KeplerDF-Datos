In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle above the Indonesian city of Ecuatorial, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, with flexible scheduling considerations for opportunistic data collection. The primary objective is to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels using the unique properties of near-infrared reflection. This will provide valuable insights into environmental conditions without the interference of daylight or atmospheric conditions that might obscure such observations during the day. The deadline for this data delivery is strictly set for overnight, as specified in our operational guidelines, ensuring that we capture the necessary information under optimal conditions for near-infrared imaging.

In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have come out, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a detailed active radar scan across Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Sesquilé. This mission is designated as a low priority importance task, with flexible scheduling considerations in place. The primary objective is to perform cloud-penetrating capture using microwave surface imaging, providing us with critical data for understanding ground geometry and surface features without the interference of atmospheric conditions. This "overnight" SAR scan will serve as a standard operational tasking, contributing to our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters that represent normal operational status.

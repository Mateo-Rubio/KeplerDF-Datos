On the day after tomorrow, at the precise time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we are tasked with capturing thermal mapping data for Malinau, Indonesia. This mission involves a "TIR" sensor, which will be used to capture heat signatures across the region overnight. The goal is to gather detailed information on surface temperatures and potential thermal anomalies without the interference of daylight or cloud cover. This low priority importance request is framed as a "background survey", with the understanding that it can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if necessary, serving as a "testbed tracking task" for our satellite constellation planner.

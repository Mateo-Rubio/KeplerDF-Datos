In three days, at the time of overnight, we request the deployment of our satellite constellation to conduct an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. This cloud-penetrating capture is aimed at providing detailed ground geometry data for the region of Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Uribe. The mission's priority level is set to low importance, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates this task as a non-essential background filler operation. We aim to deliver thermal mapping capabilities to capture heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, essential for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions in the area. This operational request is made with the understanding that it represents a deadline for data product delivery, not an orbital capture window.

On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we request the capture of thermal mapping data for the region of Italia, specifically focusing on the city of Roasio. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, with flexible scheduling considerations. The primary sensor to be utilized for this mission is the TIR (Thermal Infrared) sensor, aimed at capturing heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the region overnight. This data collection will serve as a background survey, providing insights into thermal anomalies and heat emissions without the interference of daylight or cloud cover.

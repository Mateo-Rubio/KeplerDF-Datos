In response to the urgent operational execution required by our binding contract with the Malaysian government for critical payload capture, we are tasked with conducting a standard operational tasking using the VNIR sensor. This mission aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index of vegetation in Kabong, Malaysia, through a high-resolution optical snapshot taken overnight in four days. This "cloud-penetrating capture" will provide essential data for environmental monitoring and management, ensuring that we adhere strictly to the deadline without altering or extending it.

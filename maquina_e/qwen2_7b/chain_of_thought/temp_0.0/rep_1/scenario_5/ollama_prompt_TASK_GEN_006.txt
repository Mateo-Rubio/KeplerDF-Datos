In four days, at the time when the world is enveloped in the serene calm of overnight, our Earth-observation mission will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Indonesia's beautiful yet industrious city, Pangkalan Bun. This daylight photography will serve as a true-color visual documentation, offering a clear and detailed image that can be used for various purposes such as urban planning, environmental monitoring, or disaster response. Given the nature of this request being of low priority importance, we understand that it may be scheduled flexibly, allowing for it to be captured opportunistically at our convenience without compromising the quality or integrity of the data. This non-essential background survey will contribute to our ongoing efforts in providing comprehensive and reliable information about our planet's diverse landscapes.

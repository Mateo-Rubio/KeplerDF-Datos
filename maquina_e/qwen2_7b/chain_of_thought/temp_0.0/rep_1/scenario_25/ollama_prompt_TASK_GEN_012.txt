On the day after tomorrow, at the precise time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle across the vast Indonesian sky, we are tasked with capturing a detailed snapshot of the Ecuatorial city using our VNIR sensor. This mission is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates opportunistic capture. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index across various landscapes within this region, providing valuable insights into the health of local ecosystems without the urgency of an immediate deadline. This non-essential background survey will serve as a testbed tracking task, offering us the opportunity to refine our techniques for future missions with higher priority.

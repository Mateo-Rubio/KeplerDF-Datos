In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we request a standard operational tasking for a near-infrared sensor capture over the city of Piacenza in Italia. This mission aims to provide us with a detailed near-infrared reflection dataset, which will be crucial for mapping water body boundaries and assessing soil moisture levels. The imagery collected during this time window will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking efforts, offering valuable insights into environmental conditions without altering the strict deadline for data delivery.

In three days, at the time of overnight, we request the capture of a low priority importance task involving the use of VNIR sensors for multispectral vegetation analysis. This will assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for the region in Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of San Martín. The goal is to obtain detailed information about the vegetation health and land cover conditions under the cover of darkness, ensuring that thermal anomalies or unique features are not obscured by daylight conditions.

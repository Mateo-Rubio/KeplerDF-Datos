In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we request the capture of a low priority importance task. This involves a low priority importance request for a nighttime VNIR (visual near-infrared) scan over Betulia, Colombia. The purpose is to conduct a flexible schedule, opportunistic capture of a non-essential background filler task that will serve as a testbed tracking task. We aim to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for this region under the cover of darkness, ensuring minimal interference from daylight conditions.

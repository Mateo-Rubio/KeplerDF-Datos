In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data over the picturesque region of Italia, specifically focusing on the charming town of Monte Romano. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling that accommodates our operational needs without compromising the quality or integrity of the data collection process. The primary objective is to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries using near-infrared reflection, which will provide invaluable insights into environmental conditions and support decision-making processes in the area. This "background survey" aims to contribute to ongoing monitoring efforts without imposing any urgent operational constraints.

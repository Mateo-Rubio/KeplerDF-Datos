In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we request a high-priority SAR active radar scan over the Indonesian region, specifically focusing on the city of Kuala Enok. This mission is considered a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status. The primary objective is to capture detailed microwave surface imaging for cloud-penetrating capture, ensuring comprehensive coverage of ground geometry without interference from weather conditions. This data collection will serve as a critical input for our baseline pipeline tracking setup, contributing to our ongoing monitoring efforts and providing essential information for decision-making processes.

In approximately four days, with a strict operational priority aligned to this precise deadline, our satellite constellation will undertake a critical SAR mission over the picturesque region of Puerto Libertador, Colombia. The task focuses on capturing an essential, active radar scan during the serene and optimal conditions of the morning period.

For clarity, our aim is to acquire detailed microwave surface imaging from dawn until mid-morning when the sun's angle minimizes atmospheric distortion and provides enhanced clarity. This will enable us to perform a cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry, crucial for applications in environmental monitoring, urban planning, and disaster management. The data gathered under these specific day and time parameters will offer invaluable insights, ensuring comprehensive and actionable results that meet the stringent standards set forth by our contractual obligations and institutional priorities.

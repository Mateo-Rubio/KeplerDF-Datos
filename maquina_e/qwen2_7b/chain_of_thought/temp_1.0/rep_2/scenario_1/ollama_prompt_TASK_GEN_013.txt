In strict adherence to our operational guidelines, this mission seeks to acquire thermal anomalies in Santa Rosa del Sur, Colombia on "the day after tomorrow" specifically during the "overnight" period. Acknowledging its high importance as an "absolute institutional priority" with a "binding contract requirement", we require immediate and un-preemptable execution, highlighting the urgent operational nature and the necessity of conducting this task at midnight when atmospheric conditions are most favorable for capturing high-quality near-infrared reflectance data, critical for detailed water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment without the interference of solar light or atmospheric particulates.

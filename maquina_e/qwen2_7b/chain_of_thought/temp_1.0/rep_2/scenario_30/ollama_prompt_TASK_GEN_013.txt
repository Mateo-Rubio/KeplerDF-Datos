The Earth-observation mission allocation task is to capture high-resolution imagery of the location in Puerto Rico, Colombia using the near-infrared sensor on "the day after tomorrow", which falls under the morning hours. The mission is prioritized as a critical requirement due to its 'level 3' designation, necessitating an "absolute institutional priority" for operational execution. We aim to acquire clear daylight photography of the region under heavy weather conditions through microwave surface imaging capabilities, aligning with our active radar (SAR) sensor technology. As such, the deadline dictates urgent operational requirements and is not subject to any scheduling adjustments based on the deadline proximity.

On the upcoming day, three days ahead when the sun begins to set and the sky turns its most vibrant shades of orange and red, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis imagery across Colombia. Focusing specifically on the charming town of Caramanta, we aim for an optimal evening capture time to evaluate the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a comprehensive NDVI index that reveals the health status of the local vegetation canopy against the backdrop of the rapidly cooling evening temperature. This low-priority task will serve as a valuable testbed tracking endeavor, providing us with a snapshot of the environmental conditions that are conducive for further agricultural assessments and land use planning.

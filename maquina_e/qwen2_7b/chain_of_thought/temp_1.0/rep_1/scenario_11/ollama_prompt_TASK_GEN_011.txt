In accordance with our operational priorities, there's a standard operational tasking requirement to capture high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis data from the VNIR sensor for Tagliole, Italy within the strict deadline stipulated as "in three days". The target will occur during overnight conditions. Our crew aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index with a view to performing surface reflectance mapping to identify potential land use issues or health threats due to plant stress. This data is to be collected under controlled conditions, providing a comprehensive overview of the area's ecological status at this particular point in time.

With a low priority importance ("priority_level" = 1), our mission aims to capture essential environmental data from Indonesia using the near-infrared (NIR) sensor. Specifically, we target the Bata region during the afternoon hours of "the day after tomorrow". This data acquisition is part of a flexible schedule where it can be accommodated as an opportune background task without compromising the main operational workflows. The goal is to map water bodies and assess soil moisture accurately with this sensor's unique ability to discriminate gradients, providing valuable insights without impacting more urgent tasks.

In four days, at the peak of the overnight hours when the Earth's shadow envelops this region, we aim for a high-resolution optical snapshot of the Colombian territory stretching across the Bolívar province. This daylight photography mission, as it is tagged with priority level one status, embodies a flexible schedule requirement—a low-priority importance task that is to be addressed opportunistically and rescheduled as convenient. We are seeking a clear optical imagery capturing the essence of this location under the cover of darkness, a unique moment when the thermal anomalies might not be masked by sunlight, allowing for an unimpeded observation into its natural and potentially man-made features. With no need for urgency in fulfilling this request, we consider it as a background survey, a non-essential task that does not demand immediate attention. The operational objective here is to gather data on the heat signature and surface temperature profile without the complications of daytime sunlight interference or vegetation absorption effects, thus enabling us to assess the impact of various phenomena under purely night-time conditions.

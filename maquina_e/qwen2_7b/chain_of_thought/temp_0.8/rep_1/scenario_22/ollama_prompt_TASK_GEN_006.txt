On the appointed day, four days from now, we are tasked with capturing a near-infrared (NIR) active radar scan of the Ecuatorial region in Indonesia. Our objective is to map the surface of the area using the capabilities of near-infrared sensors, specifically focusing on water body boundaries and soil moisture assessment. This task demands an "overnight" capture to ensure we can penetrate through any cloud cover that might be present during daylight hours, guaranteeing comprehensive coverage of the region's topography and hydrological features.

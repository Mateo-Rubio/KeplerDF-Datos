In response to our priority-level 3 directive regarding Colombian territory, specifically focusing on the region encompassing the city of Guamal in Colombia for operations scheduled for tomorrow morning, we are initiating a critical payload capture utilizing our Near-Infrared (NIR) sensor. This mission aims to provide essential data for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment, which will be captured through our system's capability for near-infrared reflection analysis during the specified timeframe. The goal is to deliver a comprehensive thermal mapping profile that ensures the delivery of crucial insights into environmental conditions within this specific region of interest by tomorrow morning.

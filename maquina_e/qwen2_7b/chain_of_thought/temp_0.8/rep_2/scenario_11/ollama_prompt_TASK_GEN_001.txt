To schedule an operational task with high flexibility and to serve as a low priority importance request, we aim for a true-color visual documentation snapshot of the region in Colombia, specifically Puerto López. This request is framed as a non-essential background filler task that can be accommodated within our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The mission involves capturing daylight imagery overnight on "tomorrow", which aligns with the operational requirements without impacting urgent deadlines or prioritized tasks. Our intention is to assess surface conditions and environmental phenomena through an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging techniques, particularly focusing on cloud-penetrating capture capabilities essential for our comprehensive data collection process.

With a priority level set at medium importance, this operational request aims for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging techniques to capture cloud-penetrating views of Puerto López, Colombia. The mission is scheduled for delivery exactly three days ahead, focusing on gathering insightful data in the evening when conditions are ideal for high-quality radar imagery. This strategic timing allows for the acquisition of essential information during a period when visibility and atmospheric conditions are most conducive for our SAR objectives, ensuring optimal surface geometry and surface temperature profile mapping.

In four days, at the time of overnight, we request a low priority importance tasking for a nighttime VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis. This will involve capturing high-resolution optical snapshots as daylight photography, aiming to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the region in Colombia, specifically focusing on San Martín.

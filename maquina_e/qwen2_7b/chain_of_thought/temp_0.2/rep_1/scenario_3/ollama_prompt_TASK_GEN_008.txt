In three days, at the time of overnight, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Gazzola, Italia using our VNIR sensor. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the vegetation NDVI index for this area, which will serve as a background survey or testbed tracking task. The goal is to gather near-infrared reflection data to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture, providing critical information without the need for an urgent operational execution.

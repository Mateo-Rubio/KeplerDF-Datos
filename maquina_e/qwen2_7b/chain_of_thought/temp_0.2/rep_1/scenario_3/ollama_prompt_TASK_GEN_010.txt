In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun to twinkle in the sky, we are tasked with capturing a near-infrared reflection snapshot of the city of Monterrey, Colombia. This nighttime mission aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels across the region, providing critical data for environmental monitoring and agricultural management. As this is a standard operational tasking, it will be executed according to fixed parameters representing normal operational status, ensuring that we capture the necessary information without compromising other baseline pipeline tracking setups.

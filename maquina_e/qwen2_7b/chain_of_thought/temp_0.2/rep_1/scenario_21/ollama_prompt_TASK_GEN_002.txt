In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have come out, our satellite constellation will capture thermal data over the picturesque region of Italia, specifically focusing on the charming city of Santa Croce sull'Arno. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling to accommodate any necessary adjustments or rescheduling at your convenience. The primary sensor involved will be the TIR (Thermal Infrared) instrument, which will map the heat signature across the landscape, providing invaluable insights into surface temperature profiles and thermal anomalies. This non-essential background survey aims to capture the essence of this region under the cover of darkness, offering a unique perspective on its thermal dynamics without the interference of daylight.

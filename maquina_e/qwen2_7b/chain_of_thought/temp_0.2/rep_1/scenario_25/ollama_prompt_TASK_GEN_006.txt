In four days, during the morning hours, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Singkep Barat in Indonesia using our VNIR sensor. This involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index for vegetation health assessment. The primary objective is to gather detailed multispectral data that will provide insights into the environmental conditions and plant health without altering the deadline or shifting the time frame to an adjacent part of the day.

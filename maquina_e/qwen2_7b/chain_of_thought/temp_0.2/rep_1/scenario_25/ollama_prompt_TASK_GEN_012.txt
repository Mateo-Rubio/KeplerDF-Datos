On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun their nightly dance across the sky, our satellite constellation will be tasked with capturing a low-priority importance snapshot using the VNIR sensor. This high-resolution optical snapshot aims to provide a clear visual documentation of the landscape in Indonesia, specifically focusing on the region known as Ecuatorial. The overnight period is chosen for this task due to its minimal interference from sunlight and potential for clearer imagery under darker conditions. This "non-essential background filler task" will serve as an opportunistic capture, allowing us to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate a vegetation NDVI index without the constraints of day-to-day operational schedules. The data collected during this "background survey" will contribute to our understanding of the region's ecological health and provide insights for future environmental monitoring activities.

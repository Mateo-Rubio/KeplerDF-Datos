In four days, during the morning hours, we request a low priority importance tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared sensors. This operational objective aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the region of Moncalieri, Italy. The goal is to capture high-resolution optical snapshots as daylight photography or true-color visual documentation to provide detailed insights into the vegetation health and land use patterns in the area.

In three days, at the time of overnight, we request the capture of a low priority importance task using the VNIR sensor over the region of Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of San Martín. This mission aims to conduct a low priority importance survey for opportunistic capture, which involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level through multispectral vegetation analysis. The objective is to gather data that will help in understanding surface reflectance and vegetation health without altering the deadline or shifting the time frame to an adjacent part of the day.

In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we are to capture a thermal mapping of Samacá, Colombia. This critical payload capture involves an overnight TIR scan to assess heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the region. As this is an absolute institutional priority, it involves a mandatory directive for a detailed thermal anomaly detection and mapping of heat emissions. The data collected will be essential for our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

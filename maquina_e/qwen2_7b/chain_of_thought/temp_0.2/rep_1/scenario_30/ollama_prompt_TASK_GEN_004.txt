In three days, at the time of overnight, we request a SAR active radar scan over the city of Uribe, Colombia. This mission aims to capture cloud-penetrating imagery for critical infrastructure monitoring and environmental assessment purposes. Given its low priority level, this task can be scheduled flexibly and is considered a non-essential background survey. We anticipate delivering thermal mapping data that will help in identifying heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the region, which is crucial for understanding local climatic conditions and potential anomalies.

In three days, at the time of the evening, we aim to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis of the region named "Corteno Golgi" in Italia. This task is part of our standard operational tasking and represents normal operational status. We seek to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for this area as it will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters. The imagery will serve as a valuable resource for monitoring environmental conditions and supporting decision-making processes.

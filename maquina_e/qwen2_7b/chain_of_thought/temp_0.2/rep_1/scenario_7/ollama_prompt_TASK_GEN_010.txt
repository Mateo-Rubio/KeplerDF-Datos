In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a standard operational tasking using the VNIR sensor over the city of Pantanillo in Colombia. This involves conducting a routine monitoring activity as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The parameters for this task are fixed and represent our normal operational status. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels through multispectral vegetation analysis, which will provide insights into surface reflectance and the health of the vegetation in the area.

In three days, at the time of the evening, we request a high-resolution optical snapshot of Murindó, Colombia. This daylight photography will serve as a clear visual documentation of the region's landscape and urban features. As this task carries a low priority importance, it is designated as a flexible schedule "opportunistic capture" with no constraints on the deadline. We aim to capture the heat signature and surface temperature profile of the area during this time, which will be crucial for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions. This non-essential background survey will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking setup without impacting the critical payload capture or mandatory directive requirements.

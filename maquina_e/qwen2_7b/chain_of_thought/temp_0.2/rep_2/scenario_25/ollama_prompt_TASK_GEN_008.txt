In four days, at the beginning of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring purpose. This involves a VNIR sensor to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the vegetation NDVI index in Prati di Tivo, Italia. The goal is to provide high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and clear optical imagery, serving as a true-color visual documentation of the area's environmental conditions.

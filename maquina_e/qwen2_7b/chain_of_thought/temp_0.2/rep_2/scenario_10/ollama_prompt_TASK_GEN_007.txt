On the day after tomorrow, at the precise time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our satellite constellation is tasked with capturing a thermal mapping of the region named Apartadó, located in the country of Colombia. The operational parameters are strict; we aim to capture this critical data without altering the deadline, ensuring that our satellite remains focused on the task at hand until the specified time when the sun has set and the conditions are ideal for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions.

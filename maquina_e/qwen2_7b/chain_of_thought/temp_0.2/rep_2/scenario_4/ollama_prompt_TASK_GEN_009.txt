In three days, at the start of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a standard operational tasking involving a routine monitoring of Indonesia's Rokan Hilir region using a VNIR sensor. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for this area, providing us with valuable insights into the health and productivity of the local ecosystems.

In four days, during the morning hours, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Singkep, Indonesia. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the vegetation NDVI index using near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. The objective is to gather detailed multispectral vegetation analysis data that will aid in understanding the environmental conditions and health of the region's ecosystems without altering the deadline or shifting the time frame.

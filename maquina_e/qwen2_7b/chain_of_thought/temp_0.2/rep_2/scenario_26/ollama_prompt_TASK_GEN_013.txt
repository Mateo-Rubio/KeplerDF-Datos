In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we request that our satellite constellation capture a low-priority importance VNIR (visual near-infrared) image of Betulia, Colombia. This high-resolution optical snapshot aims to provide a clear visual documentation of the landscape under the cover of darkness, offering insights into the unique features and conditions that are typically obscured during daylight hours. The overnight timeframe ensures optimal conditions for this task without interfering with our regular operational schedules or contractual obligations.

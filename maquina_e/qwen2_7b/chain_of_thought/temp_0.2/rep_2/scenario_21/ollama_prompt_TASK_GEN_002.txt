In four days, at the time of overnight, we aim to capture thermal mapping data over Santa Croce sull'Arno, Italy. Our objective is to assess heat signatures and surface temperature profiles for this region during the night hours when conditions are most conducive for our thermal imaging sensor. This task is designated as a low priority importance request with flexible scheduling considerations, allowing us to drop or reschedule at convenience if necessary. The data collected will serve as a background survey for further analysis and will be used in non-essential background filler tasks.

In four days, at the time of overnight, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey using the VNIR sensor. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the vegetation NDVI index for the region in Malaysia, specifically focusing on the area known as Rumah Budak. The goal is to gather data that will help us understand surface reflectance and provide insights into the health of the vegetation without altering the deadline or shifting it to an adjacent part of the day.

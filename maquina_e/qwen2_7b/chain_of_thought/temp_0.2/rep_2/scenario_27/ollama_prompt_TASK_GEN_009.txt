In three days, around mid-day, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey using our VNIR sensor over Indonesia's equatorial region. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index for vegetation health assessment. The goal is to provide clear optical imagery of the area, offering detailed insights into the landscape without the constraints of cloud cover or darkness. This non-essential background filler task will serve as a testbed tracking task, allowing us to gather data on the equatorial region's ecological conditions without impacting our baseline pipeline tracking or critical payload capture operations.

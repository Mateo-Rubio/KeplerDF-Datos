In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we request a low priority importance task focusing on the Ituango region in Colombia. This involves conducting a routine monitoring exercise using the VNIR sensor for multispectral vegetation analysis. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index to understand surface reflectance patterns. This standard operational tasking aims to provide valuable insights into the vegetation health and environmental conditions without imposing any urgent constraints on our mission schedule.

In three days, during the afternoon, we aim to capture high-resolution optical imagery of the region named "Italia Norte". This standard operational tasking involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level across the area using our VNIR sensor. We will be conducting this routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The imagery will serve as a critical payload capture for understanding the surface temperature profile and mapping heat emissions, which is essential for our ongoing project requirements.

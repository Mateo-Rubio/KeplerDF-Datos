In preparation for a critical commercial tasking requirement, we aim to capture high-resolution optical imagery of Suárez, Colombia, around mid-day in three days. This standard operational tasking involves a daylight photography mission using our VIS sensor to provide clear visual documentation of the region's landscape and urban features under optimal lighting conditions. The deadline is strict, with no flexibility for earlier or later capture times within the day, as it is essential that we deliver this data product precisely as planned.

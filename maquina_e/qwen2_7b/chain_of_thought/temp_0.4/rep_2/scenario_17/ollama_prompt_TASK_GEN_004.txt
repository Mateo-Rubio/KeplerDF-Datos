In three days, specifically during the evening hours, we request the capture of a low priority importance task using the VNIR sensor over the region in Colombia, focusing on the city of Caramanta. This observational mission aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and surface reflectance through a multispectral vegetation analysis, providing valuable insights into the health and distribution of vegetation in the area. The deadline for delivering this data product is firm and must be adhered to strictly as "in the evening", without any adjustments or extensions to adjacent parts of the day.

In four days, at the time of overnight when the stars are out and the world is quiet, our satellite constellation will embark on a mission to capture the thermal signature of Colombia's Simití region. We aim for a "near-infrared reflection" to map water bodies and assess soil moisture conditions, providing us with invaluable insights into environmental health without the interference of daylight or cloud cover. This standard operational tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status with fixed parameters that ensure the integrity of our baseline data collection process.

In four days, at the precise moment of "in the morning", our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot using a VNIR sensor over Sabana de Torres in Colombia. This mission is an absolute institutional priority, representing a binding contract requirement involving a mandatory directive for a cloud-penetrating radar scan to be performed in the morning. The data products are expected to provide essential information on environmental conditions and contribute significantly to various applications such as urban planning, environmental monitoring, and disaster response.

In four days, at the dead of night when the world sleeps, we are tasked with capturing a comprehensive near-infrared reflection snapshot of San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia. This operational request is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and represents a standard commercial tasking requirement. The data will be collected overnight to ensure minimal interference with local activities and optimal conditions for detailed surface mapping. We aim to deliver thermal mapping data that highlights surface temperature profiles and potential heat emissions, aiding in various applications such as agricultural monitoring and urban heat island assessment.

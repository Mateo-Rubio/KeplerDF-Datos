In three days, at the time designated as "overnight", we request the execution of a low priority importance task involving the VNIR sensor. This mission aims to capture a nighttime snapshot of Colombia's San Martín region through multispectral vegetation analysis, seeking to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. The data collection is scheduled for an overnight period to optimize conditions for this type of observation, without altering or extending the deadline.

Request for an Earth-observation task to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the daytime landscape in Rumah Budak, Malaysia. Given its low priority importance and flexible schedule, this background survey is designated as "non-essential background filler task". The mission involves assessing the vegetation health through multispectral analysis focusing on chlorophyll absorption levels or calculating the vegetation NDVI index, which will provide critical information about surface reflectance.

In four days overnight, we aim to gather data that will aid in monitoring environmental conditions and possibly help identify soil moisture assessment. This task falls within the baseline pipeline tracking framework with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, but due to its low priority level, it can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience without impacting the operational flow.

The VNIR sensor deployed for this mission will perform its observational duties using the microwave surface imaging capability characteristic of active radar scans. This allows us to capture cloud-penetrating capture imagery essential for understanding ground geometry and other environmental conditions that are typically obscured during daylight hours, thereby supplementing our operational knowledge without compromising the integrity of regular monitoring activities.

In four days, beginning overnight, we aim to gather invaluable data for our baseline pipeline tracking efforts. This mission involves an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging techniques to ensure cloud-penetrating capture, allowing us to accurately map ground geometry beneath dense atmospheric conditions. The VIS sensor is selected for its capabilities in daylight photography to provide comprehensive visual documentation of the city's topography and infrastructure.

The captured imagery will support our continuous monitoring activities by contributing to an understanding of the city's environmental and urban development patterns without imposing stringent deadlines or interrupting critical workflows. This background survey is considered a non-essential task that can be conveniently scheduled for opportune times, ensuring it does not interfere with the urgent operational execution of high-priority missions involving the capture of critical payloads.

As we look towards completing this task within our predefined upper limit of data availability—four days overnight—the Earth-observation mission team will harness our satellite constellation to achieve the best possible data collection under the conditions specified by our operational guidelines.

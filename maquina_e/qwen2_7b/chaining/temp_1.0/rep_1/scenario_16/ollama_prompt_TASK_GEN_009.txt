In response to an operational directive of medium importance, we aim to conduct a standard baseline pipeline tracking task using a near-infrared (NIR) sensor on "the day after tomorrow" during the afternoon. Our focus will be Malaysia, particularly the city of Bukit Mabong, seeking a high-resolution optical snapshot to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries accurately with minimal cloud interference. This critical payload capture involves a fixed parameter setup ensuring normal operational status without causing any disruption or preemption of existing priorities. The data obtained will support essential background filler tasks that can be adjusted for flexibility if required, acting as opportunistic survey data contributing to ongoing environmental monitoring efforts.

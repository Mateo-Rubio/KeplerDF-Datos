Since the deadline for this task has been set as in three days during the afternoon, we aim to execute this task as a flexible schedule opportunistic capture, treating it as a non-essential background filler task. There's no need to rush or prioritize it over other missions; instead, our satellite constellation will gather this data at our convenience, which aligns well with our baseline pipeline tracking setup featuring fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This "background survey" will contribute to our comprehensive monitoring efforts without interrupting more critical payload captures that require an urgent operational execution and un-preemptable mission lock.

In preparation for an upcoming environmental assessment, we are tasked with capturing a thermal map of a critical site located in Tuluá, Colombia. This low priority importance request involves the use of our thermal imaging sensor (TIR) to gather data on surface temperatures and thermal anomalies, essential for understanding local environmental conditions and potential hazards. We aim to capture this high-resolution optical snapshot overnight in three days, during the calm of the early morning hours. This "background survey" task aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup, offering a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture without compromising quality. The data collected will contribute to a comprehensive understanding of thermal emissions and heat signatures at the site, helping stakeholders make informed decisions based on real-time environmental data.

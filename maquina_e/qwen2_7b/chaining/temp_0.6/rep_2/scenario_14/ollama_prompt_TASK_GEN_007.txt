In four days overnight, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of Colombia's Bolívar region. This mission involves daylight photography to provide clear, true-color visual documentation that will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking setup. Being a low priority importance request, it carries the nature of a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture and can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience as a non-essential background filler task. The primary sensor employed for this task is VIS, aiming to deliver a comprehensive view that elucidates ground geometry and surface temperature profiles without being obstructed by heavy weather conditions.

In an absolute institutional priority context, the Earth-observation mission operator urgently requests a critical payload capture from an automated satellite constellation planner. This task involves the mandatory directive to obtain near-infrared reflection data for a water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment on day three following today's date around mid-day. The geographical context is set in the picturesque region of France, specifically the scenic valley of Valle-di-Rostino, where the operator aims to capture the essential surface features accurately under normal operational status conditions. This highly realistic dataset will serve as a non-essential background filler task for opportunistic capture, with flexible scheduling allowing for "drop or reschedule at convenience" if required by the operator's workflow update needs.

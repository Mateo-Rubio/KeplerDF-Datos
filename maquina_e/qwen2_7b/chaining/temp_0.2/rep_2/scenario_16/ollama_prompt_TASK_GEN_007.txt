In four days in the morning, we are to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the city of Páez, Colombia, for daylight photography and true-color visual documentation. This low priority importance task involves a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, treating it as a non-essential background survey or testbed tracking task.

On the day after tomorrow around mid-day, our Earth-observation mission requires a low priority importance task to capture the near-infrared reflection of a high-resolution optical snapshot over AMCO, Area Metropolitana Centro Occidente, Colombia. This "testbed tracking task" aims to assess soil moisture and delineate water body boundaries using the unique capabilities of near-infrared sensors for this critical payload capture. The natural landscape of Colombia will be documented in an opportunistic manner, allowing for flexible scheduling as a background survey or a preliminary vegetation analysis task that contributes to our understanding of chlorophyll absorption levels and NDVI index calculations. The mission's focus on AMCO ensures comprehensive coverage of the region's environmental conditions under the expected mid-day sunlight, providing valuable insights into soil moisture assessment without the constraints of an urgent operational execution.

In three days overnight, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance background survey of the city of Foraquira, Colombia. This involves a flexible schedule for opportunistically capturing a nighttime multispectral vegetation analysis using our VNIR sensor, to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. This "background filler task" is not essential but serves as a testbed tracking task, allowing us to gather valuable data without impacting more critical missions. The imagery captured will help in understanding the local vegetation dynamics and environmental conditions with minimal interference on our operational workflow.

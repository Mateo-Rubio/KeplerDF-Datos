For our Earth-observation mission, we're looking at a non-urgent background survey focused on the region of Murung Raya, Indonesia. Given the relaxed nature of this task's priority, we're open to dropping or rescheduling it at our convenience, making it a perfect candidate for filling in gaps in our observational schedule while still contributing valuable data to our ongoing analyses.

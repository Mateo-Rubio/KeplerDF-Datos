We have a standard operational tasking for a near-infrared reflection survey targeting soil moisture assessment over Sarolangun, Indonesia. This mission is part of our routine monitoring efforts and represents a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters under normal operational status. The goal is to capture detailed information regarding water body boundaries and soil gradients using near-infrared imaging. We request this data to be delivered within the timeframe of three days, specifically around mid-day, adhering strictly to the specified deadline for delivery.

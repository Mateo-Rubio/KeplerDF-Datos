We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial observation in the region of Italy, focusing on the city of Norte. The primary objective is to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis using a VNIR sensor, aiming to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. This mission forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We require this data to be captured in three days, during the afternoon. This task is part of our routine monitoring efforts and should adhere to these specified conditions without deviation.

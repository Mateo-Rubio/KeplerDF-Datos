We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Murung Raya, Indonesia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The objective is to utilize the near-infrared reflection capabilities of our sensors to perform water body boundary mapping and assess soil moisture levels in the area. This is a commercial tasking that fits within our standard workflow update procedures. Please ensure that this mission is scheduled for execution in four days overnight, as this is the upper limit for data availability.

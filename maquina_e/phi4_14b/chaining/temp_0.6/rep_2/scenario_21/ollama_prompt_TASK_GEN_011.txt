We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and requires fixed parameters to evaluate the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. As this is a commercial tasking, it falls under normal operational status with a standard workflow update. The data must be delivered by tomorrow in the morning.

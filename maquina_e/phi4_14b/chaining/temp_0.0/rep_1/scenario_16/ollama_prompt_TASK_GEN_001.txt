We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Santander de Quilichao in Colombia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. The mission involves capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. Please ensure that this task is scheduled to be completed in three days in the evening.

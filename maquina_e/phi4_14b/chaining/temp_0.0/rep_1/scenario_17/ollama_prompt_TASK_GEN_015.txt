We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Kepulauan Meranti in Indonesia. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The objective is to conduct a near-infrared reflection survey, focusing on water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. We aim to discriminate gradients effectively using this multispectral approach. Please ensure that the data capture aligns with our workflow update requirements and is completed in four days in the morning.

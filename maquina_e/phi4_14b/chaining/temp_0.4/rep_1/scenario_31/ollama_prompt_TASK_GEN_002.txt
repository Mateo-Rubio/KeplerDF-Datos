We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial satellite constellation to perform an active radar scan over the region of Norte in France. This mission involves using microwave surface imaging to achieve cloud-penetrating capture, which is crucial for piercing through heavy weather and capturing detailed ground geometry. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking, this task must adhere to fixed parameters representing normal operational status and ensure a standard workflow update. The data delivery deadline for this operation has been set as "the day after tomorrow overnight," ensuring timely availability for further analysis.

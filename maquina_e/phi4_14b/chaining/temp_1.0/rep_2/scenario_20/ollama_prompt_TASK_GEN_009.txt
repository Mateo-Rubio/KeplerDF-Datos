We have a standard operational tasking that requires routine monitoring of Pelompek, Indonesia. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and involves a commercial tasking with fixed parameters to ensure normal operational status and a routine workflow update. Our objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using the VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index along with surface reflectance for Pelompek. The data must be available by tomorrow overnight, aligning with our established data delivery requirements.

I am submitting a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation to perform a multispectral vegetation analysis over the region of Norte using the VNIR sensor. This routine monitoring is aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating an NDVI index in the area, forming part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The imagery should be captured in the morning in three days in the morning to ensure accurate data capture within the set operational timeframe.

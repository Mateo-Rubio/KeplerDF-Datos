This mission is an absolute institutional priority for capturing critical radar imagery over the region of Bukit Mabong in Malaysia. We require an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to perform a cloud-penetrating capture that can effectively map the ground geometry despite any adverse weather conditions. This is a binding contract requirement and involves a mandatory directive for urgent operational execution with an un-preemptable mission lock. The data must be delivered today overnight, as this is the upper limit for data availability.

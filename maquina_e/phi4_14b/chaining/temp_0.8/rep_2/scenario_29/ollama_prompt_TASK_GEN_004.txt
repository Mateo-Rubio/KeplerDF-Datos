We have a low-priority importance task for an opportunistic observation of the Nunchía region in Colombia. This flexible schedule allows us to conduct a background survey using VNIR sensors. The goal is to perform multispectral vegetation analysis, specifically to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index, as well as analyze surface reflectance. Please note that this task can be adjusted or rescheduled at convenience. We require the data capture to be completed by the day after tomorrow around mid-day to ensure it fits into our broader mission framework.

We have a flexible schedule for an opportunistic capture of active radar scans using microwave surface imaging in Burzanella, Italy. This low priority importance task is designed to provide a cloud-penetrating capture, which will be particularly useful under heavy weather conditions or to map ground geometry. Given the non-essential nature of this background survey task, we explicitly request that it may be dropped or rescheduled at your convenience. The data should be available in three days during the afternoon.

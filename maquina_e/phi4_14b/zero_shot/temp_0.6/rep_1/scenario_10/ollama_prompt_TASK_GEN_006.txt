We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner regarding a multispectral vegetation analysis mission over Simabur, Indonesia. This mission involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating an NDVI index for the region's surface reflectance. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, we require this to be handled as a routine monitoring activity. The data products are needed by the morning in three days at the latest, ensuring they are ingested into the pipeline before this strict delivery deadline.

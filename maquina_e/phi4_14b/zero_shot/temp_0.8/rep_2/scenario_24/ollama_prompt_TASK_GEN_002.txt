We have a task for the satellite constellation to perform a near-infrared reflection over Saas-Almagell, Switzerland. The mission's goal is to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels in this picturesque Alpine region.

This task falls under our standard operational tasking with fixed parameters, forming part of our baseline pipeline tracking. It’s essential for maintaining normal workflow updates and routine monitoring operations.

The data products are needed by the evening the day after tomorrow at the latest, ensuring they are ingested into the pipeline before that time.

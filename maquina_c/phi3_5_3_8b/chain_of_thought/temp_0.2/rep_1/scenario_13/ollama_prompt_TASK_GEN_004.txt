In accordance with our Earth-observation mission objectives, we request a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis using high-resolution optical snapshot capabilities during the nighttime period in three days from today's date. This operation is intended to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate surface reflectance indices within the city of San Martín, Colombia. The data collected will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking for environmental monitoring purposes. Please note that this tasking has been assigned a low priority importance; therefore, it can be flexibly scheduled or rescheduled at your convenience without impact on other mission priorities.

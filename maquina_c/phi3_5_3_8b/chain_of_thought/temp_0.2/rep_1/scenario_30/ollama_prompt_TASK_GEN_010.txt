In accordance with our Earth-observation mission objectives, we are requesting a thermal mapping operation over Colombia's city of Tuluá using ground-based infrared sensors. This task is designated for an opportunity to capture heat signatures during the upcoming nighttime period in three days from today. Given its low priority importance, this data collection can be scheduled flexibly and may need to be rescheduled at convenience if necessary. The focus will be on detecting thermal anomalies and mapping surface temperature profiles within Tuluá's region, contributing valuable insights into the local environmental conditions without disrupting our higher-priority missions.

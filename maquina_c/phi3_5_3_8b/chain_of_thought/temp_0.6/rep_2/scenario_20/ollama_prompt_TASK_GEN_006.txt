As a priority 2 task, we are scheduling a standard operational tracking for Sekadau in Indonesia with the aim of conducting thermal mapping to capture heat signatures and surface temperature profiles during the afternoon of the day after tomorrow. This is part of our routine monitoring designed to detect thermal anomalies and map heat emissions from the Earth's surface using Thermal Infrared (TIR) sensors on board our satellite constellation. The data acquired will contribute significantly to understanding local environmental conditions, aiding in both scientific research and practical applications that depend on accurate temperature readings of the landscape.

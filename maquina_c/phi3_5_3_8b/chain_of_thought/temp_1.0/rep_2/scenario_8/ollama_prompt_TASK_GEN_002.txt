As a low priority importance task within our Earth-observation mission, we seek to conduct an SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) active radar scan using microwave surface imaging. The objective is to perform cloud-penetrating capture and accurately map the ground geometry in Gordona, Italy. This operation aims for flexibility with its schedule, allowing it to be dropped or rescheduled at our convenience due to its lower importance relative to other missions. Please note that data delivery deadlines are set precisely for "the day after tomorrow" in the evening hours.

As a non-essential background filler task with flexible scheduling, we aim for an opportune moment to conduct a passive radar survey over Norte in Slovenija using active microwave surface imaging technology. This effort is intended to penetrate cloud cover and effectively capture ground geometry during the afternoon of the day following tomorrow. However, due to its lower operational priority, this mission can be accommodated or rescheduled according to convenience without impact on higher-priority missions currently locked down as absolute institutional requirements.

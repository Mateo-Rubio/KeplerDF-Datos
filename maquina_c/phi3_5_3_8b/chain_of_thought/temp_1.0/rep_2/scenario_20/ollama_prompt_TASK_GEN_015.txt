In accordance with standard operational tasking protocols for routine monitoring within our satellite constellation framework, we have scheduled a SAR-based active radar scan mission over Belaga, Malaysia. This endeavor is slated to be executed during the night following tomorrow and involves penetrating heavy weather conditions to capture ground geometry in detail. The goal of this mission is not only to advance our understanding of cloud formations and their interactions with topographical elements but also to enhance our radar surface imaging capabilities by focusing on microwave signal analysis for high-resolution mapping within the specified temporal window.

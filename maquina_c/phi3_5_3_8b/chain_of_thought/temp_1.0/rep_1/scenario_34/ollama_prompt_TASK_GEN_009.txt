As a low priority importance mission for opportunistic capture, we require the satellites within our automated constellation to perform an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging during "morning" on the forthcoming fourth day. This task is set as part of our routine monitoring operations and can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The objective herein is not only to achieve cloud-penetrating capture capabilities but also to provide detailed ground geometry insights, which could prove beneficial for future planning despite its non-essential status at present.

As a non-essential background filler task with a flexible schedule, we seek to carry out an overnight multispectral vegetation analysis in Arnoldstein, Austria, using near-infrared reflection capabilities to assess water body boundaries and soil moisture gradients. This opportunity allows for the collection of data that will contribute to our understanding of environmental conditions during nighttime hours. The request comes with the expectation that this operation may be dropped or rescheduled at convenience due to its lower priority level, but given the unique diurnal context of an overnight window, we believe it could still yield valuable insights for our ongoing Earth-observation initiatives.

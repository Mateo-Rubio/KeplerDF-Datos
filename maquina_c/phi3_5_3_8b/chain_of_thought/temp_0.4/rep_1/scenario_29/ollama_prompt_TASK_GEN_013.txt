In line with our institutional priority and mandatory directive for a critical payload capture, we require the automated satellite constellation planner to execute a high-resolution optical snapshot focused on true-color visual documentation of Aipe in Colombia during an overnight period starting "the day after tomorrow". This task is essential for multispectral vegetation analysis aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to monitor surface reflectance, which will significantly contribute to our environmental monitoring efforts. The delivery of these data products must adhere strictly to the specified deadline without any alterations or extensions.

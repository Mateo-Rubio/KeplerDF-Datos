In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocol, we are initiating a routine monitoring mission for multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This operation is scheduled to occur in four days during the overnight period. The objective is to assess soil moisture gradients and discriminate between different water bodies within Murung Raya, Indonesia. Given its medium importance status, this tasking will be integrated into our baseline pipeline tracking system with fixed parameters representing a standard workflow update. However, due to the flexible schedule associated with non-essential background filler tasks, operators are advised to drop or reschedule at their convenience if resources permit.

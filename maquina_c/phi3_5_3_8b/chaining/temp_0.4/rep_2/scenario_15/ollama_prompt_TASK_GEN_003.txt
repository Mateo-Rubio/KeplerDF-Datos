As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasking a satellite constellation planner with generating high-resolution radar scans using microwave surface imaging to penetrate heavy weather and accurately map the ground geometry. This standard operational tasking is scheduled for "the day after tomorrow around mid-day" as part of our routine monitoring activities. The focus will be on a city located within an equatorial region, where cloud cover can often obscure optical imagery. The data collected during this mission will enhance our understanding and tracking of the area's surface features under various weather conditions, contributing to our baseline pipeline tracking for environmental studies and natural resource management.

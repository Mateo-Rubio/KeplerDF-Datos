In three days around mid-day, we are tasked with executing a standard operational TIR sensor sweep over Ketapang in Indonesia. This thermal mapping is essential for capturing heat signatures and constructing an accurate surface temperature profile within the region. The data collected will be pivotal for identifying any potential thermal anomalies that may indicate critical environmental or infrastructural issues requiring immediate attention. As part of our routine monitoring, this mission aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup, ensuring we maintain a consistent flow of high-quality temperature readings to inform ongoing climate studies and urban planning efforts in the area.

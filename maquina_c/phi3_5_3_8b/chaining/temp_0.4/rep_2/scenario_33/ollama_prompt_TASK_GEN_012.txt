As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am issuing a task for our automated satellite constellation planner to conduct a near-infrared reflection survey over Kuantan Singingi in Indonesia. This operation is aimed at water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment to discriminate gradients within the target area. Given its low priority importance, this task has been scheduled with flexibility in mind; however, it must be completed by three days in the evening as per our upstream module's deadline for data availability. This background survey will serve as an opportunistic capture to supplement our existing datasets and enhance environmental monitoring efforts.

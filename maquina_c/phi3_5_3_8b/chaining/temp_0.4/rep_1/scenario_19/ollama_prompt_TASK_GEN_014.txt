In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols for routine monitoring, we are issuing a directive to deploy a multispectral vegetation analysis mission over Norte in Italy. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance measurements. This task forms an integral part of our baseline pipeline tracking, aiming at maintaining a consistent monitoring schedule under normal operational parameters. The data collection window for this assignment is set to occur in three days during the afternoon and must be executed as such without delay or rescheduling.

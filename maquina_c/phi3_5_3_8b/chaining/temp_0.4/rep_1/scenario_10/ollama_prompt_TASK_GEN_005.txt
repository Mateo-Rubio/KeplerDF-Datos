As part of our routine monitoring for Norte's agricultural regions, we are tasked with conducting a multispectral vegetation analysis. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) to understand surface reflectance better. The focus is on capturing near-infrared reflection for effective water body boundary mapping and soil moisture discrimination, crucial for identifying gradients in Norte's diverse landscapes. This standard operational tasking must be executed the day after tomorrow in the morning to ensure timely data integration into our environmental models.

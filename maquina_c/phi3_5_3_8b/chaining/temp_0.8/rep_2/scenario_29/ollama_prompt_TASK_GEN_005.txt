Request for SAR (active radar scan) imagery of Norte city to be completed with high-resolution microwave surface imaging capabilities. This task aims at capturing ground geometry, especially in adverse weather conditions when optical sensors would fail. As this is designated as a low priority importance activity, the schedule remains flexible; however, it must be executed before "the day after tomorrow in the evening" to ensure timely data collection for ongoing research purposes. The findings here will not only contribute to our understanding of terrain and land use but also serve as supplementary information that can enhance more critical mission objectives within a broader Earth observation framework.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am submitting a task request to our automated satellite constellation planner for scheduling a VNIR sensor payload over Indonesia, specifically targeting Simabur city. This is part of our routine monitoring aimed at performing multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index as well as surface reflectance measurements. The task falls under standard operational status within our baseline pipeline tracking, with a fixed delivery deadline set for three days in the morning. We expect this data contribution to enhance our ongoing environmental studies and resource management efforts significantly.

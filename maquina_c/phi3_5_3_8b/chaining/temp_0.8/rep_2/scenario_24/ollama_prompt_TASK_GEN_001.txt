As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am submitting a task request for standard operational tasking to capture multispectral vegetation analysis in Sabana de Torres, Colombia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), along with surface reflectance measurements using high-resolution optical snapshots taken during nighttime hours over the next three days. This routine monitoring will aid in understanding vegetative health and discrimination of environmental gradients within the specified location, enssuring it seamlessly integrates into our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

Initiating a standard operational task for multispectral vegetation analysis in Solok, Indonesia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) alongside surface reflectance measurements. This will contribute to our understanding of local flora health and coverage using near-infrared reflection techniques. With a priority set at Medium Importance, this task forms an essential part of our baseline pipeline tracking for routine monitoring of vegetation dynamics in the region. All data must be delivered within four days, specifically during the morning hours to ensure optimal lighting conditions for high-quality spectral imagery capture and analysis.

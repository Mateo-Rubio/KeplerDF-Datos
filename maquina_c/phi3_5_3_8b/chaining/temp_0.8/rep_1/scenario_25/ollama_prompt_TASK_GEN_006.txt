As a part of our routine monitoring efforts in Singkep Barat within Indonesia's agricultural heartland, we have scheduled an opportunistic capture task using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This is aimed at conducting multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate surface reflectance for improved crop health monitoring. The objective here is not mission critical but serves as valuable background survey data, with a flexible schedule in mind. We intend to complete this task within four days in the morning to enhance our ongoing agricultural studies without placing undue strain on operational resources.

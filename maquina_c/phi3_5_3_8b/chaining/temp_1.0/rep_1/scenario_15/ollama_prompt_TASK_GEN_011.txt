This is a standard operational tasking for tomorrow in the evening to conduct an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging over Corteno Golgi in Italy. The aim of this routine mission is to capture ground geometry, especially under adverse weather conditions where cloud cover may obstruct optical sensors. It's a critical part of our baseline pipeline tracking system that enssures the continuous monitoring of geographical changes and supports our comprehensive analysis for infrastructure development planning purposes. The acquired SAR imagery is expected to be instrumental in understanding terrain-related phenomena without interference from sunlight or clouds, complementing data gathered by other sensors throughout standard operations.

For our upcoming Earth-observation mission involving Norte in Slovenija, we've identified an opportunity to undertake a SAR scan with the aim of conducting active radar imaging that can penetrate cloud cover and provide insights into surface topography. Given this is currently flagged as low priority importance, it serves as a supplementary task for our satellite fleet. We seek to secure high-resolution microwave scans during the afternoon session the day after tomorrow. As such tasks are not mission-critical, they can be accommodated within available bandwidth but will require flexibility in scheduling with an understanding that resources may shift according to other pressing obligations. This initiative does not lock down satellite assets and may be temporarily set aside or rescheduled as circumstances permit.

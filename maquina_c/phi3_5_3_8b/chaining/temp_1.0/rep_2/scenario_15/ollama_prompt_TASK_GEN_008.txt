In accordance with standard operational tasks required for routine monitoring within our satellite observational framework, we are seeking to allocate resources towards a multispectral vegetation analysis. This is aimed at assessing the chlorophyll absorption level across the Piacenza region in Italy and calculating an NDVI index to understand surface reflectance with near-infrared reflection capabilities. Given its standardized role within our baseline pipeline tracking, this task necessitates alignment with a fixed schedule for regular updates on vegetation health indicators. We aim to complete these assessments "in three days in the morning," thereby ensuring data is gathered at consistent intervals conducive to seasonal variations and operational cycles.

As part of our routine monitoring for environmental changes in Norte city within Italia, we require a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels. This task is set as a non-essential background filler with flexible timing and can be dropped or rescheduled at the operator's convenience. The aim is to calculate a Vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) index and surface reflectance, capturing true-color visual documentation during daylight photography sessions in the afternoon of the day after tomorrow.

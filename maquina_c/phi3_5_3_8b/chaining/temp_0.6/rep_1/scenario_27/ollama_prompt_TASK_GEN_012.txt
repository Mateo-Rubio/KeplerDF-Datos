Request for automated satellite task scheduling with a TIR sensor aimed at thermal mapping within Kapuas Hulu, Indonesia. This operation will focus on capturing surface temperature profiles and detecting any potential thermal anomalies in the area. Considering its low operational priority, this request is set to be carried out as an opportunistic task that can be adjusted according to convenience. The data collection window has been earmarked for "the day after tomorrow during the afternoon", following which the information will be made available for analysis and reporting.

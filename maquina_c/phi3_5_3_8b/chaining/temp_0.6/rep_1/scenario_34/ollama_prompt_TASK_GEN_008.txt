This is an urgent operational execution directive for a mandatory directive involving high-resolution thermal mapping of Norte's terrain. A critical payload capture is required to assess surface temperature profiles and detect any thermal anomalies within the region, using passive microwave technology. The task has been earmarked as an absolute institutional priority due to its significance for environmental monitoring. Data must be delivered in three days overnight, ensuring that the operational deadline is met without delay. This mission lock underscores the importance of timely thermal mapping and anomaly detection capabilities provided by our satellite constellation, particularly under conditions where ground-based assessments are challenging or impossible due to weather constraints.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasking our automated satellite constellation planner with a standard operational tasking to capture multispectral vegetation analysis in Prati di Tivo, Italy. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and requires the collection of high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography within four days in the morning deadline. The focus will be on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels to calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance, enssuring comprehensive monitoring of local flora health and vigor.

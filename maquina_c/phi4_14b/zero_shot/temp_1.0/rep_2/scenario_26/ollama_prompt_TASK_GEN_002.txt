We have a standard operational tasking for the Corteno Golgi area in Italy. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and involves capturing multispectral vegetation analysis data using the VNIR sensor. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. It's a routine monitoring task with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, so no immediate urgency beyond regular workflow updates is needed.

The data products need to be available for ingestion by the end of the evening in three days. Please ensure all standard procedures are followed to meet this delivery deadline.

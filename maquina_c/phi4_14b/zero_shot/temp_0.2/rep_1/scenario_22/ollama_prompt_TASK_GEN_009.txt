We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Banyuasin, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and involves capturing chlorophyll absorption levels to calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance. The request is set with fixed parameters representing normal operational status as part of a workflow update. We need this data delivered by mid-day in three days at the latest to ensure it's ingested into the pipeline on time.

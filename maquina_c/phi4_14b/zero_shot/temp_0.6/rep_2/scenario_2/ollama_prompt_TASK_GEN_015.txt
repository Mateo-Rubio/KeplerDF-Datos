We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation to conduct a near-infrared reflection analysis over Bangka Selatan, Indonesia. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters and serves as a routine monitoring effort to assess soil moisture levels in the region. The goal is to capture the near-infrared reflection data needed for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment, ensuring accurate discrimination of gradients. Please ensure that the data products are ingested into the pipeline before the morning in three days at the latest, adhering to our strict delivery deadline.

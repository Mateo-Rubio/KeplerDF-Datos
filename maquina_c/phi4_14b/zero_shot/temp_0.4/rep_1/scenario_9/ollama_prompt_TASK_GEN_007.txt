We have a routine monitoring task for our baseline pipeline tracking that involves a multispectral vegetation analysis over San Gil, Colombia. This task focuses on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to monitor vegetation health. As part of our standard operational tasking, we require this data with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The delivery deadline is set for the day after tomorrow during the afternoon at the latest, ensuring the products are ingested into the pipeline before then.

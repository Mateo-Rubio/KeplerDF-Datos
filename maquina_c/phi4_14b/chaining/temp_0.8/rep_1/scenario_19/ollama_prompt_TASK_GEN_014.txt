We are requesting a standard operational tasking for a VNIR sensor mission over the region of Norte in Italia. This mission involves a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance for the area. As a part of our baseline pipeline tracking, this is a routine monitoring effort with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data collection should be completed in three days during the afternoon to ensure timely integration into our workflow update.

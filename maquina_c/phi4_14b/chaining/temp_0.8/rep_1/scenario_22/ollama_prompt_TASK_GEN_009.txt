We are submitting a standard operational tasking for a mission over Banyuasin, Indonesia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, reflecting a routine monitoring objective. The request is to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis using the VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. It's important that this data is captured in three days around mid-day to ensure the accuracy and relevance of our observations.

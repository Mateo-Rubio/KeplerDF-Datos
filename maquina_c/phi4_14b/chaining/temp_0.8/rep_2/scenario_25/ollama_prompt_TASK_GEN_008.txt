We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Prati di Tivo in Italy. This routine monitoring activity is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index, which involves capturing high-resolution optical snapshots of the vegetation's surface reflectance. Please ensure this task is executed efficiently and scheduled for delivery in four days in the morning.

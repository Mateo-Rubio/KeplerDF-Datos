This is an absolute institutional priority mission request for a high-resolution active radar scan to capture the near-infrared reflection over Campagnatico, Italy. Our objective is to conduct a detailed soil moisture assessment and water body boundary mapping using microwave surface imaging techniques capable of penetrating cloud cover and heavy weather conditions. This operation is critical for understanding ground geometry and is framed as a mandatory directive with an urgent operational execution required. The task is un-preemptable, and the satellite constellation must deliver the data by the day after tomorrow overnight to meet our binding contract requirements. This captures vital environmental data that supports both strategic planning and immediate operational needs.

We are submitting a standard operational tasking to monitor surface temperature variations over the picturesque region of Norte in France. This commercial tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, with fixed parameters for routine monitoring activities. The mission involves capturing thermal imagery using a high-resolution sensor dedicated to thermal mapping. The goal is to obtain detailed heat signature data that will be instrumental for identifying any thermal anomalies and accurately profiling surface temperatures. We require this data to be delivered in three days during the afternoon to maintain our regular workflow update schedule.

We are submitting a standard operational tasking to capture thermal mapping over the Ecuatorial region for our baseline pipeline tracking setup. This involves using thermal imaging to obtain a surface temperature profile during the day after tomorrow, during the afternoon. As this is part of our routine monitoring, it aligns with our fixed parameters representing normal operational status and a regular workflow update. We aim to use the task results for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions, which are critical components of our ongoing environmental assessments.

This is an absolute institutional priority and a binding contract requirement for a critical payload capture of an NIR (near-infrared reflection) sensor task. The objective is to conduct near-infrared reflection imaging, which will assist in water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment over the city of Puerto Rico, Colombia. This mission allocation carries high importance due to the pressing need for accurate data on environmental parameters such as soil moisture gradients in this region. As a mandatory directive with an urgent operational execution mandate, this task has an un-preemptable mission lock to ensure data is available by the day after tomorrow in the morning, adhering strictly to our critical timelines.

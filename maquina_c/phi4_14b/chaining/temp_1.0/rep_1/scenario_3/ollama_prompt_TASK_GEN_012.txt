This task request is an absolute institutional priority concerning the critical payload capture of near-infrared reflection data over the region of Malinau, Indonesia. We are committed to a mandatory directive for the acquisition of this high-priority observation, with the mission allocation authority firmly in place to ensure an urgent operational execution. The objective is to utilize near-infrared reflection capabilities for the precise mapping of water body boundaries and soil moisture assessment within the area. The data collected will provide invaluable insights into the terrain's characteristics, allowing for effective monitoring and management. Given the binding nature of this mission lock, it is essential that these observations are completed with no preemption, and the deliverable must be made available by in three days in the evening.

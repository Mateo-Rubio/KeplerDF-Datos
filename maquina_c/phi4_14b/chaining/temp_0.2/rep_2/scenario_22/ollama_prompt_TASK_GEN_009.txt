We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis in Banyuasin, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, utilizing fixed parameters to maintain normal operational status and ensure a workflow update. The mission involves capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot using the VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. The data collection must be completed in three days around mid-day to meet our delivery deadline.

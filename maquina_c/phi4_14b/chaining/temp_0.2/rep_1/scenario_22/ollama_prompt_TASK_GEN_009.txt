We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Banyuasin, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. We need this data delivered in three days around mid-day to ensure it aligns with our workflow update schedule.

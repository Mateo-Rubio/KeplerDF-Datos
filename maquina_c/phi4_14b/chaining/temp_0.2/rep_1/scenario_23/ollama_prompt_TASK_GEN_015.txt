We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Kepulauan Meranti in Indonesia. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters to ensure normal operational status and a workflow update. The mission involves capturing multispectral vegetation analysis using the VNIR sensor, focusing on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The data collection must be completed in four days overnight to meet our delivery requirements.

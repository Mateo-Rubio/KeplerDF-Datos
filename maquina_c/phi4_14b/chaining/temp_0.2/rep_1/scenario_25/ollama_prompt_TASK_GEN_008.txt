We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Prati di Tivo in Italy. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves capturing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to assess the health and reflectance of the vegetation in this region. Please ensure that this task is scheduled for execution in four days in the morning, aligning with our workflow update requirements.

We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner involving Kepulauan Meranti in Indonesia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Our mission involves capturing multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance using VNIR sensors. The data is crucial for routine monitoring of environmental health, and it should be completed in four days overnight to ensure timely integration into our workflow update.

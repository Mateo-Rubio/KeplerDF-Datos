We have a standard operational tasking for the Colombia region, specifically over La Cumbre. This task involves a commercial mission with fixed parameters to capture an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging. The objective is to obtain cloud-penetrating captures that can pierce through any heavy weather conditions and accurately map ground geometry. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and represents a normal operational status within our workflow update. Please ensure that the data is available in four days around mid-day.

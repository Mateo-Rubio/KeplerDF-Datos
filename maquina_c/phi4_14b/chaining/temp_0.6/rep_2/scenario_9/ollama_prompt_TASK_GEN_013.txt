This mission is an absolute institutional priority that involves a critical payload capture with mandatory directive status. We require a high-resolution optical snapshot using multispectral vegetation analysis capabilities to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the region of Cumaral in Colombia. This task must be executed urgently and has an un-preemptable mission lock due to its binding contract requirement. The satellite constellation planner is tasked with ensuring this observation occurs overnight, within the specified timeframe of "in four days overnight." This is crucial for capturing the true reflectance characteristics of the area's vegetation.

We have a standard operational tasking for a mission over Santander de Quilichao in Colombia. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The focus is on conducting multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance using the VNIR sensor. It's essential for routine monitoring, ensuring we maintain a comprehensive environmental overview. Please ensure this task is scheduled to be completed in three days in the evening.

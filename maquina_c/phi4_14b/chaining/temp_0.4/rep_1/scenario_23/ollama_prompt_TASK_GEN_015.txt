We have a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The mission involves capturing high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis over Kepulauan Meranti, Indonesia. This task aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the region. It is a commercial tasking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, ensuring it aligns with our standard workflow updates. The data must be delivered in four days overnight.

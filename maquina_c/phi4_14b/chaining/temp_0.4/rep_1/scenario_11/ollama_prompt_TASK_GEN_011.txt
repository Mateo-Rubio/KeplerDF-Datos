We are submitting a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over the region of Tagliole in Italy. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance using VNIR capabilities. This commercial tasking is scheduled for an overnight capture in three days overnight, ensuring timely data availability.

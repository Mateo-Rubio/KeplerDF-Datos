We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Rokan Hilir in Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and a workflow update. The mission involves capturing high-resolution optical snapshots to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance. This task is scheduled for completion in three days in the morning, ensuring timely data availability as per our standard operating procedures.

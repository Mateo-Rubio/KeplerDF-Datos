We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation regarding a mission over San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia. The priority level for this task is medium importance, falling under routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking. We aim to perform a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR capabilities. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating an NDVI index to accurately determine surface reflectance. The data collection must be completed overnight in three days to ensure timely delivery of these vital environmental metrics, as per the strict deadline requirements.

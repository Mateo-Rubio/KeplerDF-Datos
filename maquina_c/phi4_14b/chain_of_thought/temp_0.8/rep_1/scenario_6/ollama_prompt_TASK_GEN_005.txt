We have a low priority importance task for our Earth-observation mission involving Sizzano, Italy. This is a flexible schedule request that can be opportunistically captured and may be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. Our goal is to conduct an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to capture cloud-penetrating imagery of the region. The data collection should take place in three days around mid-day. This serves as a non-essential background filler task with no binding operational constraints.

We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Santander de Quilichao, Colombia. The purpose of this task is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate NDVI indexes using VNIR technology. This routine monitoring forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking, ensuring we maintain a regular observation schedule. The imagery must be captured in the evening, in three days' time, with the parameters set as a fixed component of our normal operational workflow.

Logging a low priority importance task for Singkep in Indonesia as part of our opportunistic background survey activities. Our aim is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the area. This is treated as a non-essential background filler task, hence it has a flexible schedule and may be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The deadline for data products is set in four days in the morning, providing us ample time to conduct this testbed tracking task.

Logging a low priority importance task, which is a flexible schedule background filler to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis for the El Castillo region in Colombia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance during the afternoon in three days. This is classified as a non-essential background survey, treated as opportunistic capture work and can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience.

Logging a low priority importance task over Singkep Barat in Indonesia as part of our opportunistic background survey with flexible scheduling options. We aim to capture a multispectral vegetation analysis over the area to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance. This is treated as a non-essential background filler task, with a schedule that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The deadline for delivering the products is set for in four days in the morning.

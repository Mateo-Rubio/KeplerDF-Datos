Logging a standard operational tasking over Murung Raya in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We are after near-infrared reflection to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture in the area, which will help us discriminate gradients for agricultural management purposes. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the delivery deadline is set for four days from now overnight. The products should contribute to our understanding of water distribution and soil health over the region.

Logging a standard operational tasking over Santander de Quilichao in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We want a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the region in three days, specifically during the evening hours. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the delivery deadline is set for the evening of our target day. The products are expected to contribute to our understanding of the environmental conditions in Santander de Quilichao and inform subsequent decision-making processes.

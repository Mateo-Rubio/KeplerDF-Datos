With the primary sensor set to VIS, our mission aims for a high-resolution optical snapshot of Indonesia's Ecuatorial region during the overnight period in four days from now. This daylight photography will serve as a clear and detailed visual documentation essential for our baseline pipeline tracking setup. Given its low importance and flexible schedule, this background survey request allows for opportunistic capture, understanding that it could be dropped or rescheduled at convenience without compromising mission priorities. The goal is to capture the true-color visual imagery that will provide invaluable information about the region's landscape and activities under the cover of darkness.

In preparation for an upcoming project requiring detailed analysis of vegetation health in Colombia, we're tasked with capturing a multispectral vegetation snapshot over San Vicente del Caguán. This low priority importance request involves the VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index, all set to take place in four days overnight. As part of this background survey, our aim is to gather essential data on surface reflectance without the constraints of daylight photography or clear optical imagery. We're looking forward to leveraging this opportunistic capture for a deeper understanding of the region's ecological conditions, ensuring that our approach aligns with flexible scheduling and the non-essential nature of this task within our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

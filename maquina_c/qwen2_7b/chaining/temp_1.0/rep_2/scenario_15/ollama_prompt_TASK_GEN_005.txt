In the picturesque region of Italia, specifically within the city of Olmo al Brembo, an opportunity for capturing essential information awaits our satellite constellation planners. We aim to execute a routine monitoring task using a VIS sensor on "the day after tomorrow overnight" to provide high-resolution optical imagery of this remarkable location. This visually rich dataset will serve as daylight photography and true-color visual documentation that showcases the vibrant landscape and architectural details of Olmo al Brembo. It is a standard operational task, requiring the satellite to scan the area carefully using its visual instrument for clear optical data collection.

As a non-essential background filler task, our operator understands there might be flexibility in scheduling this request to ensure it fits conveniently within the baseline pipeline tracking setup without disrupting more critical tasks. Labeling this task as a "flexible schedule" enables our planners to determine if this "testbed tracking task" can be accommodated while keeping priority levels in mind for other mission allocations. With this straightforward yet significant data gathering, we aim to enrich our dataset while respecting the operational guidelines and ensuring minimal impact on high-priority missions.

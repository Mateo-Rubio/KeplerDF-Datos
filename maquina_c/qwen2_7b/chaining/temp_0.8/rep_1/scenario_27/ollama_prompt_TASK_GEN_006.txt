Given the need for high-resolution imagery to assess vegetation health and soil moisture content, an essential background survey has been requested using the near-infrared (NIR) sensor. The geographical context involves capturing data from Murung Raya, Indonesia—a region of significant agricultural importance. This low priority importance task is part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

The 'NIR' sensor will be utilized for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment, providing critical information for the local community and potential for scientific research on environmental dynamics. The capture must occur in three days overnight, as this upper limit defines the deadline for data availability, emphasizing that the task is to be executed efficiently without disrupting other high-priority missions.

The region of Murung Raya poses unique challenges due to its varied landscape and climate conditions, making the overnight imagery collection particularly useful for capturing accurate data during cooler temperatures and reduced atmospheric moisture content. This data will contribute to the comprehensive monitoring of environmental parameters, supporting decision-making processes that impact agriculture, disaster management, and biodiversity conservation in the area.

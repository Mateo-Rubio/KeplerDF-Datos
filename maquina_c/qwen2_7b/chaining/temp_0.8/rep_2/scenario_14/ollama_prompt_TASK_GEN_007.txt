At the request of our institutional priorities, we aim to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Bolívar, Colombia, using a versatile visible sensor for daylight photography. This visual documentation is part of an opportunistic background survey and will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking as a non-essential task with flexible scheduling. As our commitment lies in fulfilling critical operational needs, the necessity of this task warrants its position in our queue, aligning with our ongoing efforts to assess and monitor environmental changes. Scheduled for "in four days overnight", our satellite constellation will execute this mission, delivering a true-color visual record that captures the essence of Bolívar's landscape under the veil of darkness. This task, while not holding the highest priority for contractual obligations, serves as an opportunity to enrich our dataset with detailed imagery that can be invaluable in various research and monitoring applications.

In response to a medium-importance operational tasking, we aim to capture high-resolution optical imagery of Banyuasin, Indonesia, using a VNIR sensor around mid-day in three days. This standard operational task involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the NDVI index for vegetation analysis, part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission allocation is not based on urgency but rather on contractual obligations and asset value, ensuring a routine monitoring process that contributes to our standard workflow updates.

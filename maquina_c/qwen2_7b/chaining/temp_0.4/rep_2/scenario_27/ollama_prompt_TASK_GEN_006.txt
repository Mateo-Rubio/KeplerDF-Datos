In response to a low priority importance request, we aim to capture a near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment in Murung Raya, Indonesia. This opportunistic survey task will serve as a background filler, allowing for flexible scheduling that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if necessary. The mission's allocation authority and asset contractual value do not dictate urgency; instead, this is recognized as a standard operational tasking part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data collection will occur in three days overnight, providing a clear view for the near-infrared satellite to map water bodies and assess soil moisture without interference from daylight or other environmental factors. This non-essential background filler task is treated as a testbed tracking task, focusing on gathering valuable information that can contribute to our understanding of environmental conditions without compromising other higher-priority missions.

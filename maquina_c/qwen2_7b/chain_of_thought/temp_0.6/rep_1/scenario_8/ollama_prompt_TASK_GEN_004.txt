In three days, at the onset of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner is tasked with capturing a low-priority importance high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography across Ituango, Colombia. This mission involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating a vegetation NDVI index, focusing on the unique diurnal conditions of early morning light that best highlight the ecological dynamics of the region. The timing ensures optimal illumination for clear optical imagery without the harshness of midday sunlight or the shadows cast by evening's descent.

In order to capture the critical nighttime geometry of Norte's landscape in the days ahead, our automated satellite constellation planner has been tasked with executing a standard operational SAR mission allocation for the overnight period on "the day after tomorrow". This involves an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging capabilities, specifically targeted to pierce through any cloud cover and provide us with detailed ground geometry information essential for our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The parameters are fixed, reflecting normal operational status, and this task is part of a contractual obligation requiring fixed parameters for a routine monitoring operation that does not allow for preemption or rescheduling.

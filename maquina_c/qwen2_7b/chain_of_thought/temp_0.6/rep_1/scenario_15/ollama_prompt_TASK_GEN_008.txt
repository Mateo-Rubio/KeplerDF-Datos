In three days, at the break of dawn, our satellite constellation is tasked to capture high-resolution near-infrared imagery of the city of Piacenza in Italia. This standard operational tasking involves assessing soil moisture levels and delineating water body boundaries for a routine monitoring project. The mission aligns with fixed parameters under normal operational status, ensuring thermal mapping and capturing the heat signature of surfaces across the region. We aim to deliver this data product as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup without compromising on quality or detail.

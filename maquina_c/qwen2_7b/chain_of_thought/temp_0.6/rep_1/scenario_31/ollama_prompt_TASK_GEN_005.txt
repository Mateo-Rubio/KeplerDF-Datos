Given the urgency and operational context, this Earth-observation mission requires a detailed thermal mapping of the historical city of Rosasio, located in the culturally rich country of Italia. The primary sensor tasked is the TIR (Thermal Infrared) camera for capturing heat signatures across the city's infrastructure and urban fabric overnight on "the day after tomorrow". This high-priority request stems from an absolute institutional priority to ensure that the thermal data products are delivered by the strictly defined deadline, emphasizing critical payload capture requirements that necessitate an un-preemptable mission lock.

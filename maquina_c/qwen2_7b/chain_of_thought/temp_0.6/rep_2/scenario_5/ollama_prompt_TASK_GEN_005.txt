On the designated operational day, "the day after tomorrow," our task will focus on an overnight mission. The objective is to capture high-resolution optical snapshots of the area surrounding Borgo San Lorenzo, nestled in the heart of Italia. We aim to conduct this through a routine monitoring process, leveraging our VNIR sensor capabilities for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging. This will serve as an essential background survey for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance during the evening hours, ensuring minimal interference from daylight or other external factors that could affect our observational accuracy. The data gathered overnight will provide a comprehensive thermal mapping of the region's heat signatures and surface temperatures, aiding in the detection of any anomalies and mapping heat emissions under the cover of darkness.

Given the stringent operational deadline of "in four days", our priority task requires an overnight VNIR (visual near-infrared) sensor capture over the city of Betulia, located in Colombia. This mission aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels within the urban and rural landscapes, providing invaluable insights into vegetation health and environmental conditions under the cover of darkness. As it is a low priority importance request, we may drop or reschedule this task at convenience if more urgent operational priorities emerge during the timeframe. The data products are expected to serve as a background survey for further research and monitoring activities.

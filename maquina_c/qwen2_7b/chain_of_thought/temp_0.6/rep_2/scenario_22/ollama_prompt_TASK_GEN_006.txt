We're tasked with capturing high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of Indonesia's ecuatorial region on the exact day in four days, during the overnight period. This is considered a low priority importance request, given its flexible schedule nature and role as a non-essential background filler task within our operational workflow. The NIR sensor aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels for critical agricultural and environmental monitoring purposes. This "overnight" imagery will provide invaluable data for discriminating gradients of soil moisture conditions, crucial for our baseline pipeline tracking and opportunistic capture strategy.

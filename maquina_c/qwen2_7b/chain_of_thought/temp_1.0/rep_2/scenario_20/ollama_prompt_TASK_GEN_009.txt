We are planning for the execution of a standard operational tasking on the Indonesian island of Pelompek. Our focus will be on gathering a series of multispectral vegetation data using a VNIR sensor during the overnight period tomorrow, aiming for high-resolution optical snapshots with the goal of assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating vegetation's NDVI index across various locations in Pelompek. This imagery will provide essential details for monitoring and managing agricultural resources in the region effectively under low-light conditions.

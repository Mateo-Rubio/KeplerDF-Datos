On the day following "the day after tomorrow," at night during the "overnight" period, we will undertake thermal data acquisition over Indonesia's Malinau city utilizing our TIR sensor. As this mission carries a low priority level, it operates under a flexible schedule allowing for "drop or rescheduling at convenience."

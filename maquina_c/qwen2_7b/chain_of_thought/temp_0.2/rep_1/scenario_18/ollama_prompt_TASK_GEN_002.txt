In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our satellite constellation will be tasked with capturing a near-infrared reflection snapshot of the region known as Calderara di Reno, nestled within the beautiful country of Italia. This low priority importance request is framed as a flexible schedule "opportunistic capture", where the operator explicitly notes that this task can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The primary sensor to be utilized for this mission will be the near-infrared (NIR) sensor, which will provide invaluable data for mapping water body boundaries and assessing soil moisture levels, essential for agricultural and environmental monitoring purposes.

In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we are tasked with capturing a thermal map of the region known as Norte, located in the country of Slovenia. This "overnight" mission is considered a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling. The primary sensor to be utilized for this task will be the TIR (Thermal Infrared) sensor, which will enable us to capture heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the region. This thermal mapping will serve as a background survey, providing insights into heat emissions and potential anomalies that require further investigation.

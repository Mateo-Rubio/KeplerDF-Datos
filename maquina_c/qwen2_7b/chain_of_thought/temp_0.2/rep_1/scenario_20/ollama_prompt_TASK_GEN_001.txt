In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we request that our satellite constellation capture a detailed view of San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia. Utilizing the VNIR sensor, this mission aims for an overnight snapshot to provide high-resolution optical imagery, capturing the essence of this region under the cover of darkness. This standard operational tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status with fixed parameters that ensure a routine monitoring capability.

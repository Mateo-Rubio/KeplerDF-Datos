In three days, at the beginning of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution active radar scan of the region encompassing Uribe, Colombia. This cloud-penetrating capture aims to provide detailed surface imaging using microwave surface imaging techniques, essential for understanding ground geometry and features without being obscured by atmospheric conditions or weather. The data collected during this operational tasking will serve as a standard baseline pipeline tracking asset, contributing to our ongoing monitoring efforts with fixed parameters that represent normal operational status. Given its priority level of 1, this request is considered a low priority importance task, allowing for flexible scheduling and potentially dropping or rescheduling at convenience if necessary. The primary sensor deployed for this mission will be the SAR system, enabling us to capture thermal anomalies and heat signatures across the region, providing valuable insights into surface temperature profiles and heat emissions.

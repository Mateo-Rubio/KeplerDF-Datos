In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we request a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring activity using the VNIR sensor over Venaco, France. This task involves capturing high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and clear optical imagery to provide detailed visual documentation of the region. The mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to acquire this data as part of a standard workflow update without altering the deadline or shifting it to an adjacent time period.

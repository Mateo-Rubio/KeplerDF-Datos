In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data for the region of Italia, specifically focusing on the city of Monte Romano. This task is designated as a low priority importance request with flexible scheduling considerations. We aim to document the unique thermal signatures and surface moisture conditions that are best observed during this overnight period when the Earth cools down and the atmosphere stabilizes, providing us with a clear view for our analysis. The data collected will serve as a background survey, allowing us to assess soil moisture levels and water body boundaries without the interference of daytime sunlight or cloud cover.

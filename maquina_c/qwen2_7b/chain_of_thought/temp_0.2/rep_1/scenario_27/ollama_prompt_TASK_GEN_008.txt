In three days, around mid-day, we request a low priority importance tasking for a routine monitoring purpose. This involves capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot through daylight photography for Norte, utilizing our VNIR sensor. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index, as well as surface reflectance, to provide valuable insights into the local ecosystem dynamics. This standard operational tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

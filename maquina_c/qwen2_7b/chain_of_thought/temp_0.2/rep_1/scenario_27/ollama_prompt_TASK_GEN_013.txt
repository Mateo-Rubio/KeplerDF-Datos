In three days, during the afternoon, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis over Chivatá, Colombia. This critical payload capture involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the NDVI index for the area, which is essential for understanding the health of the vegetation. As an absolute institutional priority, this mission requires an un-preemptable execution to ensure timely delivery of the data products that will provide invaluable insights into the environmental conditions and potential agricultural impacts in the region.

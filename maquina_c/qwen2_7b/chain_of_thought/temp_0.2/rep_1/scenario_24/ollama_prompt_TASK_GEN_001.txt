In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors over the city of Sabana de Torres in Colombia. This "standard operational tasking" aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels across various ecosystems within this region, providing critical insights for environmental monitoring and agricultural management. The data collected overnight will serve as a part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, offering fixed parameters that represent normal operational status without compromising on quality or precision.

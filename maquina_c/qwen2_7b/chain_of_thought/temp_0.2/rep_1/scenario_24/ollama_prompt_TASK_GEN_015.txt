In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution active radar scan over the region of Indonesia, specifically focusing on Kuala Enok. This mission is designated as a low priority importance request, which means that while it is important, it can be accommodated within our operational schedule with flexibility. The primary objective is to perform cloud-penetrating capture using microwave surface imaging techniques, aiming to provide detailed ground geometry information without the interference of atmospheric conditions. This task will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis over Arsiero, Italy. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index for the region's surface reflectance. The objective is to gather detailed information on the health and distribution of vegetation using near-infrared reflection techniques, which will help in mapping water bodies and assessing soil moisture conditions. This standard operational tasking aims to provide critical insights into environmental changes and agricultural health without compromising the baseline pipeline tracking setup.

In four days, during the morning hours, we request the capture of a low priority importance task involving a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared sensors. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for the region of Cossogno, Italy. This "background survey" aims to provide insights into surface reflectance and soil moisture assessment without altering the deadline or shifting the time frame to an adjacent part of the day.

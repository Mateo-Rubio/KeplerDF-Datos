In three days, at the time of overnight, we request the deployment of our satellite constellation to capture a detailed active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology over Tarazá, Colombia. This mission is designated as a low priority importance task with flexible scheduling considerations, serving as a non-essential background survey aimed at assessing ground geometry and surface temperature profiles under conditions that are typically obscured by heavy weather or during the cover of darkness. The objective is to ensure comprehensive coverage for cloud-penetrating capture and thermal anomaly detection, providing invaluable data for operational planning and decision-making without compromising the integrity of our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

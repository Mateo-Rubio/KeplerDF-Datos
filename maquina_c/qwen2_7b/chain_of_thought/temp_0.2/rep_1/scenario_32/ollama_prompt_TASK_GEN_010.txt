In four days, at the time of overnight, we request a low priority importance tasking for a nighttime capture using a VNIR sensor over San Vicente del Caguán, Colombia. This involves a flexible schedule for opportunistic surveying, focusing on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the vegetation NDVI index as well as surface reflectance. The objective is to gather detailed information about the local environment without altering the deadline, ensuring that the data collection occurs precisely at the specified time.

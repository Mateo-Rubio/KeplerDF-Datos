In four days, at the time when the world sleeps and the stars shine bright, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of France's enchanting landscape. This daylight photography will serve as a true-color visual documentation of the region, offering a clear view of its beauty under the cover of darkness. The mission is set to be an opportunistic background survey task, with no strict urgency attached. We aim to provide thermal mapping capabilities for this area overnight, capturing heat signatures and surface temperature profiles without compromising the natural rhythm of the day. This non-essential background filler task will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status and fitting into our flexible schedule for opportunistic capture.

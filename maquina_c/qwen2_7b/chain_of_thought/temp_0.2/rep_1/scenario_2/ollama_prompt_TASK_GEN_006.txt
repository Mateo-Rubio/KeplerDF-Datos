In three days, during the afternoon, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Indonesia's equatorial region. This involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the vegetation NDVI index using near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. The goal is to provide thermal mapping data that captures heat signatures and surface temperature profiles for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions, all part of a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

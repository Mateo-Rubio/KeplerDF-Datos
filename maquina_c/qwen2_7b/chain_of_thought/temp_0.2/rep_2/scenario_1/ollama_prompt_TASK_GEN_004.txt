Given the operational context, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance request for a nighttime observation using the VNIR sensor. Specifically, we aim to conduct a low priority importance tasking on the 'the day after tomorrow' at an 'overnight' diurnal period in Colombia, focusing on the city of Ibagué. This involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the vegetation NDVI index as well as surface reflectance for this region under the cover of darkness. The deadline to deliver the data products is strictly set for that particular overnight timeframe, without any alteration or extension to other parts of the day.

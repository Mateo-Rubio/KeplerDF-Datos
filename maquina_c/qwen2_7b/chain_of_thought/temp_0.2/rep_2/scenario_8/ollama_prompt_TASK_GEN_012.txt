In three days, at the time of overnight, we request a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography and clear optical imagery from our satellite constellation over Indonesia's equatorial region. This task is designated as a low priority importance request with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. We aim to document the area's landscape and urban environment under the cover of darkness, ensuring that we capture the heat signature and surface temperature profile of the region. This non-essential background survey will serve as a testbed tracking task, allowing us to assess the performance of our sensors in various lighting conditions without interfering with more critical operational tasks.

In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Ituango, Colombia. This involves a low priority importance request for a routine monitoring task using a VNIR sensor to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the vegetation NDVI index. The goal is to provide clear optical imagery through daylight photography, offering a true-color visual documentation of the area's surface reflectance without altering or extending the deadline.

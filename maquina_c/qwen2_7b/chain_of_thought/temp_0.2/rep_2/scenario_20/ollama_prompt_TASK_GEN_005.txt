In three days, around mid-day, we aim to capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of Pavullo nel Frignano, Italy. This operational tasking involves a standard monitoring procedure for assessing soil moisture levels and water body boundaries using the unique spectral signature provided by the near-infrared band. The imagery will be acquired at the specified time to ensure optimal conditions for distinguishing soil and water characteristics, contributing to our baseline pipeline tracking of environmental parameters in this region.

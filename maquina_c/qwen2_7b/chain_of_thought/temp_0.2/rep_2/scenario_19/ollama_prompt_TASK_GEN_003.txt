In response to the urgent operational execution required by our binding contract with the Indonesian government for a critical payload capture, we are tasked with conducting an overnight thermal mapping mission over the equatorial region of Indonesia. Specifically, this will occur "in three days" from our current date. This absolute institutional priority demands an un-preemptable mission lock, ensuring that our team delivers this essential data product on time to support critical decision-making processes.

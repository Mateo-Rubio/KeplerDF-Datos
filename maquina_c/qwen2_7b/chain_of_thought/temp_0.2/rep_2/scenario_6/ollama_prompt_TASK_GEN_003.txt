On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have come out, our satellite constellation will capture thermal mapping data over Malinau, Indonesia. This "overnight" mission is a low priority importance request, designed as a flexible schedule task to be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The primary sensor used for this operation will be the TIR (Thermal Infrared) instrument, which will provide us with a heat signature and surface temperature profile of the region. This data collection aims to support our ongoing thermal anomaly detection efforts in the area, contributing to our understanding of heat emissions from various sources.

In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun their nightly dance, our automated satellite constellation planner will be tasked with capturing a near-infrared reflection snapshot of the city of Monterrey, Colombia. This nighttime mission is considered a standard operational tasking, part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The imagery we seek aims to map water bodies and assess soil moisture levels, crucial for environmental monitoring and agricultural planning. The deadline for delivering this data product is strict, reflecting the importance of timely information for decision-making processes.

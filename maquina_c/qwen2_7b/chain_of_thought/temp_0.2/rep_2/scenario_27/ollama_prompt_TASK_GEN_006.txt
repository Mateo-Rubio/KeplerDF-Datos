In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle above Murung Raya, Indonesia, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling. The objective is to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries using the unique properties of near-infrared reflection. This "non-essential background filler task" aims to provide valuable insights into environmental conditions without imposing strict operational constraints or deadlines beyond the specified timeframe.

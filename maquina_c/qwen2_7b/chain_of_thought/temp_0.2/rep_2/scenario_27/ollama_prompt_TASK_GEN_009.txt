In three days, around mid-day, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey using a VNIR sensor for daylight photography. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the vegetation NDVI index to evaluate surface reflectance. The mission is set in Indonesia, specifically near the equator, where clear optical imagery will provide valuable insights into the region's ecological health without altering the deadline or shifting it to an adjacent part of the day.

In four days, at the absolute beginning of the morning, our satellite constellation must capture a high-resolution active radar scan over the city of Remedios in Colombia. This mission allocation is of the highest importance due to its critical payload capture requirement and contractual obligations involving a binding directive for a cloud-penetrating capture that will provide invaluable data for our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The SAR imagery collected during this time window will be essential for understanding ground geometry, offering us crucial insights into the urban landscape of Remedios without interference from atmospheric conditions or cloud cover.

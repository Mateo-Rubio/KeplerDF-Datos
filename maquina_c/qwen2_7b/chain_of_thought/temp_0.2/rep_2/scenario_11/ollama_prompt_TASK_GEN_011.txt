In three days, at the time of overnight, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis over Tagliole, Italia. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index for the area, focusing on the unique features illuminated under the moonlight. The objective is to gather detailed information about the vegetation health and distribution without interference from sunlight, ensuring that we capture a comprehensive thermal map of the region's heat signatures.

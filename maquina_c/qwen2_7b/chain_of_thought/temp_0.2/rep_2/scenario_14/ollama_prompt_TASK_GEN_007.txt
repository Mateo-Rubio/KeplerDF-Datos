In four days, at the time when the world sleeps and the stars are most visible, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the city of Bolívar in Colombia. This "true-color visual documentation" will serve as an overnight mission, a low priority importance task that allows us to assess the city's landscape under the cover of darkness. The imagery will be collected during the "overnight" period, ensuring minimal disturbance to daily activities and providing unique insights into urban environments illuminated by artificial lights. This "background survey" is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, where we can flexibly schedule the task for opportunistic capture without compromising the quality or timeliness of data delivery.

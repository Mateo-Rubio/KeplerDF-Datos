In approximately four days, around mid-day, we require a standard operational tasking for a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging. This will involve cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry in Colombia, specifically in the city of La Cumbre. The mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to deliver thermal mapping data that captures heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, as well as assess vegetation health through multispectral analysis and near-infrared reflection for water body boundaries and soil moisture assessment.

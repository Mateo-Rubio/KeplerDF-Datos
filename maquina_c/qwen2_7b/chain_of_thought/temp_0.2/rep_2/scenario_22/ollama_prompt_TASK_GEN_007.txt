In three days, overnight, our satellite constellation will capture a high-resolution active radar scan of Sesquilé, Colombia. This mission, designated as a low priority importance tasking, involves an "overnight" SAR acquisition to provide cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry. The objective is to deliver thermal mapping data that captures heat signatures and surface temperature profiles for critical infrastructure monitoring in the region. This "overnight" SAR imagery will serve as a baseline pipeline tracking asset, contributing to our standard operational tasking without altering the deadline for data delivery.

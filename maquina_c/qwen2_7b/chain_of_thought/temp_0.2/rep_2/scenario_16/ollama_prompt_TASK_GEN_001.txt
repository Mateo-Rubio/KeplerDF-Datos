In three days, at the time when the sun begins its descent and casts long shadows across the landscape of Santander de Quilichao, Colombia, we request the capture of a multispectral vegetation analysis using the VNIR sensor. This high-resolution optical snapshot aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the area, providing critical insights into the health and productivity of the local flora. The operational deadline is strict, requiring this data product to be delivered promptly as the evening approaches.

In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun to twinkle in the sky, our satellite constellation will be tasked with capturing a near-infrared reflection snapshot of Mulya Bakti, Indonesia. This overnight mission is part of our standard operational tasking, representing normal pipeline tracking with fixed parameters that ensure the integrity of our baseline services. The primary sensor onboard will perform a detailed assessment of water body boundaries and soil moisture levels, providing invaluable data for environmental monitoring and agricultural management in this region.

In three days, at the start of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of the city of Piacenza in Italia. This task is considered a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status. The primary objective is to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels for agricultural monitoring purposes. The imagery will provide critical data on water bodies and soil conditions without being influenced by cloud cover or atmospheric conditions, ensuring accurate and reliable information for decision-making.

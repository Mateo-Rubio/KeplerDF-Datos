In four days, at the dawn of morning light, we are to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the picturesque region of Páez, Colombia. This task is presented as a low priority importance request, with flexibility in scheduling and execution time. It's a non-essential background survey that can be dropped or rescheduled according to convenience. The primary sensor involved will provide us with clear daylight photography, enabling us to document the landscape with true-color visual imagery. This mission aims to capture the essence of Páez during the serene hours of early morning without altering the deadline.

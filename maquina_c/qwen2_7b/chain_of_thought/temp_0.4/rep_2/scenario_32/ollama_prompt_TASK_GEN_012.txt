In four days, at the time of overnight, we request a tasking for the near-infrared (NIR) sensor over the city of Murung Raya in Indonesia. The objective is to capture a thermal mapping of the area using this sensor during the night hours when atmospheric conditions are most conducive for accurate surface temperature profiling and thermal anomaly detection. This standard operational tasking aims to provide critical insights into heat signatures and surface temperatures without interference from daylight or cloud cover, contributing to baseline pipeline tracking and monitoring activities with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

In four days, at the start of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will be tasked with capturing a detailed active radar scan across the region of Norte, France. This operation is classified as a low priority importance request, implying that while it's an essential task, it can be accommodated into the operational schedule with flexibility. The primary sensor to be deployed is SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar), which will enable us to perform cloud-penetrating capture, providing invaluable insights into ground geometry and surface features without being obscured by weather conditions. This mission aims to deliver a comprehensive set of data products that will serve as a critical input for our baseline pipeline tracking setup, contributing to normal operational status updates and potentially serving as a background survey to assess environmental changes or support commercial tasking activities.

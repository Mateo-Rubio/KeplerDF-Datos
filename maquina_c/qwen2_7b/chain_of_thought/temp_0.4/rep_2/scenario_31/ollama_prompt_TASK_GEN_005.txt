On the day after tomorrow, at midnight when the world sleeps and the stars are most visible, our satellite constellation will capture a thermal mapping snapshot of the city of Roasio in Italia. This "overnight" mission is designated as a low priority importance task, allowing for a flexible schedule to accommodate this background survey or testbed tracking task without compromising our normal operational status. The primary sensor onboard will be conducting a TIR scan to capture heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the cityscape, providing invaluable data on thermal anomalies and heat emissions that can inform urban planning and environmental monitoring efforts.

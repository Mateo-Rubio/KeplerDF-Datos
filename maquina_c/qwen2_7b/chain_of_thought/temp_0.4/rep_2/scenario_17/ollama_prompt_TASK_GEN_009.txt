In three days, during the morning hours, we are tasked with capturing high-resolution SAR imagery over Indonesia's equatorial region. This operational task involves conducting an active radar scan to gather cloud-penetrating data for critical surface imaging purposes. The mission is considered a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling to accommodate opportunistic capture while ensuring that the data aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup and normal operational status. The primary objective is to deliver thermal mapping capabilities, focusing on capturing heat signatures and surface temperature profiles without altering the deadline to an adjacent part of the day.

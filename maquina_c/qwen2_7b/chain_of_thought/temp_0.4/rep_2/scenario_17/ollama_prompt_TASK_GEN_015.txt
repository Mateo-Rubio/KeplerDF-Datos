In response to your request for data collection on a standard operational tasking basis, we aim to capture high-resolution optical imagery using near-infrared sensors. The mission will focus on the Kepulauan Meranti region in Indonesia, scheduled for execution in four days, with the primary objective to gather data during the morning hours. This will ensure clear conditions for optimal imaging without the interference of cloud cover or low light levels typically associated with other times of day. The imagery will serve as a baseline pipeline tracking tool and contribute to normal operational status updates, providing valuable insights into environmental conditions and facilitating routine monitoring activities.

Given the high priority level and the deadline set for tomorrow evening, our satellite constellation is tasked with capturing a low-priority importance VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for Vista Hermosa, Colombia. This "opportunistic capture" aims to provide valuable information on surface reflectance without interfering with more critical operational tasks or altering the established deadline. The imagery will serve as a background survey, offering insights into the region's ecological conditions without compromising the urgency of higher-priority missions.

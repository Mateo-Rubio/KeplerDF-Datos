This involves an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology, piercing through any cloud cover or atmospheric obstructions to capture detailed ground geometry. The deadline for this critical data collection is set firmly at "in the evening", ensuring that our satellite assets are allocated with the necessary priority level of medium importance. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, adhering to fixed parameters representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. The high-resolution optical snapshot we aim to achieve will serve as an essential component in our ongoing efforts to monitor and understand the intricate dynamics of this region, providing invaluable insights for environmental management and urban planning.

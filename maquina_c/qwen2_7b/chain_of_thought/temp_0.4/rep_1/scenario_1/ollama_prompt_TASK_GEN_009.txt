In three days, at the time of the evening, we aim to capture a detailed active radar scan over the region of Kubu Raya, Indonesia. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. The primary objective is to obtain high-resolution optical imagery through the use of microwave surface imaging, enabling cloud-penetrating capture that will provide critical insights into ground geometry and surface features, essential for our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

To address our operational priorities, we are tasked with capturing high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors on the satellite constellation. This mission aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the region of Prati di Tivo, located in the picturesque country of Italia. The deadline for delivering this critical data product is set for the morning four days from now, ensuring a clear optical imagery capture without any cloud interference.

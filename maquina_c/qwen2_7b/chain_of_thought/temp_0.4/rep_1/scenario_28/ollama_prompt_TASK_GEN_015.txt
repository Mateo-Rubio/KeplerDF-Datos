In four days, during the overnight period, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis for the region. This high-resolution optical snapshot aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the area surrounding Rumah Budak, Malaysia. The objective is to document surface reflectance characteristics under the cover of darkness, ensuring thermal anomalies are detected and mapped accurately without interference from daylight or atmospheric conditions.

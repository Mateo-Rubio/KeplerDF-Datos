In four days, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of Pontagna, Italy, during the overnight period. This mission is categorized as a low priority importance request, which means it can be accommodated into our flexible schedule for opportunistic capture without compromising our core operations. As part of this task, we aim to provide clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation of the region under the cover of darkness. This will enable us to assess the landscape and urban structures with minimal interference from natural light sources.

Given the nature of the request, it is understood that the deadline for data delivery is strict and must be met as specified, without any adjustments to the time frame or shifting the capture window to another part of the day. The primary sensor involved will be our VIS (visible) sensor, which will capture the essence of the scene during the overnight period, ensuring that we document the region accurately and efficiently.

In response to the high priority directive for three days hence, our satellite constellation will capture a mandatory infrared (NIR) observation of the evening landscape in Silea, Italia. This critical payload acquisition aims to provide essential data on water body boundaries and soil moisture assessment, ensuring a comprehensive thermal mapping of the region's heat emissions as the sun sets. With an un-preemptable mission lock on this deadline, we aim to deliver the most accurate and detailed imagery for decision-making purposes without altering the specified operational timeframe.

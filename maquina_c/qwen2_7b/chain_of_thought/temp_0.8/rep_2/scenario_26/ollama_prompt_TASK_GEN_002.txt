In the evening three days from now, our satellite constellation will execute a standard operational tasking requirement in the picturesque city of Corteno Golgi, Italia. The task entails capturing an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology for daylight photography purposes, focusing on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating NDVI indices alongside surface reflectance measurements. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking effort, with an absolute institutional priority due to the nature of its objectives and the importance of delivering high-quality data products within the specified deadline.

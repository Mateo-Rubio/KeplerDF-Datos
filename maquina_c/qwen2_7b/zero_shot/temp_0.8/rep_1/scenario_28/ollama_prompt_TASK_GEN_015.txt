To address a low priority importance task, our Earth-observation mission requires the capture of the "Malaysia - Rumah Budak" region using the VNIR sensor for a vegetation health assessment. Specifically, this involves analyzing the chlorophyll absorption level across diverse ecosystems within the area to calculate the vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. This is part of an opportunistic background survey designed to provide valuable insights into environmental conditions without imposing strict deadlines.

With a flexible schedule, we aim to obtain these data products by "in four days", ensuring they are ingested into our baseline pipeline tracking setup before the overnight period starts on that day. Our intention is to execute this task when convenient for the operational workflow updates, treating it as a non-essential background filler task without compromising the primary focus areas of higher priority missions.

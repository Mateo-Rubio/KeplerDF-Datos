Tasking Request: Multispectral Vegetation Analysis in Colombia

We require a multispectral vegetation analysis mission to be allocated for the region of Vista Hermosa in Colombia. This task is part of our standard operational tasking and is considered a commercial tasking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

The objective of this mission is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance using near-infrared reflection. This will enable us to discriminate gradients and obtain valuable insights into the region's vegetation health.

We need the data products to be ingested into our pipeline before the day after tomorrow overnight at the latest, as this is a routine monitoring part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

Please execute this task with high priority, but not urgency, as it is a standard operational tasking.

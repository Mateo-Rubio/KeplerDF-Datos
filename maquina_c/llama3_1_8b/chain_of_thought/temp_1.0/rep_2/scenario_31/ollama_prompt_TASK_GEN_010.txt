STAGE 1 - REASONING

a) The exact string in 'target_day' is "the day after tomorrow".

b) The exact string in 'target_diurnal_period' is "in the morning".

c) The single word contained in the string "in the morning" is "morning".

d) The request specifies a deadline for the next day, which is a shift in time. However, this is an operational requirement and cannot be altered or extended. The temporal parameters represent a strict operational DEADLINE to deliver the data products, NOT the orbital capture window. This means that the deadline is indeed being shifted to the morning of the next day, but it must be executed on that specific date and time.

No adjustments are made to the deadline as it is an operational requirement to deliver the data products by the specified time.

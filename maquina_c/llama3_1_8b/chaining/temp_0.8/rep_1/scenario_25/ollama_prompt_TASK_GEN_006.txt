We require a low-priority importance task with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, to be treated as a non-essential background filler task where we can drop or reschedule at convenience. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over the region of Singkep Barat in Indonesia, with a focus on clear optical imagery for true-color visual documentation. We aim to capture this data in four days in the morning.

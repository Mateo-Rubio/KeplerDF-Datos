Tasking Request: Baseline Pipeline Tracking for Multispectral Vegetation Analysis in Indonesia
We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission is to capture high-resolution multispectral data of vegetation health and NDVI index in the Simabur region, Indonesia. The primary objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and surface reflectance using active multispectral sensors. This tasking has been designated as a standard operational tasking with commercial priority, warranting a critical payload capture and mandatory directive. Data delivery is required "in three days in the morning".

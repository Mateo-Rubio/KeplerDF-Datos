We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The target location is in the region of Rheinwald, Switzerland, where we aim to capture clear optical imagery for true-color visual documentation. This task has been designated as a standard operational tasking with a commercial tasking priority, and we require data to be delivered around mid-day in four days.

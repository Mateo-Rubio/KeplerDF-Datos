As part of our routine monitoring and baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters, we're logging a standard operational tasking over Kepulauan Meranti in Indonesia for our near-infrared sensor VNIR to perform a multispectral vegetation analysis. This involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels across the region and calculating an NDVI index along with surface reflectance. We aim to gather this data in four days at an overnight timeframe, ensuring normal operational status without any urgency. The products should be ready for ingest into our pipeline by then.

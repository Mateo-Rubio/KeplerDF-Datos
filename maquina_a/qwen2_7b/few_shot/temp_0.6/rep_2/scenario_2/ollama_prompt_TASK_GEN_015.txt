Logging a standard operational tasking over Bangka Selatan in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. For the near-infrared reflection, we aim to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels for improved agricultural management. This involves a 'cloud-penetrating capture' using microwave surface imaging to provide critical information on water bodies and soil moisture status. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the delivery deadline is set for in three days in the morning, with the products expected to enhance decision-making processes for local stakeholders.

Logging a low priority importance task over Singkep in Indonesia as part of our flexible schedule and opportunistic capture tasks. We aim to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the area. This is a background survey, treating it as a testbed tracking task. The deadline for delivering the products is set in four days in the morning, allowing for convenient rescheduling or dropping if needed.

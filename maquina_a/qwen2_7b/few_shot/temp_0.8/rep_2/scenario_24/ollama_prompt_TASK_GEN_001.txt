Logging a standard operational tasking over Sabana de Torres in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim for a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and work out an NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the rural landscapes around town. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and we need the products by three days from now overnight, capturing the twilight conditions that allow optimal capture of these spectral signatures.

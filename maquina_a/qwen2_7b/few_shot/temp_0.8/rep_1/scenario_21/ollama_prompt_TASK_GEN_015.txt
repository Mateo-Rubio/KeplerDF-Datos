Logging a standard operational tasking for the Solok region in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim to perform a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance for the area. This involves assessing the health and productivity of the local vegetation using near-infrared reflection, particularly important for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. The products should be ready for ingestion into our systems in four days during the morning time frame. This is a routine monitoring task at normal operational status, representing standard workflow updates.

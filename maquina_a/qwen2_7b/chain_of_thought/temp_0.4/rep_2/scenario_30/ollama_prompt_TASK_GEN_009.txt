In response to the high importance and binding contract requirement indicated by a priority level of 3, we are tasked with capturing an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. This is aimed at cloud-penetrating the capture for a critical payload, specifically targeting the region in Indonesia, focusing on the city of Gilirang. The deadline for this mission allocation is set for an overnight period, precisely "in three days" from now. This operational tasking involves the use of an advanced SAR system to ensure we gather comprehensive data on ground geometry and surface features without interference from atmospheric conditions or cloud cover.

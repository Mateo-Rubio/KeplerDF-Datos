In three days, specifically during the evening, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis for the area of interest located in the picturesque city of Corteno Golgi, nestled within the beautiful country of Italia. This standard operational tasking involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to provide valuable insights into the health and productivity of the local vegetation. The goal is to obtain detailed information on surface reflectance for this area, crucial for agricultural monitoring and environmental studies.

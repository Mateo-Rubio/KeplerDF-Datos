In four days, overnight, our satellite constellation will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Toledo, Colombia. This true-color visual documentation will serve as a critical background survey for environmental monitoring purposes, providing us with essential data on land use and vegetation health without the need for immediate action or a binding contract requirement. The imagery will be collected during the overnight period to ensure optimal conditions for clear optical photography, allowing for detailed examination of the city's landscape and urban development under low-light conditions.

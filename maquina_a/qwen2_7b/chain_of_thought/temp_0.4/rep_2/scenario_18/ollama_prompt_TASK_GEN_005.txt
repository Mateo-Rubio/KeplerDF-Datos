On the day after tomorrow, in the evening, our Earth-observation mission will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the stunning city of Norte, nestled within the vibrant country of Italia. This low-priority importance task is framed as a flexible schedule opportunity for opportunistic data collection, aiming to document the city's beauty under the golden hues of dusk. The imagery acquired will serve as a true-color visual documentation, providing invaluable insights into the urban landscape and natural surroundings without the constraints of daytime lighting or cloud cover.

In three days, at the time when the sun begins its descent, illuminating the skies with a warm glow, our Earth-observation mission operator will capture thermal mapping data of Bontang, Indonesia. This task is classified as a low priority importance request, allowing for a flexible schedule where the operator can drop or reschedule at their convenience. The primary sensor deployed will be the TIR (Thermal Infrared) instrument, aiming to provide insights into surface temperature profiles and thermal anomalies across the region. As daylight fades into evening, the operator will ensure that the data captured represents a comprehensive heat signature, enabling us to discern various heat emissions and temperature gradients within Bontang's urban landscape.

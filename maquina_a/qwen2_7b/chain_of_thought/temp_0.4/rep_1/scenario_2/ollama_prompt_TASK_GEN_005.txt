In four days, at the time when most would be asleep, our mission is to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Sotta, France, during the overnight period. This daylight photography will serve as a clear and detailed visual documentation of the region under the cover of darkness. Our task is an opportunistic background survey that can be scheduled with flexibility, fitting into our baseline pipeline tracking without disrupting more critical operations. We aim to provide thermal mapping capabilities for capturing heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, essential for detecting anomalies and understanding heat emissions across the area. This request does not alter the strict deadline for data delivery; it remains firmly set for the four days ahead at midnight, ensuring we capture the essence of Sotta under the moonlight without any adjustments to the timeframe.

In response to the request for an Earth-observation mission allocation with a priority level of Low Importance, we are scheduled to capture high-resolution optical imagery through near-infrared (NIR) sensors on the Indonesian territory, specifically in the city of Sintang. The operational tasking is set for "in three days" during the overnight period, adhering strictly to the deadline without any adjustments to the time frame. This non-essential background survey aims to assess soil moisture and discriminate soil gradients through near-infrared reflection, serving as a testbed tracking task for our satellite constellation planner.

In three days, at the time of the evening, we aim to capture a detailed active radar scan of the region known as El Piñal, located in the country of Colombia. This task is part of our standard operational pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and a routine monitoring activity. The primary sensor involved will be our advanced SAR system, designed for cloud-penetrating capture using microwave surface imaging to provide us with comprehensive ground geometry insights, essential for our baseline pipeline tracking operations.

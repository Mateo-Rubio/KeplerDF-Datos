In three days, at the precise time when the sun has set and the stars have begun to twinkle in the vast Colombian sky above Tarazá, Colombia, we are tasked with capturing a critical snapshot of the region using our advanced Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR). This mission is of low priority importance, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates opportunistic capture. The SAR sensor will perform an active radar scan, piercing through any cloud cover or weather conditions, to provide us with invaluable insights into ground geometry and surface features. This data collection is part of our standard operational tasking, contributing to the baseline pipeline tracking of the region. As we aim to capture this thermal mapping, we seek to document heat signatures, surface temperature profiles, and detect any thermal anomalies that might indicate critical environmental changes or activities. The imagery produced will not only serve as a testament to our commitment to monitoring the natural environment but also assist in making informed decisions based on accurate data.

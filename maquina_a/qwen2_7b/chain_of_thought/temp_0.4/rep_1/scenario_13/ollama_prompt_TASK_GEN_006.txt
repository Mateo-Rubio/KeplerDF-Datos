In three days, around mid-day, our satellite constellation will capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of Sarolangun, Indonesia. This task, with a priority level of medium importance, involves an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to pierce through any cloud cover and provide detailed ground geometry data. The deadline for delivering this data is strict, as it represents a critical operational requirement for our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters. We aim to capture a heat signature or surface temperature profile for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions, as well as assess water body boundaries or soil moisture levels to discriminate gradients in our background survey task.

Tomorrow, at an overnight period, our Earth-observation mission operator aims to capture a near-infrared (NIR) reflection dataset targeting the strategic location of Malinau city, Indonesia. This low priority importance task is part of ongoing baseline pipeline tracking efforts, contributing to environmental studies, agricultural planning, and disaster management. The imagery collected will serve as a critical resource for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment, providing insights into ecosystem health and land use patterns during the overnight hours.

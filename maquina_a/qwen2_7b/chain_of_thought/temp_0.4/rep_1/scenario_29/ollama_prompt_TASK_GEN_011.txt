In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we aim to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis over Venaco, France. Our objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the area, as well as gather detailed surface reflectance data. This standard operational tasking requires an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to ensure cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry, providing us with critical insights for our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

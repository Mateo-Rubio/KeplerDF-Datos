In three days, at the precise time when the sun dips below the horizon and the stars begin their nightly dance, we are tasked with capturing a near-infrared reflection snapshot of Calderara di Reno, Italia. Our objective is to map the boundaries of water bodies and assess soil moisture levels under the cover of darkness, ensuring that our data collection aligns with the strict operational deadline set for "overnight". This task, while not carrying the highest priority, remains a critical background survey that allows us to capture the unique characteristics of the region under moonlight, providing invaluable insights without interfering with more urgent missions.

In response to the request for an Earth-observation mission, our team will capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of the city of Zetaquira in Colombia tomorrow morning. This task is designated as a low priority importance request with flexible scheduling considerations. The data collection will be executed as part of a background survey aimed at mapping water body boundaries and assessing soil moisture levels without altering the deadline to deliver the data products, which is set for the morning hours on the target day.

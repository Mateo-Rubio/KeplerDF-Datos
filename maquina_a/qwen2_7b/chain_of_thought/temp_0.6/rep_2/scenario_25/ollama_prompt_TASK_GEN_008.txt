In four days, at a time when the sun's rays are bright and the air is crisp with freshness, our satellite constellation will embark on a standard operational tasking to capture high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis. This mission aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels within the lush landscapes of Prati di Tivo, Italy, using our VNIR sensor in the morning hours. The goal is not just to document the vibrant greenery but to calculate the Vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) and surface reflectance, providing valuable insights into the health and productivity of the flora. This baseline pipeline tracking setup ensures that every parameter is fixed, reflecting a normal operational status with a focus on gathering essential data without the need for urgent or expedited execution.

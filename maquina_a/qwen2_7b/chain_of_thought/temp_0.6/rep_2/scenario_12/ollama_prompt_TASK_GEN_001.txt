On the night before tomorrow, our Earth-observation mission will focus on conducting a near-infrared reflection scan of the region surrounding San José del Guaviare in Colombia. This task is designated as a low priority importance request with flexible scheduling options, allowing for "opportunistic capture" without compromising our primary operational commitments. The aim is to gather data that will help in mapping water body boundaries and assessing soil moisture levels, providing valuable insights into environmental conditions within the specified area overnight.

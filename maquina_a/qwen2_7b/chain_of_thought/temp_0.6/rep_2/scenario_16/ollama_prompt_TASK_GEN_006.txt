In three days, at the time designated for an overnight satellite capture, our automated constellation planner is tasked with delivering a low priority importance observational mission focusing on the Indonesian territory specifically in the region surrounding Mulya Bakti. This mission involves conducting a near-infrared (NIR) sensor scan over the area to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries under the cover of darkness for optimal conditions. The data is expected to serve as a valuable background survey, offering insights into environmental conditions without the interference of daylight or other day-time activities.

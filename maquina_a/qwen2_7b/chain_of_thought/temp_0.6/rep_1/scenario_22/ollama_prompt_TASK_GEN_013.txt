In three days, our Earth-observation mission will focus on capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of the charming city of Ráquira, Colombia. This daylight photography will serve as true-color visual documentation for our baseline pipeline tracking setup. Given the "mid-day" target time, we aim to leverage clear optical imagery that can pierce through any cloud cover and provide us with an unobstructed view of the city's geographical features. As this request falls under Priority 1 (Low Importance), we are treating it as a flexible background survey task with no strict urgency for immediate execution; instead, it is considered a non-essential background filler task that can be conveniently scheduled or dropped according to our operational convenience. The primary sensor we will utilize for this mission is the VIS sensor, enabling us to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance to further understand the environmental dynamics of the area.

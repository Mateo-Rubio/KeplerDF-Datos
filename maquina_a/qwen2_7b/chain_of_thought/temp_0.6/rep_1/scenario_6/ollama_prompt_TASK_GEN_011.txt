In preparation for capturing essential data on the ground in Schweiz/Suisse/Svizzera/Svizra, particularly in the picturesque Rheinwald region, our team is scheduling a standard operational tasking using a near-infrared sensor. This mission aims to focus on an active radar scan around mid-day in four days, enabling us to assess water body boundaries and soil moisture levels with precision. The deadline for delivering this data product is firmly set for that specific day and time, aligning with our baseline pipeline tracking setup and maintaining 'fixed parameters' for normal operational status.

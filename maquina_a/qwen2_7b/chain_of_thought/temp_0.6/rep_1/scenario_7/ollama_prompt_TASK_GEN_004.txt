To ensure capture of comprehensive thermal data from the region of Huasanó, Colombia, our Earth-observation mission will be allocated as a low priority importance task with a flexible schedule to accommodate opportunistic capture. The operational request involves a thermal infrared (TIR) scan to be executed overnight, precisely three days hence. This mission is part of a background survey aimed at mapping heat signatures and surface temperature profiles without the constraint of daylight hours, providing invaluable insights into thermal anomalies and heat emissions in Huasanó during this specific timeframe.

In preparation for a comprehensive environmental assessment scheduled for the day after tomorrow, our mission prioritizes capturing detailed multispectral vegetation data in mid-day conditions using the VNIR sensor. This high-resolution optical snapshot will enable a clear analysis of chlorophyll absorption levels and surface reflectance across the region in Colombia, specifically focusing on the town of Nunchía. As a low-priority importance task with flexible scheduling considerations, this operational request adheres to the designated deadline while acknowledging the need for opportunistic capture if necessary. The data collected will contribute as a valuable background survey and testbed tracking task, reinforcing our continuous monitoring baseline pipeline.

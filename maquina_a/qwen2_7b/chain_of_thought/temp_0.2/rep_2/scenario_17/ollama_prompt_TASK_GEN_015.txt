In four days, at the beginning of the morning, we aim to capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of the Indonesian region, specifically focusing on the Kepulauan Meranti area. This standard operational tasking involves assessing soil moisture levels and delineating water body boundaries using the unique properties of near-infrared reflection. The imagery will serve as part of our baseline pipeline tracking for environmental monitoring and will be captured with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

In three days, at the time of overnight observation, we request the capture of thermal mapping data for the region of Tuluá, Colombia. This task involves a low priority importance request with flexible scheduling for opportunistic capture. The primary sensor utilized will be the TIR (Thermal Infrared) sensor to assess heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the area. This data collection aims to provide insights into thermal anomalies and heat emissions without the interference of daylight conditions or cloud cover, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of the region's thermal dynamics under the cover of darkness.

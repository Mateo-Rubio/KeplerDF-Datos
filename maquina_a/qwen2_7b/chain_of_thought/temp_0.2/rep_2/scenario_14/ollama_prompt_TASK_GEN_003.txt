On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun sets and the sky turns a deep shade of orange, our Earth-observation mission operator will embark on capturing thermal data over the region of Indonesia, specifically focusing on the city of Murung Raya. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, with flexible scheduling for opportunistic capture. The primary sensor to be utilized is the TIR (Thermal Infrared) instrument, which will provide us with critical information on heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the region. As we aim to capture this data in the evening, we understand that conditions may vary from day to day, but our commitment remains steadfast to deliver this thermal mapping for Murung Raya by the specified deadline.

In three days, at the time when the sun begins its descent and casts long shadows across the landscape of Indonesia's Kuantan Singingi region, we request the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data. This operational tasking aims to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries with precision. The deadline is strict, as it represents a critical moment for our analysis, ensuring that the data collected will provide invaluable insights into environmental conditions without any delay or alteration to the specified time frame.

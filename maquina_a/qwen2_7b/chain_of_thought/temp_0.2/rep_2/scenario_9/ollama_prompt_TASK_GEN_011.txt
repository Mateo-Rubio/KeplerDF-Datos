In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we are tasked with capturing thermal mapping data for the region of Norte, located in Slovenia. This mission is classified as a low priority importance request, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates opportunistic capture. The primary sensor to be utilized for this task is the TIR (Thermal Infrared) sensor, which will enable us to map heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the region overnight. This data collection is part of a non-essential background survey aimed at providing insights into thermal anomalies and heat emissions without compromising more critical operational tasks.

In three days, during the afternoon, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of Valle Castellana, Italy. This daylight photography will provide us with clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation for our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The mission is considered standard operational tasking, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to deliver thermal mapping data that captures heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, essential for detecting thermal anomalies and mapping heat emissions. This task is part of our routine monitoring efforts, focusing on the health of vegetation through multispectral analysis and assessing soil moisture levels using near-infrared reflection techniques. The deadline for delivering these data products is strict and must be adhered to without altering or extending it.

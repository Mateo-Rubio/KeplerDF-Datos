Given the stringent deadline of "the day after tomorrow" in the morning, our Earth-observation mission operator requests a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. This task aims to capture cloud-penetrating imagery for the region of Italia, specifically focusing on the city of Poggi del Sasso. As this request carries a low priority importance, it is designated as a flexible schedule "opportunistic capture" task, allowing for potential rescheduling or dropping at convenience. The primary objective is to gather data that will aid in understanding ground geometry and surface features without interference from atmospheric conditions, making this an essential background survey for our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

In four days, at the start of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of the Indonesian archipelago, focusing on the region of Kepulauan Meranti. This standard operational tasking aims to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries using the unique spectral signature provided by the near-infrared band. The imagery will serve as part of our baseline pipeline tracking for environmental monitoring and management purposes, ensuring that we maintain a comprehensive understanding of the region's ecological health and water resources.

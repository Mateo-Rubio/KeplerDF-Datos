In four days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun their nightly dance, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a near-infrared reflection snapshot of San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia. This nighttime mission is part of a standard operational tasking, representing normal pipeline tracking with fixed parameters that ensure the integrity of our baseline monitoring efforts. The primary sensor onboard will assess water body boundaries and soil moisture levels, providing invaluable data for environmental management and agricultural planning under the cover of darkness when conditions are most conducive to accurate near-infrared reflection mapping.

On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun has set and the stars have begun their nightly dance, our satellite constellation will be tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of Diano Borello, nestled in the beautiful country of Italia. This "overnight" mission is classified as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling to accommodate any necessary adjustments or rescheduling at convenience. The primary sensor involved will provide us with clear optical imagery, enabling us to document the landscape under the cover of darkness, capturing details that might be obscured by daylight. This non-essential background survey serves as a testbed tracking task, contributing to our ongoing efforts in background monitoring and data collection.

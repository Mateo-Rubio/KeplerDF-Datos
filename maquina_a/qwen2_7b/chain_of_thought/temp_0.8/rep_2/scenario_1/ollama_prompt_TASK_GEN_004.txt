Operating under a low priority importance designation, our task at hand requires an overnight capture of the day after tomorrow over the region in Colombia, specifically within the city of Ibagué. Targeting a 'vegetation health survey', our VNIR sensor aims to gather detailed multispectral data on the chlorophyll absorption levels across the area. This high-resolution optical snapshot seeks to provide us with a comprehensive view of the local vegetation's condition, facilitating critical insights for environmental management and agricultural planning in the region.

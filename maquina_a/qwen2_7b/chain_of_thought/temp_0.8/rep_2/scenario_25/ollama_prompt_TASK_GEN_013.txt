To capture a critical snapshot of daylight photography across the beautiful landscapes of Colombia, specifically in the region of Caraíra, an opportunistic survey mission has been allocated with the priority level of Low Importance. This task is classified as a 'background survey', wherein a flexible schedule allows for rescheduling or dropping the mission at our convenience. We aim to collect high-resolution optical imagery during the overnight period, four days ahead, to assess the heat signatures and conduct thermal mapping for environmental monitoring purposes. The primary sensor onboard will utilize its VIS capabilities to document the region's terrain and features clearly under the cover of darkness, providing us with invaluable data on the flora and fauna, as well as any potential anomalies that may require immediate attention in the future. This mission is part of our non-essential background filler tasks which contribute to the overall workflow update without compromising our baseline pipeline tracking activities.

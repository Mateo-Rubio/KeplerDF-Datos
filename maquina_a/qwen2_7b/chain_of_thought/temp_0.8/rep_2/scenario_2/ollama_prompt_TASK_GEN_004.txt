Given the deadline of "in four days", around mid-day, our Earth-observation mission aims to utilize a satellite constellation equipped with an active radar system (SAR) for an in-depth scan of Colombia's Nóvita city region. The primary objective is to gather detailed surface geometry information through cloud-penetrating radar scans, complemented by high-resolution optical snapshots taken during daylight hours for true-color visual documentation. This task holds low priority importance, acting as a "background survey" or "testbed tracking task", which can be flexibly scheduled according to operational requirements without compromising the deadline.

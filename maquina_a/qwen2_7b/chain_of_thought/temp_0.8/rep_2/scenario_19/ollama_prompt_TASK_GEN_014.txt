To meet the operational needs within the specified context, a routine monitoring task has been initiated for a three-day timeframe with a clear emphasis on the afternoon period. The primary sensor involved will be the VNIR instrument to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis, focusing on capturing daylight photography and collecting true-color visual documentation of the region known as "Norte" in the country of Italia. This task aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels within this area and deliver the results during the afternoon of the target day, ensuring that it represents standard baseline pipeline tracking status with fixed parameters.

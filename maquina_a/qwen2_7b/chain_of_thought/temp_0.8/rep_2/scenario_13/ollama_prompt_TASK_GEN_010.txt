In three days, at the onset of the morning period, we require a standard operational tasking involving multispectral vegetation analysis. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level across various ecosystems and calculating the NDVI index for the region of Puerto Rico in Colombia. The goal is to gather detailed information about the plant health and diversity, which will serve as an essential component of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters that represent normal operational status. This task is part of a routine monitoring effort aimed at understanding environmental changes and agricultural statuses in the area.

In accordance with our standard operational tasking procedures, we are planning for a "standard optical capture" tomorrow evening. Based in Colombia, our mission targets the city of Planadas using high-resolution cameras aboard one of our Earth-observation satellites. This captures the essence of clear daylight photography across the region's landscape as the sun sets. The operation is set with an absolute institutional priority, emphasizing the importance of this "critical payload capture" for monitoring environmental conditions and urban developments. We aim to deliver thermal mapping capabilities, providing insights into surface temperature profiles and heat emissions, which will contribute significantly to our baseline pipeline tracking and essential data collection efforts in Colombia.

In four days, overnight, our Earth-observation mission aims to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the region in Indonesia, specifically focusing on the city of Bulungan. This "true-color visual documentation" is for a low priority importance task, classified as a non-essential background survey or testbed tracking task. The imagery will serve as a "daylight photography" and "clear optical imagery", providing insights without the interference of cloud cover or daylight intensity issues typical during the daytime hours. As part of this baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, we are seeking to assess ground geometry using our active radar scan capabilities for cloud-penetrating capture and thermal mapping to detect surface temperature profiles or anomalies in heat emissions from the region. The final products will assist in understanding the thermal signatures of the landscape, particularly useful for environmental monitoring, agricultural assessment, and urban planning considerations during the overnight period when solar radiation levels are minimal. This task is scheduled as a flexible, opportunistic capture that may be dropped or rescheduled at convenience to ensure our operational schedule remains fluid and adaptable to other priorities within our portfolio.

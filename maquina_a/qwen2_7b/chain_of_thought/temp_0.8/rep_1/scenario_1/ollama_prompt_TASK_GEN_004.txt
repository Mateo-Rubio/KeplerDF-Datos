In Colombia, specifically within the bustling city of Ibagué, our Earth observation mission aims for a routine monitoring task scheduled for the evening of the day after tomorrow. This standard operational tasking involves capturing essential data using the VNIR sensor to perform a thorough assessment of the chlorophyll absorption levels across various vegetation zones. By deploying the VNIR sensor during the overnight period, we aim to gather invaluable insights into the health and distribution of local ecosystems, contributing to ongoing environmental research and management efforts.

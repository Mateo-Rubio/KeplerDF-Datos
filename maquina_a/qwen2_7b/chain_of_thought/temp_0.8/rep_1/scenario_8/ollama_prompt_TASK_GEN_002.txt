"Given the specified deadline of 'the day after tomorrow in the evening', our Earth-observation mission aims to capture a critical SAR (radar) scan of the region encompassed by the beautiful Italian city of Gordona, located in the picturesque country of Italia. The priority level designated for this task is Low Importance, which allows for a flexible schedule and categorizes it as a 'background survey'. The objective is to gather high-resolution SAR imagery on the day following the day after tomorrow when the sun sets in the evening, enabling a cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry without interference from daylight."

In response to our urgent request for a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis on "the day after tomorrow" during the overnight period in La Macarena, Colombia, we aim to capture a clear and detailed "true-color visual documentation" of the region's vegetation health using the VNIR sensor. This "non-essential background filler task" is scheduled to provide insights into the chlorophyll absorption levels without being constrained by day or weather conditions, allowing us to assess soil moisture and discriminate gradients during an opportune time for optimal data collection.

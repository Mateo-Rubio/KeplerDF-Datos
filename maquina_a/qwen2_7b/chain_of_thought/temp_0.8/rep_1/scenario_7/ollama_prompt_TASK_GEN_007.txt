As we approach "the day after tomorrow" at mid-day, our mission is to conduct a comprehensive environmental assessment of Valdivia, Colombia. This involves utilizing our Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) sensor for detailed radar imaging under daylight conditions and thermal mapping through the TIR sensor to identify anomalies and heat emissions. Concurrently, we will perform multispectral vegetation analysis with VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index, along with near-infrared (NIR) reflectance measurements for water body boundaries and soil moisture assessment. This operational request is executed under priority level 1 guidelines to ensure that despite its lower importance status, we adhere to strict deadlines and provide critical data products for environmental monitoring and baseline pipeline tracking in Colombia.

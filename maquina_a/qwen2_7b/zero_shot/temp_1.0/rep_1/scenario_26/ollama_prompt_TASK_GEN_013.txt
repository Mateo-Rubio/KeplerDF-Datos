In four days, with a strict delivery deadline set for the night time, we are required to capture a low priority importance VNIR-based task concerning vegetation assessment. Our objective is to evaluate chlorophyll absorption levels in the region of Betulia, Colombia. This "testbed tracking task" aims to gather data that will help discriminate gradients for soil moisture assessment and understand water body boundaries within the area, all by ensuring the products are ingested into our pipeline before the night falls on the target day.

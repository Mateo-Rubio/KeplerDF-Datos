In response to an operational directive of medium importance, our satellite constellation is tasked with conducting a standard operational tasking on Colombia's landscape. Specifically, we aim to capture a detailed radar scan across the region of Dolores using our SAR sensor. This active microwave surface imaging will serve as a cloud-penetrating capture, essential for understanding ground geometry and navigating through heavy weather conditions. The primary objective is to gather high-resolution data that will provide insights into the region's topography and infrastructure.

This mission is part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status. It involves capturing thermal mapping data to identify heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, crucial for detecting thermal anomalies and mapping heat emissions across the area. Additionally, we will assess chlorophyll absorption levels through multispectral vegetation analysis, calculating the NDVI index and surface reflectance to provide a comprehensive view of the vegetation health.

The task is scheduled for execution in three days during the morning hours. This deadline has been set by an upstream module and must be adhered to strictly. The request is framed as a standard operational tasking with normal priority, indicating that it is part of our routine monitoring activities. It is not considered an urgent operational execution or a binding contract requirement but rather a standard workflow update.

This satellite mission will contribute to our ongoing efforts in providing accurate and timely data for various applications, including environmental monitoring, urban planning, and disaster response. The data collected will be invaluable for decision-making processes and will help us maintain a robust baseline of information about the region's conditions.

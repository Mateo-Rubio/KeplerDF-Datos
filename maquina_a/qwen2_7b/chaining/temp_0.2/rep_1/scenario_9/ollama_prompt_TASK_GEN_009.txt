In preparation for an opportunistic background survey, we aim to capture a thermal mapping of the Ecuatorial region in Indonesia. This task involves assessing surface temperature profiles and identifying thermal anomalies using our TIR sensor. As part of this flexible schedule request with low priority importance, we seek to perform this non-essential background filler task around mid-day in three days, ensuring that it fits into our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This "testbed tracking task" will contribute to our understanding of heat emissions without imposing any urgent operational execution or contractual requirements.

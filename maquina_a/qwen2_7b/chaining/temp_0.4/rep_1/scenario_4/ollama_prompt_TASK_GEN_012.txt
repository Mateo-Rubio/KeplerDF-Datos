In three days in the morning, we request a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography of Suka Maju, Indonesia. This true-color visual documentation is part of our opportunistic background survey task, which can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if needed. The imagery will serve as a clear optical record to capture the landscape and urban environment of this region. We aim to observe the area using our VIS sensor to provide detailed information on the surroundings without any interference from cloud cover or weather conditions.

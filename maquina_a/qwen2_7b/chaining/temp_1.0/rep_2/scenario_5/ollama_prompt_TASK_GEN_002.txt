With daylight photography and true-color visual documentation capabilities, this task aims to capture the essence of the region's landscape through a high-resolution optical snapshot.

Considering the operational guidelines for sensor interpretation, our objective is framed to emphasize the unique aspect of SAR technology in observing the target area under various weather conditions. Our aim is to gather data in the morning on "the day after tomorrow," when this task becomes available as our upper limit for data availability has been already resolved by an upstream module.

As this request falls under low priority importance, we acknowledge that there might be opportunities to adjust scheduling for more flexibility or reschedule if necessary. This tracking task is recognized as a non-essential background filler task that does not require immediate attention and can be prioritized based on operational availability, ensuring it is seamlessly integrated into our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

Conduct a low priority importance, opportunistic capture of high-resolution optical imagery for a daylight photography and clear optical documentation task in Pontagna, Italia. The mission will involve a flexible schedule with a deadline set as "in four days overnight" as part of an opportune testbed tracking task. We aim to obtain thermal mapping insights through the process to identify surface temperature profiles or heat anomalies that might occur overnight. The imagery collection is essential for evaluating chlorophyll absorption levels in the vegetation for the NDVI index and surface reflectance calculations without any rush in timeline constraints.

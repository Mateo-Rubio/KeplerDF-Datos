To achieve an insightful analysis for environmental monitoring purposes, we aim to capture a nighttime infrared reflection of Murung Raya, Indonesia's landscape. This low-priority importance task requires us to document the unique signature of water bodies and assess soil moisture levels without the constraints of daylight conditions. Expecting to receive these valuable data within the allocated three days overnight timeframe, our objective is to contribute to a broader background survey, which can serve as a testbed tracking task for ongoing research without interrupting more critical operational schedules. This 'NIR' sensor-driven endeavor will not only map water body boundaries and estimate soil moisture but also provide crucial insights into the thermal dynamics of the region under study.

In anticipation of capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography and true-color visual documentation in three days around mid-day, our Earth-observation mission operator requests to observe Ráquira, Colombia. This "low priority importance" task will be executed with flexibility as a non-essential background filler, allowing for opportunistic scheduling. The primary sensor, VIS, aims to deliver clear optical imagery that showcases the vibrant landscapes of this picturesque city, providing insights into its urban and natural environments without any technical system tags or coordinate arrays. This request is part of a larger baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We expect this mission to contribute to our understanding of Colombia's environmental conditions under natural daylight conditions.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This involves a commercial tasking to capture high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and true-color visual documentation using multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over the region of Prati di Tivo in Italia. The data must be ready by in four days in the morning at the latest.

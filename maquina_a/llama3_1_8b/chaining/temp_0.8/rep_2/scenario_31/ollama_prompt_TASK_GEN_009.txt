Obtain a high-resolution optical snapshot of Malaysia's Kabong region during nighttime hours in four days overnight. This task is an absolute institutional priority and a binding contract requirement for a critical payload capture, involving mandatory directives for urgent operational execution and un-preemptable mission lock. The objective is to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance of the region's vegetation.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This involves a commercial tasking to capture high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography using multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over the region of Rokan Hilir, Indonesia. The mission allocation authority and asset contractual value dictate this as a standard operational tasking with an urgent operational execution and un-preemptable mission lock, requiring us to deliver the dataset in three days in the morning.

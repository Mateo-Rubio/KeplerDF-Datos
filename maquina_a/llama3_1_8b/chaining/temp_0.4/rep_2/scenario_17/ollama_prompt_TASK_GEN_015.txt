Obtain a high-resolution near-infrared reflection image of the water bodies surrounding Kepulauan Meranti in Indonesia for soil moisture assessment and boundary mapping. This is a standard operational tasking, part of a routine monitoring setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data should be captured using a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The task has an absolute institutional priority and is a binding contract requirement involving a critical payload capture. The dataset must be delivered in four days in the morning.

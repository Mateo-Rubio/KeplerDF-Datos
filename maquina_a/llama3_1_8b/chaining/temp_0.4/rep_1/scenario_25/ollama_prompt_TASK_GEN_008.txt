We require a standard operational tasking for a commercial satellite constellation to capture high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis data in the region of Prati di Tivo, Italia. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance using the VNIR sensor. This task is part of a routine monitoring setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. We need the data to be captured in four days in the morning.

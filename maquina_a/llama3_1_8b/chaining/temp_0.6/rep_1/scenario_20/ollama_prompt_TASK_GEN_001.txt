We require a standard operational tasking for commercial satellite imaging as part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The task involves a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR technology to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia, which is a critical region for monitoring crop health and agricultural productivity. This is a standard operational tasking with medium importance, and the data should be delivered in three days overnight.

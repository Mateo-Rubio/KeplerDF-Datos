We require a routine monitoring tasking for commercial satellite data collection to be performed in three days around mid-day. The objective is to capture high-resolution near-infrared reflection imagery for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment over Pavullo nel Frignano, a town located in the region of Emilia-Romagna, Italy. This standard operational tasking is part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, and it should be treated as a standard workflow update.

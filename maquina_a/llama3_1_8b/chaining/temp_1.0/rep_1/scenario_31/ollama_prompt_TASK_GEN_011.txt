We require a flexible low-priority importance task for opportunistic capture of thermal mapping data using an active heat signature detection method to identify temperature anomalies and surface heat emissions at Radicondoli in Italia. The goal is to collect high-resolution thermal data for daylight photography and true-color visual documentation, capturing the temperature profile of the region. This is a non-essential background filler task that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience, treating it as a background survey to be executed with flexible schedule in the evening the day after tomorrow.

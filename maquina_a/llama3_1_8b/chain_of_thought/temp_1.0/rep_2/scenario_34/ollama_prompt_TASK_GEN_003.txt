STAGE 1 - REASONING

a) The exact string in 'target_day' is "tomorrow".

b) The exact string in 'target_diurnal_period' is "in the evening".

c) The single word that appears literally in the string for 'target_diurnal_period' is "evening".

d) The deadline is specified as tomorrow in the morning, so I am not shifting the deadline to an adjacent part of the day. 

The temporal parameters represent a strict operational deadline to deliver the data products, and this does not represent the orbital capture window.

The 'target_day' value is a fixed date ("tomorrow") and cannot be altered or extended. The 'target_diurnal_period' value specifies a particular time within that day ("in the evening"), and we are capturing this at a fixed operational parameter, without altering the original deadline.

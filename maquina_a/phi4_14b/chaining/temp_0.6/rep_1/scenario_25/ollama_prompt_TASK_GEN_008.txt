We have a standard operational tasking that involves routine monitoring of Prati di Tivo, Italy, as part of our baseline pipeline tracking. The mission requires a multispectral vegetation analysis utilizing VNIR technology to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. This task is set with fixed parameters to ensure it aligns with our normal operational status and workflow update requirements. Please ensure that this data capture aligns with our delivery deadline, which is in four days in the morning.

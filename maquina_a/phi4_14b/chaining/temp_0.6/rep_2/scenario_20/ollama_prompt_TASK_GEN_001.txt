We have a standard operational tasking to address involving the region of San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters for routine monitoring. Our objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR technology, aiming to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index to evaluate surface reflectance. The data should be captured by in three days overnight, ensuring timely delivery and integration into our ongoing environmental assessments.

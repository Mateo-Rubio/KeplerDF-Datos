We are submitting a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring of the vegetation health in Puerto Rico, Colombia. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and involves using the multispectral capabilities of the VNIR sensor to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index over this region. The task is set with fixed parameters indicating it as a normal operational status within our workflow update requirements. Data availability must be ensured by in three days in the morning.

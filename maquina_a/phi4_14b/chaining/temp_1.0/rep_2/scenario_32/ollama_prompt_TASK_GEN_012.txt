We have a standard operational tasking for a baseline pipeline tracking mission focused on the city of Murung Raya in Indonesia. This mission involves acquiring near-infrared reflection data, specifically aimed at assessing soil moisture levels and delineating water body boundaries to help discriminate gradient variations. Given its designation as a medium importance task, this is part of our routine monitoring with fixed parameters representing our normal operational status, following our standard workflow update protocol. The data must be delivered in four days overnight to maintain the regularity of our analysis cycles.

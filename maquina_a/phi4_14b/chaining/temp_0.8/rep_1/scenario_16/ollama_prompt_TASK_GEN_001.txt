We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner to conduct routine monitoring of the Santander de Quilichao region in Colombia. This baseline pipeline tracking setup involves capturing multispectral vegetation analysis data. Specifically, we aim to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index to understand surface reflectance. As part of the normal operational status, this task adheres to our fixed parameters for workflow updates. Please ensure that the imagery is acquired in three days in the evening, as this represents the upper limit for data availability.

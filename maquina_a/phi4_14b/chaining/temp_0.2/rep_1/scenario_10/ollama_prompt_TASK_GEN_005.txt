We have a standard operational tasking for a near-infrared reflection mission over the region of Norte. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Our objective is to conduct water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment in this area. We aim to discriminate gradients effectively using the NIR capabilities of our satellite constellation. Please ensure that data capture is scheduled for the day after tomorrow in the morning, as this is the upper limit for data availability.

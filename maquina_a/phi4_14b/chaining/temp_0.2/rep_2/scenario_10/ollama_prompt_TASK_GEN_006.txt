We have a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring in Simabur, Indonesia. This involves a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters to ensure normal operational status. The mission is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors. Our goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. This task is part of our regular workflow update, and we need the data delivered in three days in the morning.

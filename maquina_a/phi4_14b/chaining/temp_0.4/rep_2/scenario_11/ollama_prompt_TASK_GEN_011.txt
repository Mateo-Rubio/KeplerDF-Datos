We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over Tagliole, Italy. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for the region. The task involves capturing clear optical imagery during daylight hours using VNIR sensors. Please ensure that this data is available in three days overnight, as it aligns with our normal operational status and workflow update requirements.

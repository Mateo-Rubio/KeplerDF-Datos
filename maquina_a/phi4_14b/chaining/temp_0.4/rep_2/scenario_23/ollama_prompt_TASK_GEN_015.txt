We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial mission to monitor the Kepulauan Meranti region in Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Our objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors, focusing on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index for surface reflectance. The data collection should be completed in four days overnight, aligning with our workflow update requirements.

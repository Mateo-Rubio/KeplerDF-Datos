We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation to conduct an active radar scan over Giugliano in Campania, Italy. This mission involves using microwave surface imaging to achieve cloud-penetrating capture, which is crucial for mapping ground geometry regardless of weather conditions. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and will adhere to fixed parameters, ensuring a routine monitoring update within our workflow. The data must be delivered in three days around mid-day to maintain our operational schedule.

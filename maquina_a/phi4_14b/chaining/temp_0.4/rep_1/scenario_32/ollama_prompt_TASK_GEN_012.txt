We have a standard operational tasking for a near-infrared reflection mission over Murung Raya, Indonesia. This task is part of our routine monitoring efforts, focusing on water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment to understand the environmental gradients in the region. As this is a commercial tasking within our baseline pipeline tracking setup, we have fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data delivery deadline for this mission is set to be completed "in four days overnight," ensuring that all necessary information is available by then.

We have an absolute institutional priority to capture critical thermal data over Povici di Sotto in Italy. This mission is a binding contract requirement involving a mandatory directive for urgent operational execution with an un-preemptable mission lock. The objective is to conduct thermal mapping, capturing a detailed surface temperature profile and detecting any thermal anomalies in the area. This high-importance task must be completed with an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to ensure cloud-penetrating capture, even under challenging weather conditions. The data must be delivered by in four days in the morning.

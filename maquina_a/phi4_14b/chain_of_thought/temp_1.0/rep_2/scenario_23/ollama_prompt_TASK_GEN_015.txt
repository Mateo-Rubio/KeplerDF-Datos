We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis in the Kepulauan Meranti region of Indonesia. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index using VNIR sensors. This task forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking, ensuring that we maintain a normal operational status within our workflow update schedule. The data collection must occur "in four days" during the "overnight" period, adhering strictly to the delivery deadline.

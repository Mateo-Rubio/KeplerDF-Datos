We have a low priority importance request scheduled for capturing an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging over France, specifically in the region of Norte. This task involves obtaining cloud-penetrating captures to analyze ground geometry effectively. Due to its flexible nature and non-essential status, this observation is considered a background survey that should be opportunistically executed or rescheduled as convenience permits. We aim to complete this data capture in four days, with the satellite operation occurring in the morning. The goal is not only to achieve precise imaging but also to integrate this as part of our broader exploratory initiatives.

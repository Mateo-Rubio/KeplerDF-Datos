We are planning a low-priority importance observation task with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture in the region around Norte. The mission is classified as a non-essential background filler task, allowing operators to drop or reschedule at convenience. This is considered a background survey activity where we aim to take a high-resolution optical snapshot using VIS sensors. The goal is to achieve daylight photography that provides clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation. The deadline for delivering the data products from this observation is set to be "in four days, in the morning."

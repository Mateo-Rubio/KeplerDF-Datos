This mission request constitutes an absolute institutional priority with a binding contract requirement for the acquisition of high-resolution imagery over Isola del Gran Sasso d'Italia, located in Italy. As part of our critical payload capture mandate, we demand an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to provide cloud-penetrating capture during adverse weather conditions and obtain detailed ground geometry mapping. The task must be conducted tomorrow overnight and is considered an un-preemptable mission lock. It is crucial that this directive be executed with utmost urgency to ensure compliance with our operational objectives.

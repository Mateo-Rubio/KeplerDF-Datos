This mission request pertains to an absolute institutional priority involving a critical payload capture for the region of Kabong, Malaysia. We require a VNIR sensor-based multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. This task is part of a binding contract requirement and must be executed with an urgent operational execution, ensuring an un-preemptable mission lock. The data capture is scheduled for in four days during the overnight period, emphasizing the necessity for timely delivery of the data products within this strict operational deadline.

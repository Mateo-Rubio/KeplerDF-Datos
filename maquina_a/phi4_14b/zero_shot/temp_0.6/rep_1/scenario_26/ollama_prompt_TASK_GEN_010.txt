We have a standard operational tasking for an active radar scan over Puerto López, Colombia. This mission involves using microwave surface imaging to achieve cloud-penetrating capture, crucial for understanding ground geometry in this area. It's part of our baseline pipeline tracking and represents a routine monitoring effort with fixed parameters.

The data products are needed by the evening in three days at the latest, ensuring they are ingested into the pipeline before that time. This timeframe aligns with our normal operational status and workflow update requirements.

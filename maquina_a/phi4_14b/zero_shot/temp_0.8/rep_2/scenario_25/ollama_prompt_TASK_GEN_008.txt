We have a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring in Prati di Tivo, Italy. This involves conducting a multispectral vegetation analysis using a VNIR sensor to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index of the area. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking, we require this data to be captured with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The delivery deadline for this task is set for in four days, in the morning, ensuring the products are ingested into the pipeline before that time.

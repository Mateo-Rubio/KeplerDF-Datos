In accordance with the high institutional priority assigned to this operation, we have a binding contract requirement for an absolute optical snapshot of Chivatá, Colombia. This request specifically targets multispectral vegetation analysis within three days during the afternoon window to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI). The task is set as part of a critical payload capture mission directive aimed at enhancing our baseline pipeline tracking for environmental monitoring, with an urgent operational execution deadline firmly in place.

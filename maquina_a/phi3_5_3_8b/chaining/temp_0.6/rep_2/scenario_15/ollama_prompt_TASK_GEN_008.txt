In accordance with our routine monitoring requirements for multispectral vegetation analysis in Piacenza, Italy, we are tasked to utilize near-infrared reflection capabilities of the satellite sensors. The aim is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a surface reflectance NDVI index as part of our normal operational status. This standard operational tasking aligns with our commercial data collection efforts, enssuring we capture high-resolution optical snapshots for true-color visual documentation during the specified diurnal period in three days' time, specifically in the morning hours.

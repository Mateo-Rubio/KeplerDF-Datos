This is a non-essential background filler task aimed at obtaining multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance. The focus will be on Calamar, Colombia, for which high-resolution optical snapshot objectives are set during daylight photography in three days' time, specifically in the evening hours, as resolved by an upstream module. These operations are to be completed with a flexible schedule; operators may drop or reschedule at their convenience without impacting core mission activities.

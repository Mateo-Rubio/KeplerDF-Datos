The mission planner is tasked with organizing an automated satellite pass over Indonesia's equatorial city for a critical radar survey. Utilizing active microwave surface imaging, the objective is to penetrate any cloud cover and accurately capture ground geometry under challenging weather conditions. Given its low operational priority, this tasking can be arranged with flexibility in scheduling; however, it must align with data availability by "the day after tomorrow during the afternoon." This pass should serve as an opportunistic filler within our broader environmental monitoring initiative, and if necessary, may need to be dropped or rescheduled at convenience.

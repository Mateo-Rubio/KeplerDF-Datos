This is a standard operational tasking to carry out multispectral vegetation analysis in Pavullo nel Frignano, Italy. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate surface reflectance using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This routine monitoring will be integrated into our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to complete this task within three days around mid-day, as per data availability resolution by an upstream module. The execution of this request is essential for maintaining the continuity and integrity of our current workload without interfering with other critical operations already in place.

In accordance with our routine monitoring protocols, we are tasked to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis focusing on near-infrared reflection in the city of Bangka Selatan, within Indonesia. This standard operational tasking is scheduled for three days hence and specifically targets early morning sessions. The objective here is to assess surface reflectance which will contribute significantly towards calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)—a key indicator of vegetation health and density in our baseline pipeline tracking system.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am directing a standard operational tasking to be executed on Monterrey in Colombia. The objective is multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI). This routine monitoring will enhance our understanding of surface reflectance and contribute to environmental studies critical for agricultural planning. The task is scheduled for an overnight delivery deadline in three days, ensuring data freshness without compromising on the quality required for subsequent analysis phases.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am directing a standard operational task for our automated satellite constellation planner. We aim to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis in the city of Norte within Italy to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate surface reflectance indices using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking, scheduled for three days from now during the afternoon hours. The data collected will be instrumental in understanding vegetation health and dynamics over time within this specific geographic context.

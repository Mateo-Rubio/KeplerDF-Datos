As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasked to schedule a SAR (active radar scan) for our automated satellite constellation planner. The objective is to conduct microwave surface imaging in the city of Belaga within Malaysia's dense rainforests and mountainous terrain. This operation forms an essential part of our routine monitoring, aimed at capturing cloud-penetrating images that will contribute significantly to our understanding of the area's topography and vegetation patterns under various weather conditions. Given its critical payload capture for environmental research and resource management, this tasking is set as a standard operational assignment within our baseline pipeline tracking system with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data must be delivered by the day after tomorrow overnight to ensure timely analysis and reporting.

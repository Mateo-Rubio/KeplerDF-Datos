We are tasked with conducting a standard operational tasking to carry out multispectral vegetation analysis in Solok, Indonesia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance measurements. This routine monitoring will be part of our baseline pipeline tracking system with fixed parameters representing a normal operational status. The data collection window has been set for four days from now, specifically in the morning hours, to ensure optimal light conditions for accurate imagery capture and analysis.

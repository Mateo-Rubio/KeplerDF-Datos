As part of our Earth-observation efforts for upcoming climate impact studies in Colombia, we're seeking to initiate a high-priority mission targeting thermal mapping over Puerto López. The focus is on capturing detailed heat signature data and constructing a precise surface temperature profile to pinpoint potential thermal anomalies within the region. Given that this task stands at priority level 1, it will serve as supplementary information for our broader climate monitoring objectives rather than a critical payload capture or mandatory directive. While not urgent operational execution is required beyond "the day after tomorrow in the morning", we encourage operators to consider this background survey as opportunistic and therefore flexible in scheduling, with an understanding that it can be adjusted based on operational convenience if necessary.

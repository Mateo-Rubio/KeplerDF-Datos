We are initiating a standard operational tasking for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging in the vicinity of Ecuador's Equator city to penetrate through cloud cover and accurately capture ground geometry. This routine monitoring is set to be executed "the day after tomorrow around mid-day," as part of our baseline pipeline tracking system with fixed parameters, ensuring consistent workflow updates. The data collected during this mission will contribute significantly to our comprehension of the terrain'selevation and landscape structure under varied weather conditions.

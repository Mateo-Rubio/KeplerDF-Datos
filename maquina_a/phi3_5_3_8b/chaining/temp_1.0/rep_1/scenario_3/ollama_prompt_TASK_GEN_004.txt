The Canelo Hills region in Colombia requires a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection. This routine monitoring is scheduled to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index, crucial for understanding surface reflectance patterns. We aim to accomplish this high-resolution optical snapshot during daylight photography in the morning of the day after tomorrow. It's a typical element within our baseline pipeline tracking setup that helps maintain normal operational status and workflow updates for environmental monitoring purposes.

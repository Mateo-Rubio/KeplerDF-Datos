Initiate a high-priority institutional priority for a mandatory directive to capture multispectral vegetation analysis in Greve in Chianti, Italia. The aim is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) alongside surface reflectance using non-visible near-infrared reflection capabilities of our satellite assets. This directive demands a high-resolution optical snapshot for true-color visual documentation during an overnight operation, enssuring data availability within four days to meet the fixed parameters set out in this urgent operational execution plan.

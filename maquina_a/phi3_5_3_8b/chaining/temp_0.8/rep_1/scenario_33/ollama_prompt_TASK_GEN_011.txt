The mission at hand is to schedule an opportunistic SAR scan in Tesis, Italy. Given its low priority importance, this task will be carried out with a flexible approach and can be adjusted as per convenience. The objective is to obtain an active radar scan for cloud-penetrating capture, which will assist in understanding the terrain's structure even under less than ideal visibility conditions. This tasking falls outside of our standard operational window but can be done at any time before the day after tomorrow around mid-day as we have secured a delivery deadline for these data sets by that precise moment.

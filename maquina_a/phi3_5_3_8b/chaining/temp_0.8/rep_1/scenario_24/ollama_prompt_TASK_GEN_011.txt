As a low priority importance task within our Earth-observation mission operator's schedule for opportunistic capture, we are setting forth an appeal to utilize near-infrared reflection capabilities of our automated satellite constellation planner. The focus is on water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment in the region known as Campli, located within Italy. This initiative serves not only for environmental monitoring but also aids in agricultural resource management by discriminating between various soil moisture gradients—a crucial aspect of our baseline pipeline tracking during routine operations. We aim to capture these near-infrared reflections specifically four days hence, in the morning timeframe, adhering strictly to this flexible schedule as an added measure for comprehensive water body delineation and soil moisture analysis.

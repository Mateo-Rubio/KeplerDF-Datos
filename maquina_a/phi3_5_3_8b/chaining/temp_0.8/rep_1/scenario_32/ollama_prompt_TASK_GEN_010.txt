As part of our routine monitoring to ensure a comprehensive agricultural assessment, we are tasking an automated satellite constellation planner with the following directive: Within four days overnight, deploy VNIR sensors for multispectral vegetation analysis in San Vicente del Caguán, Colombia. This operation, due its low priority importance, is to be scheduled flexibly and can be rescheduled at convenience without impacting our standard operational flow. The specific aim here is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level across various crops and calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), alongside surface reflectance measurements, which will greatly assist in evaluating plant health and vitality under current agricultural conditions.

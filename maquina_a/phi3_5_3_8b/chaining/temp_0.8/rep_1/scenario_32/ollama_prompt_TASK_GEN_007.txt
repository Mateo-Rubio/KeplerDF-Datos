As part of our routine monitoring to maintain an up-to-date understanding of environmental changes in Colombia's infrastructure, we require a SAR (active radar scan) focused on the city of Tarazá. Given its critical importance within our low priority scheme but acknowledging the flexible schedule at hand, this request shall be undertaken as opportunistic capture aimed for three days overnight. By deploying microwave surface imaging capabilities of the satellite constellation planner system, we anticipate capturing cloud-penetrating data that may reveal ground geometry and contribute valuable insights into regional geomorphology and urban development patterns. This background survey will enrich our repository with complementary datasets to those acquired under higher operational importance conditions.

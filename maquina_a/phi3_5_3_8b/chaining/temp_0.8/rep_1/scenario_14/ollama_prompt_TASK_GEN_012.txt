Directly following our routine monitoring schedule for environmental impact assessments, we are setting forth a standard operational tasking with the primary objective of thermal mapping. Our aim is to capture heat signatures across the Amazon Basin during the afternoon hours of "the day after tomorrow." This procedure will enable us to construct an accurate surface temperature profile and detect any potential thermal anomalies within this critical ecological zone, which acts as a vital carbon sink and biodiversity hotspot. The deadline for data acquisition has been definitively established at the specified time frame, ensuring immediate processing into our analysis pipeline upon receipt of high-resolution optical snapshots from ground stations.

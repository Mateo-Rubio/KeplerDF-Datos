This is an urgent operational execution for a mandatory directive requiring SAR to conduct an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging in Sanggau, Indonesia. The objective is critical payload capture focused on cloud-penetrating capabilities and detailed ground geometry analysis within the region. As this task holds low priority importance with a flexible schedule, it can be postponed or rescheduled at convenience without significant operational disruption. Nonetheless, to maintain our comprehensive Earth-observation data stream, we aim for execution "the day after tomorrow in the morning" deadline. This background survey task will fill an essential gap and aid in refining ongoing environmental monitoring efforts.

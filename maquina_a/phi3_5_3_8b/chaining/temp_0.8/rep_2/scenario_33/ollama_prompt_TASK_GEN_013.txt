We are setting forth an operational directive for a non-essential background filler task aimed at obtaining active radar scans using microwave surface imaging over the city of Puerto López in Colombia. This task, characterized by its low priority importance, is scheduled to proceed with a flexible schedule and should be considered as a secondary objective within our ongoing activities. It's essential for this operation that we capture clear ground geometry, especially given the potential challenges posed by weather conditions which may obstruct optical imaging modalities. We aim to conduct these radar scans in three days around mid-day with an understanding that while not time-critical, any data acquired will enhance our knowledge base of surface features and contribute to a more comprehensive environmental assessment when available.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasked to direct a specialized SAR sensor on our automated satellite constellation for active radar scanning in the Medio Baudó area of Colombia. This operation forms part of our routine monitoring and is scheduled as a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters set for tomorrow during the afternoon. The focus here is to achieve cloud-penetrating capture, which will allow us to pierce through heavy weather conditions and accurately map ground geometries without interference from atmospheric obstructions.

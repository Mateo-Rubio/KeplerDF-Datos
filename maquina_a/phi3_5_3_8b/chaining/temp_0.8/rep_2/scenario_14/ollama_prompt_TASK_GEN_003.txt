As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am issuing a task to our automated satellite constellation planner for the execution of a thermal mapping mission using Thermal Infrared (TIR) imaging. The objective is to capture a heat signature and construct a surface temperature profile in Murung Raya, Indonesia, which will contribute significantly to our understanding of regional thermal dynamics as part of environmental monitoring efforts. Given its low operational priority, this task can be accommodated on Murung Raya's flexible schedule; however, it must not conflict with higher-priority missions. We aim to complete the data acquisition by evening "the day after tomorrow".

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am directing a routine monitoring task for multispectral vegetation analysis in Pekanbaru, Indonesia. This standard operational tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and requires the satellite constellation to capture near-infrared reflection data by the day after tomorrow morning. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance analysis, enssuring our ongoing environmental monitoring efforts remain robust and informative.

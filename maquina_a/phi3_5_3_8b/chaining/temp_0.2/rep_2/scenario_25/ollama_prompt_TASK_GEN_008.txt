As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasking our automated satellite constellation planner with a standard operational tasking that requires us to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis in Prati di Tivo, Italy. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and will involve assessing chlorophyll absorption levels as well as calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance measurements. The objective here is to capture high-resolution optical snapshots during daylight photography, enssuring true-color visual documentation of the region's vegetative health and dynamics. This tasking must be executed within four days in the morning deadline as provided by our upstream module for data availability.

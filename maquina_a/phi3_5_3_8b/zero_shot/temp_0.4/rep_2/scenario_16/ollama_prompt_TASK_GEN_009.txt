The following task is a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis in Bukit Mabong, Malaysia. We aim to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) using near-infrared reflection capabilities of our satellite sensors. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data products are needed by the day after tomorrow during the afternoon at the latest, ensuring they are ingested into the processing pipeline in time for analysis and reporting.

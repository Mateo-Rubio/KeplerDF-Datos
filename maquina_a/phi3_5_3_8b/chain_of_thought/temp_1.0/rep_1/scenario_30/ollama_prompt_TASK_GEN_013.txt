In accordance with an absolute institutional priority for mission allocation, we hereby direct a high importance task to capture multispectral vegetation analysis in Colombia's Puerto Rico using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This directive is time-sensitive and requires the acquisition of data during the morning phase on "the day after tomorrow". The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels, calculate a Vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), and analyze surface reflectance, contributing significantly to our understanding of vegetative health and moisture gradients. This mission lock emphasizes the urgent operational execution required for this task within its strictly defined deadline without any alterations.

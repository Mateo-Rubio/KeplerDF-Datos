As operator for this Earth-observation task, we have scheduled a SAR active radar scan to be conducted in three days during the overnight timeframe. The aim is to execute an unprecedented microwave surface imaging operation from our satellite platform situated above Kubu Raya, Indonesia. Given its low operational priority and non-essential nature of this task within our standardized mission execution flow, it is set as a flexible opportunity for additional data capture without impacting high importance tasks scheduled closer to their deadlines. The result will aid in ground geometry analysis even during nocturnal hours when other sensors might not be operational due to environmental conditions or constraints on the spacecraft's power and bandwidth resources. However, this background survey task is subject to adjustment according to available system capacity and operator discretion at a later time.

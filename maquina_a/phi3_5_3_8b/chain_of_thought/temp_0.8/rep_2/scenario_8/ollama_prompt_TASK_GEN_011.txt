In four days, during the morning phase, we require a high-resolution optical snapshot for true-color visual documentation as part of our standard operational tasking in Arsiero, Italy. This routine monitoring is set within the multispectral vegetation analysis scope to assess chlorophyll absorption level and surface reflectance using near-infrared reflection techniques. The aim is consistent with baseline pipeline tracking where fixed parameters are monitored under normal operational conditions as a critical component of our environmental observation efforts.

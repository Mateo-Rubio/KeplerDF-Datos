To execute a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis with high-resolution optical snapshot capabilities, our satellite constellation planner must prepare an instrumental payload capture on "the day after tomorrow" during the early hours of morning light conditions. This baseline pipeline tracking setup will facilitate accurate assessment of chlorophyll absorption levels and calculation of surface reflectance to determine vegetation health in the vicinity of Ecuatorial, Indonesia. The task is scheduled as a routine part of our mission operations with fixed parameters representative of normal operational statuses.

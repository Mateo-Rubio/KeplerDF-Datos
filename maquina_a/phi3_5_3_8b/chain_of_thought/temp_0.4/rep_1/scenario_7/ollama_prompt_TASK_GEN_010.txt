In accordance with operational guidelines for a standard operational tasking, we are scheduled to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using high-resolution optical snapshot capabilities in three days during the morning hours over Pantanillo city within Colombia's region of interest. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

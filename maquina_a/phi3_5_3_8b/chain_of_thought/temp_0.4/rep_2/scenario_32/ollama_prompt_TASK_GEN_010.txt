In accordance with our standard operational tasking procedures for routine monitoring and baseline pipeline tracking, we seek to deploy a VNIR sensor-based mission over San Vicente del Caguán in Colombia within four days during the designated overnight period. This initiative is aimed at conducting multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) and surface reflectance, thereby facilitating a detailed understanding of local flora health and vigor. The task has been assigned as part of our ongoing efforts to maintain comprehensive environmental monitoring in alignment with institutional guidelines for data acquisition and analysis.

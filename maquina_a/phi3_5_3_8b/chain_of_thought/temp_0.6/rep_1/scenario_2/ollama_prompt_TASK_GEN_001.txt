As an operator for today's Earth-observation mission focusing on Colombia, specifically in Génova, we have scheduled an SAR sensor pass with the goal of conducting an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging. This task is crucial for capturing ground geometry and penetrating heavy weather conditions, thereby providing essential data that cannot be obtained through optical means alone. Given its low priority importance, this operation can be seen as a flexible schedule opportunity aimed at opportunistic capture. Please note that while the execution of this mission does not preclude other activities, it is advisable to drop or reschedule if circumstances necessitate due attention elsewhere.

This task involves conducting a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities during an overnight period in four days from today's date. The objective is to capture high-resolution optical snapshots that will allow us to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate surface reflectance, thereby evaluating vegetation health and density around Santa Rosa del Sur, Colombia. Given the absolute institutional priority of this mission allocation due to its critical payload capture requirement, it is imperative that we execute this urgently without any preemption or delay. The data obtained will be instrumental for our environmental monitoring initiatives, contributing significantly to our understanding and management of local ecosystems under changing climatic conditions.

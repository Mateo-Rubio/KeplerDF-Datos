In accordance with our operational guidelines for Earth-observation mission requests, I hereby submit a task request aimed at conducting multispectral vegetation analysis within Calamar, Colombia, which is located in close proximity to the city of Cartagena and near significant wetlands. This task, falling under low priority importance, requires us to gather data during evening hours over the course of three days starting today. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance analysis while discriminating gradients that may indicate environmental changes or stress within local flora. This background survey task can be rescheduled at the convenience of our operations team, as it serves to supplement rather than replace urgent institutional priorities.

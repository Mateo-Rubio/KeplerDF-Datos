As an Earth-observation mission operator, I submit an operational request for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging in Indonesia's city of Sanggau. This task has been identified as a low priority importance endeavor with the flexibility to be conducted at our discretion. The schedule is non-urgent and can be adjusted according to operational convenience, treating it as an opportunistic capture for background survey purposes. Specifically, we aim to conduct this SAR scan in Sanggau during the morning of the day after tomorrow. This will contribute to cloud-penetrating capture capabilities, enhancing our understanding of ground geometry and surface features under various weather conditions. The data obtained from this task is not time-critical but can provide valuable insights for long-term environmental monitoring and planning.

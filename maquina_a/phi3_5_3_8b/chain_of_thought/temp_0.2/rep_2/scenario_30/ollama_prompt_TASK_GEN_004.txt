In accordance with our Earth-observation mission requirements, we are requesting a SAR (active radar scan using microwave surface imaging) operation over Uribe, Colombia, which is scheduled for three days from now during the nighttime period. This task, being of low priority importance, can be adjusted or postponed at our convenience. The objective here is to capture cloud-penetrating images and map the ground geometry beneath heavy weather conditions, providing valuable data that complements our existing surveillance efforts without disrupting higher-priority missions.

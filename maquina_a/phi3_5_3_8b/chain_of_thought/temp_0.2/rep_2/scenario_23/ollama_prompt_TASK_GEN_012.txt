In accordance with our standard operational tasking schedule, we are setting up a routine monitoring mission for Malinau city in Indonesia. The objective is to perform multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) as well as surface reflectance, particularly during morning hours. This tasking aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup and is scheduled for "the day after tomorrow" in the morning. The data collected will contribute to our ongoing environmental monitoring efforts without altering or extending the strict operational deadline already established.

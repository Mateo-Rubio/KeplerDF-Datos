This is a standard operational tasking to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis in Norte, Italy. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This routine monitoring forms an integral part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to achieve this as efficiently as possible within tomorrow's morning window, ensuring that we have all necessary data available by then without delaying other mission priorities.

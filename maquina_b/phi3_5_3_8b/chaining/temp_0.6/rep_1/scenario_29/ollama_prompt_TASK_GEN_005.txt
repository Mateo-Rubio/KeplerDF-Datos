As a part of our routine monitoring for environmental changes in Norte, we're initiating an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to pierce through heavy cloud cover and capture the ground geometry with exceptional detail. This task is scheduled as low priority importance, allowing us some flexibility in planning around it. We aim to carry out this opportunity for opportunistic capture within our flexible schedule. The data collected will be instrumental for further analysis and has been set up to be delivered by the day after tomorrow evening at the latest.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am directing a specific operation to capture multispectral vegetation analysis in Venaco, France. This task is part of our routine monitoring efforts aimed at assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance measurements within three days from now in the morning. The data obtained will be instrumental to our ongoing environmental studies, contributing significantly to understanding plant health and vegetation dynamics over time.

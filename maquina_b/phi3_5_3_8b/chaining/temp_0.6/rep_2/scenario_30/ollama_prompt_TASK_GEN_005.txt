In accordance with our current low priority importance directive for opportunistic capture tasks, we seek to conduct a non-essential background survey. Specifically, this task involves the assessment of near-infrared reflection over Norte's water bodies and soil moisture gradients during an upcoming nighttime observation window, which is expected to be available "the day after tomorrow overnight". This endeavor will contribute valuable data for boundary mapping between aquatic environments and surrounding terrain. The results from this flexible schedule operation can enhance our understanding of local hydrological patterns when resources permit a postponement or rescheduling at convenience.

The Earth-observation mission operator is issuing a low priority importance task for thermal mapping of Serravalle di Chienti in Italy. The goal involves capturing the heat signature and surface temperature profile to detect any potential thermal anomalies, using true-color thermal imaging. This background survey should be accommodated with a flexible schedule but must meet the data availability deadline "the day after tomorrow in the morning". If resources are limited or if there's an overlapping need for urgent tasks, this operation can be dropped or rescheduled at our convenience without impacting critical missions.

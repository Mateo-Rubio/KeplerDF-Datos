As an Earth-observation mission operator with a clear priority to keep our baseline pipeline tracking on course, we are issuing a standard operational tasking for the Ituango area in Colombia. In three days during the morning hours, it is essential to capture multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This routine monitoring will assist us with assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating surface reflectance, contributing valuable data towards our environmental research efforts without disrupting higher priority missions.

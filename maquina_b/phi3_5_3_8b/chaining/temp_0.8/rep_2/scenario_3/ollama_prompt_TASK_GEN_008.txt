This is to establish a low priority importance task within our Earth-observation satellite fleet. We seek an opportunistic capture of multispectral vegetation analysis in the town of Gazzola, located in Italy. The focus will be on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) to better understand surface reflectance properties during overnight hours within three days from today's date. This background survey task can be rescheduled or dropped at our convenience, as it forms a supplementary data stream contributing to our broader environmental monitoring objectives without imposing urgent operational execution demands.

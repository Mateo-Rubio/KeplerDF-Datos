In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols for routine monitoring, we are currently planning a multispectral vegetation analysis in Santander de Quilichao, Colombia. This initiative is scheduled to commence within three days in the evening and will be executed as part of our regular baseline pipeline tracking activities. The aim is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels across various green spaces and calculate surface reflectance indices, thus contributing essential data for environmental studies and ecosystem monitoring efforts. This work aligns with our established fixed parameters representing a continuation of our dedication to maintaining an up-to-date observational dataset critical to both scientific research and operational planning.

In accordance with our routine monitoring efforts, we aim to schedule a SAR (active radar scan) using microwave surface imaging over the equatorial region of Indonesia. This operation is intended for cloud-penetrating capture and precise ground geometry analysis, particularly in areas where natural phenomena or unexpected events may occur. As this constitutes a low priority importance task with an opportunistic approach to data acquisition, we have a flexible schedule in mind. The targeted timeframe for completing this survey is within three days in the evening—an upper limit that has already been established by our upstream module. Please be aware that should circumstances change, or if resources are needed elsewhere, this mission can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience without any penalty to operational efficiency. Treat this request as a non-essential background filler task within the broader spectrum of environmental monitoring and situational awareness our satellite constellation provides.

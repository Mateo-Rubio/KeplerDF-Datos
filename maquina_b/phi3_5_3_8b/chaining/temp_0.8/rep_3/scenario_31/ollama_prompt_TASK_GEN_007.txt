In three days during the afternoon, we're tasked with executing a routine monitoring mission targeting thermal mapping for surface temperature profiles in Mapiripán, Colombia. This standard operational tasking involves capturing TIR (thermal imagery) to detect and document any heat anomalies on the ground which could indicate environmental changes or potential issues needing further investigation. By understanding these heat signatures, we can better comprehend local thermal dynamics and contribute valuable data to our continuous surveillance efforts over Mapiripán's landscape.

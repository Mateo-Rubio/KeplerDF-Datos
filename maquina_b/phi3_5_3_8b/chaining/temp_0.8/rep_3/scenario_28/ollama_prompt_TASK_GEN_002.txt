In accordance with our objective to enhance agricultural efficiency in Norte through detailed multispectral vegetation analysis, we are initiating a standard operational tasking for tomorrow during the afternoon. This involves deploying an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging over the region known for its diverse crop patterns and rich farmlands to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index, providing us with surface reflectance metrics crucial for our environmental monitoring efforts. The data collected will contribute significantly to understanding plant health and inform necessary agricultural interventions.

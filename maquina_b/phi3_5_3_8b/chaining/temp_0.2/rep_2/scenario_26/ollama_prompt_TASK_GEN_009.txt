As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasking our automated satellite constellation planner with a standard operational tasking that involves conducting thermal mapping over the equatorial region of Indonesia. The objective is to capture a heat signature and create a surface temperature profile within this biodiverse area known for its unique ecological dynamics. This mission will provide critical insights into local environmental conditions, particularly in understanding how these regions respond thermally during different times of daylight. We aim to complete the thermal mapping task using active radar scanning with microwave surface imaging capabilities within four days, specifically targeting morning hours for optimal data acquisition. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and will contribute significantly to our environmental studies portfolio.

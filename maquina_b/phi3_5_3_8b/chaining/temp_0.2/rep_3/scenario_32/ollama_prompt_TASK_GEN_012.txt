As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am tasking a routine monitoring operation for Murung Raya in Indonesia. The objective is to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities over the course of four days, specifically targeting nighttime operations. This standard operational tasking forms an integral part of our baseline pipeline tracking and will assist in assessing chlorophyll absorption levels as well as calculating surface reflectance indices for Murung Raya's vegetation cover. The data collected is expected to contribute significantly to our understanding of the area’s ecological dynamics under varying nocturnal conditions, with a strict deadline set within four days overnight.

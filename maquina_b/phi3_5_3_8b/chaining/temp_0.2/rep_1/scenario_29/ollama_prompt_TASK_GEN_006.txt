As an Earth-observation mission operator, I am issuing a task for our automated satellite constellation planner to conduct a thermal mapping of the Bontang region in Indonesia. This operation is aimed at capturing heat signatures and creating surface temperature profiles within three days in the evening deadline. Given its low priority importance, this request can be scheduled flexibly; however, it remains an essential part of our routine monitoring to detect any thermal anomalies that may arise. The data obtained will contribute significantly to understanding local environmental conditions and potential heat-related issues.

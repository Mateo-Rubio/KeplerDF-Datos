This task is scheduled as a low priority importance request, which allows for some flexibility in timing due to its non-essential nature. We aim to capture this data opportunity during the afternoon of the day after tomorrow when conditions are expected to be optimal for such observations. Operators may drop or reschedule at their convenience if necessary, as we treat this as a background survey task within our broader research initiative.

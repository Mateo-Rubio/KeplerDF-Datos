In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols, I am directing a routine monitoring mission for Santander de Quilichao in Colombia to be executed. The objective is to carry out multispectral vegetation analysis using the visible near-infrared spectrum within three days from now during the evening hours. This will facilitate an assessment of chlorophyll absorption levels and surface reflectance, crucial for calculating a Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI). The data collected is expected to contribute significantly to our environmental monitoring efforts without disrupting ongoing baseline pipeline tracking operations.

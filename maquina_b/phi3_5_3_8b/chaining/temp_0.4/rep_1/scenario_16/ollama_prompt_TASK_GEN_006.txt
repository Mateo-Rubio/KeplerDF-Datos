In three days overnight, we require a standard operational tasking to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis in Mulya Bakti, Indonesia. The focus is on assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance evaluation. This routine monitoring will ensure our baseline pipeline tracking remains up-to-date with normal operational statuses of vegetative health in this region, contributing to environmental studies and agricultural planning efforts.

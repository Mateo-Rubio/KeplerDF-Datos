Initiate a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis in Bangka Selatan, Indonesia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) using near-infrared reflection capabilities within three days at morning hours. This routine monitoring will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking by providing essential data on surface reflectance and vegetation health in the region, enssuring a comprehensive understanding of environmental conditions for future reference.

In accordance with our Earth-observation mission objectives, we are seeking to initiate a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis in the region of Rumah Budak, Malaysia. The primary goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate surface reflectance using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This routine monitoring will be conducted as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, enssuring a continuous update on vegetative health indicators. Given the low priority importance of this tasking, we maintain flexibility in scheduling; however, please note that the data must be delivered within four days overnight to meet upstream module requirements for timely analysis and reporting.

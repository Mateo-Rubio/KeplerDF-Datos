This is an absolute institutional priority to conduct a high-resolution optical snapshot of multispectral vegetation in Chivatá, Colombia. The focus will be on assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating surface reflectance for true-color visual documentation. Given its critical payload capture status, this mission has been earmarked as an urgent operational execution that must adhere to a binding contract requirement with no room for delays or rescheduling at convenience due to the immediacy of our institutional demands. The schedule is firmly set within three days during the afternoon when optimal lighting conditions will facilitate accurate multispectral analysis. All assets are expected to lock into this un-preemptable mission as soon as they can provide a comprehensive data delivery under these stringent timelines and objectives.

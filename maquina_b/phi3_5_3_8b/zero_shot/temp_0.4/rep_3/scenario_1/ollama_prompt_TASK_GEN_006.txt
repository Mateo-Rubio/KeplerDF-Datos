The following task is a standard operational tasking for multispectral vegetation analysis in Pekanbaru, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data products are needed by the day after tomorrow in the morning at the latest, ensuring they are ingested into the workflow promptly to maintain our regular reporting cadence.

We have a standard operational tasking scheduled for Murung Raya in Indonesia, where we aim to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters and is intended to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index for surface reflectance. The data products are needed by in four days overnight at the latest, ensuring they are ready for integration into our system before this deadline.

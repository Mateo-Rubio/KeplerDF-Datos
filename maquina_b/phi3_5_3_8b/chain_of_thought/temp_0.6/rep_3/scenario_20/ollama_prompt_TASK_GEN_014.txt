In accordance with our institutional priority, we are directing a mandatory directive for an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging at Isola del Gran Sasso d'Italia in Italy. This critical payload capture is to be executed tomorrow during the designated overnight operational window. The aim of this mission lock, which has been set as absolute and non-preemptable due to its high importance, is to penetrate any adverse weather conditions and accurately map the ground geometry beneath heavy cloud cover or other obstructions that may impede visual observation methods.

In accordance with our Earth observation mission requirements, we are requesting a thermal mapping operation over Indonesia's Equatorial region. This task involves capturing heat signatures using high-resolution optical snapshot techniques for true-color visual documentation of surface temperature profiles and detecting any thermal anomalies that may occur around mid-day within the next three days. Due to its low priority importance, this operation can be adjusted or postponed as deemed suitable by our team's convenience. However, it is acknowledged that these data products are essential for maintaining a comprehensive understanding of environmental heat dynamics in equatorial regions.

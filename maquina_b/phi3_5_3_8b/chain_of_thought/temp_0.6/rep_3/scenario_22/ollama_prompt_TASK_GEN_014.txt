As a request from Earth-observation mission operator, we are looking forward to conducting a non-essential background survey with a flexible schedule. Our objective is to capture high-resolution optical snapshots during overnight conditions for daylight photography purposes in Prados di Sopra, located within the picturesque country of Italia. While this task holds low priority importance and can be adjusted or postponed at convenience, it remains a part of our efforts to maintain comprehensive coverage. The data products are expected to be delivered by "the day after tomorrow" without altering the deadline.

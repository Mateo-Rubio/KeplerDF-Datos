In accordance with our Earth-observation mission objectives for a routine monitoring task scheduled three days from today during the nighttime hours, we seek to conduct a high-resolution optical snapshot focused on multispectral vegetation analysis within San Martín, Colombia. The aim is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate surface reflectance by leveraging near-infrared reflection capabilities of our satellite instruments. This task will contribute valuable data for the ongoing baseline pipeline tracking, supporting environmental monitoring efforts without altering or extending the strict operational deadline in place.

In three days, conduct a non-essential background survey for multispectral vegetation analysis in Colombia's San Martín during the nighttime hours. This task is considered low priority and can be adjusted or rescheduled according to current operational capacity and convenience. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels, calculate a vegetation NDVI index, and examine surface reflectance using high-resolution optical snapshots in the near-infrared spectrum to discriminate gradients associated with vegetative health and patterns.

As a task requirement, we are setting up a thermal mapping mission using active radar microwave surface imaging (TIR sensor) with absolute institutional priority and un-preemptable operational lock. This initiative is to capture heat signatures essential for understanding the nocturnal temperature dynamics in Norte within Slovenia. The targeted data acquisition will occur precisely three days from now, specifically during the overnight hours to ensure optimal conditions for thermal anomaly detection and mapping of surface emissions without any alterations or extensions to this deadline.

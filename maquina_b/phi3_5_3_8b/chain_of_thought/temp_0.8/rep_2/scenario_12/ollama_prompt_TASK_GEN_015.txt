As a routine monitoring task scheduled for tomorrow morning, we have established a priority 2 operational directive focusing on multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities of our satellite assets to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) index. This standard tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking, aimed at continuously monitoring environmental health indicators in Kutai Kartanegara, Indonesia. The collected data products are expected to enhance our understanding of vegetation dynamics and inform necessary adjustments within agricultural practices or conservation efforts where required.

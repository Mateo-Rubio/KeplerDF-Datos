As a non-essential background filler task scheduled for execution, we aim to capture overnight imagery using high-resolution optical snapshots through multispectral vegetation analysis in Indonesia's equatorial city of Ecuatorial. This opportunistic task is intended to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index for surface reflectance, providing valuable insights into the region's natural resources without disrupting our primary mission objectives. The data products are expected within "the day after tomorrow" following an overnight collection period.

In accordance with our ongoing efforts to monitor environmental changes through thermal anomalies, we have scheduled a non-essential background survey task aimed at capturing a heat signature in Serravalle di Chienti, Italy. Specifically, this entails conducting an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging designed for cloud-penetrating capture and detailing the ground's geometry under various atmospheric conditions. This flexible schedule task is to be carried out at dawn in two days, providing us with a unique opportunity to observe natural thermal dynamics during this specific time of day when temperature differentials can offer insightful data. The request aligns with our general workflow update and will contribute valuable information for further analysis within the broader context of climate monitoring studies.

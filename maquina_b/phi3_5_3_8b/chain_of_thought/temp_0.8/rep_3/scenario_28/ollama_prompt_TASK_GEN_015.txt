In four days, during an overnight period, we aim to conduct a low priority importance task that involves taking high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and true-color visual documentation of Rumah Budak city and its surrounding area using the multispectral capabilities of our VNIR sensor. This opportunistic capture will assist in assessing chlorophyll absorption levels, calculating surface reflectance, and determining vegetation health through NDVI index analysis without impacting any critical operational deadlines or mission priorities.

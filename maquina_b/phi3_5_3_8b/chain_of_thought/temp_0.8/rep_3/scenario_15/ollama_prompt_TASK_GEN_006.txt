As requested for a VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis mission, our objective is to capture high-resolution optical snapshots with true-color visual documentation within Sintang city of Indonesia on the morrow at dawn. This background survey task has been assigned low operational priority and can be adjusted or postponed as convenience dictates; however, adherence to this timeline is essential for our scheduled workflow update. The data gathered will assist in assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating a vegetation NDVI index along with surface reflectance measurements.

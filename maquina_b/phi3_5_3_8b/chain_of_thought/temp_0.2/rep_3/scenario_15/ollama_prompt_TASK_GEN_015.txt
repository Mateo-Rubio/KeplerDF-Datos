As a non-essential background filler task with flexible scheduling, we seek to conduct an overnight thermal mapping in Indonesia, specifically targeting Basa Ampek Balai Tapan area using ground temperature profiling through high-resolution infrared observation. This operation is not time-critical and can be adjusted according to convenience; however, it should ideally align with the nighttime hours for optimal data collection conditions. The objective here is to capture a heat signature that will contribute to our understanding of surface temperature dynamics in this region during nocturnal periods.

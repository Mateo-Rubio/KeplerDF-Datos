In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols and within the framework of routine monitoring, we request a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities during the nighttime period in three days' timeframe to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate a vegetation Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) index and surface reflectance. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, aimed at maintaining the continuity of our environmental monitoring efforts in Sabana de Torres, Colombia.

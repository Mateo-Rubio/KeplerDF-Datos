In accordance with our standard operational tasking protocols and within the framework of routine monitoring for environmental assessment purposes, we are requesting a SAR (active radar scan) operation over Puerto López in Colombia. This mission is scheduled to occur three days from today's date during the evening timeframe. The objective here is to capture cloud-penetrating microwave surface imagery that will provide us with detailed ground geometry and facilitate our ongoing analysis of terrain changes, especially under adverse weather conditions where optical sensors may be limited. This tasking aligns with our regular monitoring cycle and aims to maintain the continuity of data collection for environmental studies without altering or extending beyond the established deadline.

As an Earth-observation mission operator, I hereby submit a task request for today's operation in Tanjung Jabung Timur, Indonesia. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities of our satellite constellation during the nighttime period. This low priority importance task will provide insights into water body boundaries and soil moisture assessment by discriminating gradients in the absence of sunlight, which is essential for understanding nocturnal environmental dynamics. The data products are expected to be delivered promptly within today's overnight window without altering or extending this strict operational deadline.

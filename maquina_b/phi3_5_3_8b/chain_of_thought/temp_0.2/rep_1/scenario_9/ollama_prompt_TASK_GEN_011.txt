In accordance with our current operational requirements for a low priority importance task, we seek to conduct an overnight thermal mapping operation using ground-based infrared sensors in Slovenia's Norte city. This initiative is aimed at capturing the heat signature of the region during the nighttime hours within three days from today. The objective here is not urgent operational execution but rather a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, which can be dropped or rescheduled according to convenience. Please note that this task serves as background survey and testbed tracking, with no immediate deadline pressure beyond the stated timeframe.

As a non-essential background filler task with flexible scheduling requirements, we seek to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis in Cossogno, Italy, specifically aiming for morning observations within four days from today's date. This initiative is intended as an opportunistic capture and does not necessitate immediate action; however, should circumstances demand, it can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The goal here is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index along with surface reflectance using high-resolution optical snapshots for true-color visual documentation during daylight hours.

In accordance with our institutional requirements for routine monitoring and standard operational tasking within the Colombian agricultural sector, we seek to perform a VNIR sensor-based vegetation analysis from San Martín. Specifically, this initiative targets capturing high-resolution optical snapshots during an overnight period in four days' timeframe to assess multispectral reflectance and compute surface reflectance metrics indicative of plant health. The data gathered will be instrumental for ongoing agricultural monitoring projects focused on sustainable farming practices.

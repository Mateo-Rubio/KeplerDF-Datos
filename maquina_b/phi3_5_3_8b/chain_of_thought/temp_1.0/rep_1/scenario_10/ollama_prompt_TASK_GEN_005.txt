In alignment with our routine monitoring schedule, we are assigning a standard operational tasking to capture near-infrared reflection from water bodies and assess soil moisture gradients over Norte city starting on "the day after tomorrow, specifically in the morning." This mission is an integral component of our multispectral vegetation analysis cycle, aimed at discriminating moisture content variations within the region. The requested imagery will contribute to a comprehensive understanding of environmental hydration patterns and support further research on water body boundary mapping using near-infrared reflection techniques during typical daylight hours in the morning when atmospheric conditions are expected to be most stable for our purposes.

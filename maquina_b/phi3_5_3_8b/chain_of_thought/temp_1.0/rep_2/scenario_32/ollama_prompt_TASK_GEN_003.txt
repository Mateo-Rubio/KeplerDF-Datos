In compliance with standard operational tasking protocols and maintaining our established baseline pipeline tracking system, we are scheduled to deploy TIR (Thermal Mapping) sensors from an Earth-observation satellite constellation to capture thermal imagery of the Indonesian city of Ketapang. This mission is aimed at monitoring surface temperature profiles during a designated afternoon period, specifically "the day after tomorrow" afternoon, with no alterations or extensions made to this deadline. The data gathered will aid in identifying thermal anomalies and contribute valuable insights into local heat emissions as part of our environmental surveillance efforts. This tasking aligns seamlessly within the routine monitoring framework and is set to proceed as per its institutionally allocated priority level, ensuring timely delivery without impeding higher-priority operations.

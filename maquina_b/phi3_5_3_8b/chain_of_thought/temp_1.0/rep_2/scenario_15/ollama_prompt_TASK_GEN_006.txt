As a non-essential background filler task with flexible scheduling considerations, we aim to conduct multispectral vegetation analysis in Sintang, Indonesia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) and surface reflectance using high-resolution optical snapshot imagery during clear morning light conditions on 'the day after tomorrow'. This task can be temporarily dropped or rescheduled at our convenience, serving as a supplementary procedure to enhance the routine monitoring of vegetative health in targeted regions.

In accordance with our routine monitoring schedule for multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection capabilities, we have established a target tasking within four days during the morning period. This standard operational tasking is set to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) along with surface reflectance in Arsiero, Italia. The objective is to ensure that our baseline pipeline tracking maintains normal operational status by capturing essential data for vegetation health monitoring within this region.

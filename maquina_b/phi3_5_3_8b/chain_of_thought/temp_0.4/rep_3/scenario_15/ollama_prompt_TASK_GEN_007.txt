In accordance with our institutional priority for absolute urgency and critical payload capture, we hereby issue a binding contract requirement for a thermal mapping mission over Bolívar, Colombia. This operation is scheduled to commence precisely in the evening, three days hence, employing an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. The intent of this high-resolution optical snapshot is not only to penetrate dense weather conditions but also to accurately map heat emissions and detect thermal anomalies within the specified geographic context. Given the critical nature of these observations, they are integral to our workflow update and must be executed with an un-preemptable mission lock. The data products derived from this tasking will significantly contribute to our understanding of surface temperature profiles in Bolívar during evening hours.

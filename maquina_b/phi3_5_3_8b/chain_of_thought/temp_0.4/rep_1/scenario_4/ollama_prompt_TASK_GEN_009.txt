As part of our routine monitoring within Indonesia, we have a commercial tasking to capture high-resolution optical snapshots using multispectral vegetation analysis in Rokan Hilir city. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for surface reflectance within three days, precisely during the morning hours. This standard operational tasking aligns with our baseline pipeline tracking setup, ensuring normal operational status continuity while providing valuable environmental data.

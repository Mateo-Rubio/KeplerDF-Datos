In accordance with our current task priorities and within a span of four days from today's date, we are requesting an SAR-based active radar scan specifically scheduled for early morning operations in Kuala Enok, Indonesia. This mission is designated as a low priority importance task that can be adjusted or postponed at the operator's discretion due to its flexible schedule. The primary objective of this operation is to capture cloud-penetrating microwave surface imagery and to map out ground geometry under various weather conditions, providing valuable data for environmental monitoring and analysis.

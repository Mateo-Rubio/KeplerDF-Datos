As a matter of standard operational procedure, we are tasked today with conducting a thermal mapping over Tuluá, Colombia, during the evening hours. This initiative is aimed at capturing heat signatures and surface temperature profiles to detect any potential thermal anomalies in the region. The operation will involve an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology to penetrate through heavy weather conditions when necessary, ensuring comprehensive data collection even under less-than-ideal atmospheric circumstances. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and will contribute significantly to the environmental analysis within the established deadline.

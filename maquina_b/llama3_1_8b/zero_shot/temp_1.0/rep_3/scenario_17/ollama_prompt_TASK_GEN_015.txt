Tasking Request for Remote Sensing Mission

We require a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index for the region of Kepulauan Meranti, Indonesia. This is a standard operational tasking as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The products must be ready by in four days in the morning at the latest to ensure they are ingested into the pipeline on time.

We require a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This task involves a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography, clear optical imagery, or true-color visual documentation using multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level or calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over the region of San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia. The data must be ready by in three days overnight at the latest.

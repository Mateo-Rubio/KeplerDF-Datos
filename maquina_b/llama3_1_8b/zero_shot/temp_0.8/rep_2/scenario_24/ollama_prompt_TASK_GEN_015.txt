Tasking Request for Earth Observation Satellite Constellation

We require a low-priority importance task with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture of an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging for cloud-penetrating capture and ground geometry assessment over the region of Indonesia, specifically focusing on Kuala Enok. This task is to be treated as a non-essential background filler task where the operator can drop or reschedule at convenience.

The data products from this mission are needed by in four days in the morning at the latest. The goal is to capture high-resolution images for cloud-penetrating radar, which will aid in our understanding of the region's topography and surface features. This request is part of a background survey task.

Tasking Request: High-Resolution Vegetation Analysis for Tagliole, Italia

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring and baseline pipeline tracking of vegetation health in Tagliole, Italia. The mission allocation priority is set as a commercial tasking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

The objective is to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot using multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. This will be achieved through a VNIR sensor.

The data must be ready by in three days overnight, ensuring timely ingestion into the pipeline before the deadline.

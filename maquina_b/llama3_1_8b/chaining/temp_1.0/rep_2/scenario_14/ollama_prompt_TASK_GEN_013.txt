We are tasking the satellite constellation to capture a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging for cloud-penetrating capture around mid-day in three days. The target location is Suratá, Colombia, where we are interested in observing the ground geometry without any specific urgency, as this request has a low priority importance and can be treated as a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture. This task will serve as a background survey to provide normal operational status updates and a routine monitoring part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters.

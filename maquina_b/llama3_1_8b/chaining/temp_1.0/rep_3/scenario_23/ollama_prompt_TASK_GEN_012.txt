Obtain a high-resolution near-infrared reflection snapshot of the water body boundary mapping in Malinau, Indonesia, using fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This is a standard operational tasking part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with normal operational status, and it represents a routine monitoring task with commercial tasking priority. The goal is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels or calculating a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance during daylight photography in the morning on the day after tomorrow.

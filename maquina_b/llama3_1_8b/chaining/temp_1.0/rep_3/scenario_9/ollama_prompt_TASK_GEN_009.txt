We require a low priority importance task to capture thermal mapping for thermal anomaly detection and heat emissions mapping over Indonesia, particularly focusing on the equatorial region around the city of Ecuatorial. The mission allocation is flexible with a drop-or-reschedule-at-convenience policy in place for opportunistic capture. The objective is to gather a high-resolution thermal snapshot using active microwave surface imaging during daylight hours, preferably around mid-day in three days. This task serves as a background survey and testbed tracking task, not requiring urgent operational execution or un-preemptable mission lock.

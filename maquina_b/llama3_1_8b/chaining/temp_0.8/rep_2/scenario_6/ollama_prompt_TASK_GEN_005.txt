Obtain an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging for cloud-penetrating capture of the northern Italian countryside around Sizzano, a small town in the region of Piedmont. This low priority importance task is part of a flexible schedule and can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. As a non-essential background filler task, we require this routine monitoring as a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the area in three days around mid-day.

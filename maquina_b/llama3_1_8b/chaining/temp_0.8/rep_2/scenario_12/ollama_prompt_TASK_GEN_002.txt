The operational objective for this mission allocation is to capture a high-resolution thermal map of the region of Campania in southern Italy, specifically focusing on the city of Napoli, where we will utilize our active thermal sensor to detect and analyze surface temperature profiles and heat signatures. This task is designated as an absolute institutional priority, with a binding contract requirement for critical payload capture, mandating an urgent operational execution and un-preemptable mission lock. The data must be collected by the day after tomorrow in the evening.

Tasking Request for Baseline Pipeline Tracking: High-Resolution Optical Snapshot of Indonesian Vegetation 

Obtain a high-resolution optical snapshot of Indonesian vegetation using multispectral VNIR sensors during the overnight period to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate vegetation NDVI indices. This tasking is part of a standard operational tasking and commercial tasking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, for baseline pipeline tracking. The target location is in Indonesia, specifically in the city of Setiris, where we aim to capture clear optical imagery of vegetation patterns during the overnight hours. The data must be delivered today overnight.

We require a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The commercial tasking involves multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared reflection to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance over Colombia, specifically in the city of Canelos. This is a standard operational tasking with a flexible schedule for opportunistic capture, where we aim to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot for daylight photography in the morning of the day after tomorrow.

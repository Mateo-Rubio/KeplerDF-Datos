We are assigning a mission with absolute institutional priority to capture high-resolution optical snapshot data for daylight photography and clear optical imagery using multispectral vegetation analysis in Colombia. The target location is Chivatá, where we will be assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index and surface reflectance during the afternoon on the next three days. The results of this critical payload capture will have a binding contract requirement for our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

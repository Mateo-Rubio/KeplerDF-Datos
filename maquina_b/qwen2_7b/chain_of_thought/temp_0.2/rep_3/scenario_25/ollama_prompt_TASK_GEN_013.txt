In four days, at the time when the world sleeps and the stars shine bright, our automated satellite constellation will capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Colombia's picturesque region, specifically the charming city of Cáchira. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates opportunistic capture without compromising operational efficiency. The imagery gathered overnight will serve as a valuable resource for monitoring environmental changes and urban development, providing us with clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation to assess the landscape under the serene cover of darkness.

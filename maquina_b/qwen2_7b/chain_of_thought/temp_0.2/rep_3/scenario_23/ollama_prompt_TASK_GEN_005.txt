In three days, at the time when the sun dips below the horizon and darkness envelops Sesta Godano, Italia, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot. This daylight photography will provide us with clear visual documentation of the region's landscape under the cover of night. As part of our standard operational tasking, this mission aims to gather essential data for baseline pipeline tracking, utilizing fixed parameters that represent our normal operational status. The imagery we seek will serve as a critical component in our ongoing efforts to monitor and understand the environmental conditions of this beautiful Italian city.

In three days, at the time of the evening, we request a low priority importance task for the capture of a multispectral vegetation analysis using near-infrared sensors over the city of Caramanta in Colombia. This "testbed tracking task" aims to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance, providing us with valuable insights into the health and productivity of the local vegetation without altering the deadline or shifting the time to an adjacent part of the day.

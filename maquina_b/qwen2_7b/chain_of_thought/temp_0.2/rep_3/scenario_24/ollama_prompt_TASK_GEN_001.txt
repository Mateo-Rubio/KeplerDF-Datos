In three days, we aim for an overnight mission allocation with a medium importance level. The primary sensor will be VNIR, tasked with conducting a nighttime multispectral vegetation analysis over Colombia's Sabana de Torres region. This operational tasking involves capturing high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and true-color visual documentation. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance, providing critical data on vegetation health and environmental conditions without the interference of daytime light or cloud cover.

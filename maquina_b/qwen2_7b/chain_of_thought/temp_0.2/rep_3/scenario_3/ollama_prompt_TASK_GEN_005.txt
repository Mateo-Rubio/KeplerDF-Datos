In three days, at the beginning of the morning, we request a low priority importance task focusing on the region of Italia, specifically the city of Porto Recanati. This mission involves conducting a routine monitoring operation using the VNIR sensor for multispectral vegetation analysis. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index, providing us with surface reflectance data. This task aims to capture high-resolution optical snapshots as daylight photography, offering clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation of the area. The deadline for delivering the data products is strictly set for the morning of the target day, without any adjustments or extensions to other parts of the day.

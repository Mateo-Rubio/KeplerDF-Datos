In three days, at the time when the world is enveloped in the peaceful quietness of overnight, our Earth-observation mission will capture a thermal mapping snapshot of the beautiful Slovenian region, specifically focusing on the city of Norte. This task is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling to accommodate any necessary adjustments or rescheduling at your convenience. The primary sensor involved will be the TIR (Thermal Infrared) instrument, which will provide us with invaluable data on heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the region. This thermal mapping will serve as a background survey, offering insights into heat emissions and potential anomalies that require further investigation.

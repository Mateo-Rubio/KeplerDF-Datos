In three days, at the time of overnight observation, we request the allocation of a satellite mission with medium importance for the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data over the region of Monterrey, Colombia. This task aims to document water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels using the unique capabilities of NIR sensors. The operational objective is to ensure that this critical data collection occurs precisely at the specified time, without any adjustments to the day or the diurnal period, adhering strictly to the deadline for delivering the data products.

In four days, around mid-day, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of the city of Mamuju, Indonesia. This standard operational tasking involves obtaining clear daylight photography and true-color visual documentation of the region using our visible light sensor. The mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to provide this data as part of our routine monitoring efforts, contributing to our ongoing commercial tasking and workflow updates.

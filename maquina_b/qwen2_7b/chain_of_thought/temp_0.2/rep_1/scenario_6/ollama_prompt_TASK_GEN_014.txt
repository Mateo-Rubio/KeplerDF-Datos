In response to the high-priority directive for tomorrow, we are tasked with capturing a critical dataset using near-infrared (NIR) sensors. This mission aims to deliver a comprehensive thermal mapping of Val Masino, Italy, focusing on mid-day conditions around noon. The operational deadline is strictly set for this specific time, emphasizing the importance of capturing the heat signature and surface temperature profile accurately under direct sunlight. This data collection is essential for understanding thermal anomalies and heat emissions in the region, contributing to critical decision-making processes without altering the deadline or shifting the capture window to an adjacent part of the day.

In three days, an overnight mission allocation is required for a standard operational tasking involving a VNIR sensor. This request is to capture a nighttime snapshot of Tagliole, Italy, focusing on the unique conditions that arise after sunset. The imagery will serve as a baseline pipeline tracking asset, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for this region, providing insights into the health and composition of local ecosystems under the cover of darkness.

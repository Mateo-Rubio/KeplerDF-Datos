In response to the urgent operational execution required by our binding contract with the Indonesian government for the critical payload capture of Murung Raya city's overnight thermal signature, we are tasked with conducting a comprehensive near-infrared (NIR) observation. This high-resolution optical snapshot will serve as a true-color visual documentation of Murung Raya during the overnight period exactly three days from now. The primary objective is to assess water body boundaries and soil moisture levels for critical infrastructure planning and environmental monitoring purposes. We are committed to delivering this data product by the deadline specified, adhering strictly to the operational parameters set forth without any adjustments or extensions to the target day or diurnal period.

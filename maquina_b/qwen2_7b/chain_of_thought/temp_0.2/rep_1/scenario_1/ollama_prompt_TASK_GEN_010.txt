In three days, during the afternoon, we are tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey using the VNIR sensor over El Castillo, Colombia. This involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the vegetation NDVI index for the area. The goal is to provide thermal mapping data that captures heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, which will be essential for detecting thermal anomalies and mapping heat emissions. This non-essential background filler task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim to deliver this data product by the deadline without altering or extending the operational window.

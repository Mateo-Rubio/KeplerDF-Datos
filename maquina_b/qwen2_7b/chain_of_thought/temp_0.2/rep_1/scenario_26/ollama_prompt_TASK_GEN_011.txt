Given the operational context, our Earth-observation mission requires capturing a near-infrared reflection dataset for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. This task is designated as a low priority importance request with a flexible schedule, treating it as a non-essential background filler task. We aim to capture a snapshot of the Earth's surface conditions under these specific temporal parameters without altering or extending the deadline.

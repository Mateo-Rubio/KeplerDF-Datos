On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun has set and the stars are out, our satellite constellation will be tasked with capturing a near-infrared reflection snapshot of the Ecuatorial region in Indonesia. This overnight mission is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for a flexible schedule that accommodates opportunistic capture. The primary sensor to be deployed will focus on mapping water body boundaries and assessing soil moisture levels across the area, providing critical insights into environmental conditions without the interference of daylight or cloud cover.

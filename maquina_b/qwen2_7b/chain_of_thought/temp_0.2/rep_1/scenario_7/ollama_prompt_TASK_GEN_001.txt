In three days, at the time when the sun has set and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, we are tasked with capturing a detailed snapshot of the city of Foraquira in Colombia. Our primary sensor will be the VNIR camera, designed for multispectral vegetation analysis. This mission aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption level across various landscapes within the city, providing crucial insights into the health and vitality of its vegetation. The data collected overnight will serve as a valuable resource for environmental monitoring and management, ensuring that we capture the unique nighttime conditions that reveal the true nature of Foraquira's green spaces.

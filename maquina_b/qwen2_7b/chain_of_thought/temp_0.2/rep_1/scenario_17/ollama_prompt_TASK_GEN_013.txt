In four days, around mid-day, we are tasked with capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot of San Pablo, Colombia. This daylight photography will serve as a clear visual documentation of the region's landscape and urban environment. The mission is designated as a low priority importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling to accommodate opportunistic capture. We aim to deliver thermal mapping data that highlights surface temperature profiles and heat emissions, providing valuable insights into local conditions without altering the deadline for data delivery.

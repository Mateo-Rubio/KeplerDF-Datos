In four days, at the time when the sun dips below the horizon and darkness blankets the land, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a near-infrared reflection snapshot of San Carlos de Guaroa, Colombia. This nighttime observation is part of a standard operational tasking, designed to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries with precision. The imagery gathered overnight will contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking efforts, providing critical data for environmental monitoring and agricultural assessment without the interference of daylight conditions.

In response to the high-priority directive received from our institutional stakeholders, we are tasked with capturing a detailed multispectral vegetation analysis of the region in Colombia, specifically focusing on Sabana de Torres. This critical payload capture is essential for assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index across the landscape. The operational deadline for this mission is set for four days hence, with the requirement to conduct the active radar scan during the morning hours. This ensures optimal conditions for cloud-penetrating capture, providing us with unparalleled insights into ground geometry and vegetation health under clear skies.

In four days, during the morning hours, our automated satellite constellation planner is tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of the Indonesian region, specifically focusing on Singkep Barat. This mission involves a low priority importance request for a routine monitoring task using a VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for this area. The goal is to provide clear optical imagery for daylight photography, true-color visual documentation, and to capture surface reflectance data without altering the deadline or shifting it to an adjacent part of the day.

In response to the urgent operational execution requested for our absolute institutional priority, we aim to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of Cumaral, Colombia, through a routine monitoring tasking. This will involve an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to pierce heavy weather conditions and capture ground geometry overnight in four days. The primary sensor utilized will be the VNIR (visual near-infrared) for multispectral vegetation analysis, assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to provide a comprehensive view of the area's vegetation health. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data products are due by the deadline specified, which is in four days overnight, without any alteration or extension of the timeframe.

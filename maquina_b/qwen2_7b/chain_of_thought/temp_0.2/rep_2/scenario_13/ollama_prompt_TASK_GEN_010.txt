In three days, at the start of the morning, we aim to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis over Puerto Rico, Colombia. This task involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption levels across various landscapes and calculating the vegetation NDVI index for each area. We seek to understand surface reflectance patterns and identify any anomalies that might indicate environmental stress or health issues in the vegetation. This standard operational tasking is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status.

In four days, at the time of overnight, we request the capture of a low priority importance task using a VNIR sensor over the region of Malaysia, specifically focusing on the area known as Rumah Budak. This request is framed as a flexible schedule background survey, where the operator explicitly notes to drop or reschedule at convenience. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for this area. This task aims to provide insights into surface reflectance, contributing to our understanding of the local ecosystem without imposing any urgent operational execution requirements.

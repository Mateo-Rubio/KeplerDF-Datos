Given the high importance of this mission, we are tasked with capturing a critical payload on "the day after tomorrow" during the overnight period. This absolute institutional priority involves a mandatory directive for a routine monitoring task that is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The standard operational tasking requires us to perform a VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis using our satellite constellation. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index, as well as map surface reflectance across the region of Italia, specifically focusing on Borgo Valsugana. This active radar scan will provide invaluable data for understanding the health of the vegetation and environmental conditions in the area during the overnight period, when conditions are optimal for our active microwave surface imaging technology to pierce through any cloud cover and capture ground geometry accurately.

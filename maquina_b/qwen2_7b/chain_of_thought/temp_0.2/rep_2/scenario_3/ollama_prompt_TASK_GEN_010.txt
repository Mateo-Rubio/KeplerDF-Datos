In three days, at the time of overnight observations, we request a standard operational tasking for the capture of near-infrared (NIR) data over the region encompassing Colombia and its city Monterrey. This mission aims to provide thermal mapping capabilities, focusing on capturing heat signatures and surface temperature profiles across the area. The primary objective is to assess soil moisture levels and delineate water body boundaries using the unique properties of near-infrared reflection. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, ensuring that this request is treated as a standard workflow update without altering the deadline for data delivery.

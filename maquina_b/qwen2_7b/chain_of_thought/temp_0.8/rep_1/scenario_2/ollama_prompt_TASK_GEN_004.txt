The satellite mission will capture a mid-day active radar scan in Colombia, specifically around the city of Nóvita. Scheduled for four days from now, this task is marked as a low priority importance request, fitting into our standard operational pipeline tracking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This 'around mid-day' SAR observation aims to provide detailed microwave surface imaging for cloud-penetrating capture and ground geometry assessment, essential for comprehensive monitoring and operational planning in the region.

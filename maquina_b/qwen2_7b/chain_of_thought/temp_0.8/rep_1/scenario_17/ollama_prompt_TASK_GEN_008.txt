In accordance with our standard operational tasking, we aim to execute an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging on the region of interest in three days, specifically around mid-day. The geographical context encompasses the picturesque area of Italia, particularly the city of Giugliano in Campania. This operation is not time-critical; however, it is essential for the baseline pipeline tracking and should be performed with normal operational status, ensuring fixed parameters are adhered to throughout the process. The primary sensor selected for this task utilizes SAR technology for cloud-penetrating capture of ground geometry, which aligns well with our ongoing commercial tracking and monitoring efforts in the region.

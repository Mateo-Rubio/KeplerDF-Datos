In three days, at around mid-day, the satellite constellation will be tasked with an operational mission of medium importance involving active microwave surface imaging from the SAR sensor. This mission is to capture high-resolution optical snapshots for clear daylight photography across the region of Italia, specifically focusing on the city of Giugliano in Campania. The primary objective is to conduct an active radar scan to extract detailed ground geometry and cloud-penetrating information, ensuring our baseline pipeline tracking system receives fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This data gathering mission aims to provide essential insights without compromising urgent operational execution or contractual obligations.

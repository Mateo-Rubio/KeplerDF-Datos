Operating tomorrow overnight, our satellite constellation is tasked with conducting a crucial SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) scan on a location in Indonesia's Rokan Hilir. This active microwave surface imaging will be pivotal for penetrating cloud cover or capturing detailed ground geometry, essential for our operational baseline pipeline tracking and non-essential background survey requirements.

Given the high importance of this task given its priority level, we are to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the region, which involves daylight photography, clear optical imagery, and true-color visual documentation of any surface features under observation. This imagery will complement our baseline workflow updates with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

Upon completion of the SAR scan overnight, thermal mapping through TIR (Thermal Infrared) sensors is to be performed to identify heat signatures or surface temperature profiles that may indicate potential anomalies, such as industrial emissions or vegetation stress. This process will enhance our capabilities in detecting and monitoring environmental changes that occur at night when they might otherwise go unnoticed.

For the multispectral VNIR (Visible Near-Infrared) sensor component of this mission, we aim to analyze vegetation for chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate a vegetation NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) and surface reflectance data. This will provide critical insights into land use, health of crops, and overall environmental conditions without the day's obfuscating factors.

With a low priority importance level assigned to this task, it is marked as an opportunistic capture that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience without compromising the integrity of our baseline pipeline tracking. The data products derived from these observations will serve as valuable background information for future analysis and planning, ensuring that we remain vigilant and responsive to environmental changes across Indonesia's Rokan Hilir region.

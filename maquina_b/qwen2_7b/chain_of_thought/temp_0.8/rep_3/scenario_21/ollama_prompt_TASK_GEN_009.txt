In three days, during the morning hours, we request to capture high-resolution optical imagery using visual imaging capabilities over Ecuatorial, Indonesia. This tasking is assigned with medium importance and involves an active radar scan for cloud-penetrating capture and ground geometry mapping, thermal mapping to detect heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, and multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate NDVI index and surface reflectance. This mission allocation is part of our standard operational tasks that contribute to our baseline pipeline tracking with normal operational status. The priority level reflects the institutional importance of this task within our operational framework.

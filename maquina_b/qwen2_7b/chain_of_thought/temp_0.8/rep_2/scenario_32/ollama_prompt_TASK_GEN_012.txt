To address a standard operational tasking request with medium importance and fixed parameters, we will capture high-resolution optical snapshots using near-infrared (NIR) sensors in four days. Specifically, this involves an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to pierce heavy weather and capture ground geometry overnight at the location of Murung Raya, Indonesia. The requirement is for thermal mapping to assess water body boundaries or soil moisture levels as part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup. This data collection will be performed in a standard operational status context without altering the deadline for delivery.

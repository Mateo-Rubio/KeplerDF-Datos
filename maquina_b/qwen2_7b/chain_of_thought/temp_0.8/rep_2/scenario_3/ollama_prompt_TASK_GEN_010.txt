Nearing the operational deadline, our satellite constellation planner will be tasked with capturing a near-infrared reflection profile overnight in Colombia. Specifically, focusing on the city of Monterrey, the priority mission request involves an "in three days" capture to ensure that water body boundaries and soil moisture are accurately mapped under the cover of darkness. This standard operational tasking aims to optimize surveillance capabilities during the overnight period, providing crucial insights into ground conditions without the interference of daylight illumination.

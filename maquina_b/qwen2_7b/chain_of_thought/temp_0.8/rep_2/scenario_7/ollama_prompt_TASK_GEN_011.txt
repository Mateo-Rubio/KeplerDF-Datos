In a few days on "the day after tomorrow" at mid-day, when daylight is at its zenith and the sky is clear, we are tasked with capturing high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) imagery of Valle-di-Rostino in France. This request originates from an absolute institutional priority as it involves capturing critical data that is essential for detailed soil moisture assessment, which could impact agricultural planning and water resource management. To ensure accuracy and timeliness, this specific capture window must be adhered to without exception. We aim to gather comprehensive information on surface reflectance and water body boundaries through this mission, providing invaluable insights for environmental monitoring activities.

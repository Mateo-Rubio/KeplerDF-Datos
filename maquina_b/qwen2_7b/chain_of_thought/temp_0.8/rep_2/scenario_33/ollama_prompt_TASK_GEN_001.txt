In three days, during the morning period, a standard operational tasking is required: An active radar scan using microwave surface imaging is to capture cloud-penetrating data for the region known as Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Dolores. This mission aims to provide critical insights into ground geometry and surface features without being impeded by weather conditions. Since it holds a low priority importance with flexible scheduling, this task can be accommodated as part of our normal operational status or can serve as a background survey for our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

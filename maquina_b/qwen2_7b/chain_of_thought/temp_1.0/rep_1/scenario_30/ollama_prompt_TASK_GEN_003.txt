Constituting a task of low priority importance for our Earth-observation mission operator, we are to gather data pertaining to the Indonesian city of Malinau on the day immediately ahead, during the exact period defined as "overnight." The primary sensor utilized will be our Near-Infrared (NIR) instrument. Our aim is to leverage its capabilities for near-infrared reflection assessments, particularly for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture evaluation, ensuring a critical component of our background survey operations.

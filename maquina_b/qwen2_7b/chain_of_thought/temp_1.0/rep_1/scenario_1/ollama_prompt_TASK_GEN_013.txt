Nirvana's request is for a unique observational task, focusing on Santa Rosa del Sur, Colombia, under the constraint of 'the day after tomorrow' and 'overnight'. As a non-essential background filler task with low priority importance, our team aims to capture high-resolution near-infrared imagery to delineate water body boundaries or assess soil moisture levels flexibly, without preemption from other high-priority missions. This mission encapsulates an opportune survey window under nighttime conditions for comprehensive data collection and potential research applications.

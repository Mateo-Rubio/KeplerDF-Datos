In four days, at the start of the overnight period when Earth's shadow deepens and provides optimal conditions for thermal mapping and near-infrared reflection observation, an opportunistic background survey is requested for the region of Rumah Budak, Malaysia. This low priority importance task aims to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot for true-color visual documentation, to complement existing baseline pipeline tracking activities and fulfill standard operational tasks related to multispectral vegetation analysis. The purpose is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels or calculate the NDVI index for land use assessment without altering the deadline timeframe. Expectations are to gather information that can help in discriminating gradients and understanding surface temperature profiles without changing the planned schedule.

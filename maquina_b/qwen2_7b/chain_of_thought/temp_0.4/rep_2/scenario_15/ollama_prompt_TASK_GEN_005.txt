On the day after tomorrow, at the time when the sun has set and the stars are beginning to twinkle in the sky, we request the capture of a high-resolution optical snapshot over Olmo al Brembo, located in the beautiful country of Italia. This "overnight" mission is classified as a low priority importance task with flexible scheduling requirements, designed to be a non-essential background filler task that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The primary sensor for this operation will be a VIS camera, aimed to provide us with true-color visual documentation of the area under consideration during this unique part of the day when the landscape might reveal distinct features not visible during daylight hours.

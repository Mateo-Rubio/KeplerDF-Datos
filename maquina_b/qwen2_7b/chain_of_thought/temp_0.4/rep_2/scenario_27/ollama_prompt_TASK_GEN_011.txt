In three days, during the afternoon, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a thermal mapping dataset for Norte, France. This task is part of our standard operational pipeline tracking, representing normal operational status with fixed parameters. The critical payload involves assessing surface temperature profiles and detecting thermal anomalies, essential for understanding heat emissions across the region. Our aim is to provide high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and clear optical imagery, complementing the thermal data to offer a comprehensive view of the area's conditions.

In four days, during the morning hours, our satellite constellation is tasked with capturing high-resolution multispectral vegetation data for the region of Prati di Tivo in Italia. This standard operational tasking involves assessing chlorophyll absorption levels and calculating the NDVI index to provide detailed surface reflectance information. The imagery acquired will serve as a valuable resource for baseline pipeline tracking, offering critical insights into ecological health and environmental conditions without altering the specified deadline.

In three days, at the time when the sun dips below the horizon and the stars begin to twinkle in the sky, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a near-infrared reflection snapshot of the beautiful city of Calderara di Reno, Italia. This low priority importance request has been framed with flexibility and understanding that it is part of a background survey task. Our operator acknowledges that while this mission is not an absolute institutional priority or a binding contract requirement, it is still a standard operational tasking within our baseline pipeline tracking setup. We will ensure that the satellite assets are deployed to capture this thermal mapping data when the city is bathed in the calm of the overnight hours, allowing for a focused and undisturbed view of its surface temperature profile and potential heat emissions.

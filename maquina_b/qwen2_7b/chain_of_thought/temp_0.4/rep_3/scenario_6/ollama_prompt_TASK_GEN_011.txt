In preparation for our operational pipeline tracking, we are scheduling a standard monitoring task involving near-infrared (NIR) sensor observations. This mission focuses on capturing high-resolution optical snapshots of the region in Schweiz/Suisse/Svizzera/Svizra, specifically the city of Rheinwald. The deadline for delivering this data product is set for four days from now, with an emphasis on acquiring imagery around mid-day. Our aim is to capture clear optical imagery that will serve as a true-color visual documentation of the area during daylight hours. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and represents normal operational status.

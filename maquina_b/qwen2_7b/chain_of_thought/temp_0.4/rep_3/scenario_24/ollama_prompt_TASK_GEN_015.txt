In four days, at the start of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture high-resolution active radar scans over Indonesia's stunning landscape. Specifically, we're focusing on the city of Kuala Enok. This mission is a routine monitoring task, part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Given its importance level, it's a standard operational tasking that doesn't require urgent execution but should be prioritized within this timeframe. We aim to obtain cloud-penetrating capture for a detailed microwave surface imaging, providing invaluable insights into ground geometry and enabling us to pierce through any heavy weather conditions for accurate data collection.

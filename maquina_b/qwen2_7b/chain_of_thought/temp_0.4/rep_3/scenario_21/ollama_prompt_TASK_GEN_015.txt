In four days, during the morning hours, we request a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission involving the capture of multispectral vegetation analysis data using the VNIR sensor over the city of Solok in Indonesia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The high-resolution optical snapshot aims to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for the purpose of surface reflectance analysis. The deadline for delivering the data products is strictly set for that morning, as indicated in the target_diurnal_period metadata.

To address a standard operational tasking requirement with medium importance, we aim to capture high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography and clear optical imagery of Rokan Hilir, Indonesia. This "standard operational tasking" involves the use of a VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. The deadline for delivering this data product is set for an "in the morning" capture on "in three days", adhering strictly to the operational deadline without altering or extending it.

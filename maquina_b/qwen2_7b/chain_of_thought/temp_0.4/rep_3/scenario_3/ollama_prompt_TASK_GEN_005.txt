In three days, focusing on capturing data during the morning hours, we aim to conduct a routine monitoring task using our VNIR sensor over the picturesque region of Italia, specifically targeting the coastal city of Porto Recanati. This low priority importance request is framed as a flexible schedule opportunity for opportunistic capture, serving as a non-essential background filler task that can be conveniently dropped or rescheduled according to operational convenience. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance through multispectral vegetation analysis, providing valuable insights into the ecological conditions of the area without compromising our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

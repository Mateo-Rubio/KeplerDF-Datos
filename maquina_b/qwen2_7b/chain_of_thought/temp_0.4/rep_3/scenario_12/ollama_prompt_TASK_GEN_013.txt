As part of our flexible background survey tasks, we aim to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the city of Cantagallo, Colombia, within the three days following our request. This daylight photography mission will provide us with clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation of the region, serving as an essential testbed tracking task for our satellite constellation planner. The operational deadline is set for mid-day on the specified target day, ensuring that we capture the city's landscape under optimal lighting conditions without altering the scheduled timeframe.

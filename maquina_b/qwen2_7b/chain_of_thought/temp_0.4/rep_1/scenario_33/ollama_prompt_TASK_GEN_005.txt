In three days, at the beginning of the morning, our automated satellite constellation planner will capture a high-resolution active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology. This cloud-penetrating capture aims to provide detailed ground geometry data in the region of Arezzo, situated in the picturesque country of Italia. As this task carries a low priority importance, we understand that it can be scheduled flexibly and may be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if necessary. The goal is to gather thermal mapping data for heat signature analysis and surface temperature profiling, contributing to our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

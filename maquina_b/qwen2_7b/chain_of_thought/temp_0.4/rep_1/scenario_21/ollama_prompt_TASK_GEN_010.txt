On the specified operational deadline, "the day after tomorrow", with the target time set for the morning, our Earth-observation mission requires a high-resolution optical snapshot of El Retorno, Colombia. This daylight photography will serve as a clear optical imagery to document the region, essential for baseline pipeline tracking and routine monitoring activities. Given its low priority level, this request is framed as a flexible schedule task, allowing for opportunistic capture with no immediate need for urgent execution or contractual obligations. The primary sensor utilized will be the VIS system, enabling us to assess the chlorophyll absorption levels and surface reflectance, contributing to our understanding of vegetation health and environmental conditions in the area.

Given the operational guidelines and interpreting the metadata provided, our Earth-observation mission aims to capture a high-resolution optical snapshot of the Ecuatorial region in Indonesia. This daylight photography will serve as clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation for our baseline pipeline tracking setup. The mission is designated with a priority level that aligns with a low importance request, allowing for flexible scheduling and opportunistic capture. The task is to be executed in the morning of the target day, which is scheduled four days ahead. This "in the morning" deadline represents an absolute institutional priority, emphasizing the need for thermal mapping and capturing the heat signature of the region without altering or extending the operational window.

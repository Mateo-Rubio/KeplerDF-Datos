As per our operational guidelines, we request the capture of high-resolution optical imagery over the region named "Rassa" in the beautiful country of Italia. This mission is designated with a low priority importance level, which means that it's part of our flexible schedule for opportunistic capture and can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The imagery required will provide a clear optical snapshot during daylight hours, serving as true-color visual documentation of the landscape. We aim to complete this task "in three days in the morning" to ensure optimal lighting conditions for the best possible image quality.

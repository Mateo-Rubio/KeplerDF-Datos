For a standard operational tasking with a medium importance level, we aim to capture high-resolution near-infrared (NIR) data from Indonesia's Ecuatorial region on the specified day, which falls on "the day after tomorrow", and specifically in the morning. This essential data collection mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, employing normal operational status and fixed parameters for routine monitoring purposes. The NIR sensor will be used to map water body boundaries or assess soil moisture levels, contributing to our ongoing efforts in environmental monitoring and resource management activities.

In three days, during the evening timeframe, we request the capture of a low priority importance task using a VNIR sensor over the region encompassing Colombia with a specific focus on the city of Caramanta. This mission involves the acquisition of high-resolution optical imagery as part of a routine monitoring process. The goal is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the vegetation NDVI index for the area, providing us with valuable insights into local environmental conditions without altering or extending the deadline.

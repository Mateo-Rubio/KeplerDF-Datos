In response to the request with a priority level of medium importance, we are tasked to acquire data using near-infrared (NIR) sensors over the region of Italia, specifically focusing on the city of Norte. The operational deadline for this mission allocation is set for tomorrow, with the capture to occur in the morning hours. Our objective will be to obtain critical information on water body boundaries and soil moisture levels for this area, providing essential data for environmental monitoring and agricultural assessments. The imagery captured under these conditions will offer invaluable insights into land use and resource management needs without interference from atmospheric conditions that might affect optical clarity.

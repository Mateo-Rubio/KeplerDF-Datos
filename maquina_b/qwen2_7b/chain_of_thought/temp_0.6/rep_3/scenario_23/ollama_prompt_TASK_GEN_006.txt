In response to the Earth-observation mission operator's request, we will capture high-resolution active radar scans using microwave surface imaging technology. This scan aims to perform a cloud-penetrating capture in Ecuatorial Indonesia, focusing on critical features and ground geometry details. Given the priority level of Low Importance, this task is designated as a flexible schedule background survey that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The operational deadline for delivering the data products is set for an evening session exactly three days from now.

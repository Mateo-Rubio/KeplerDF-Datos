In approximately four days, during the morning hours, our satellite constellation is tasked with capturing high-resolution near-infrared reflection imagery over the Indonesian region of Kepulauan Meranti. This operational mission aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture conditions in the area using an active radar scan technique for cloud-penetrating capture. This standard operational tasking falls under our baseline pipeline tracking setup, representing normal operational status with fixed parameters and is part of our routine monitoring efforts.

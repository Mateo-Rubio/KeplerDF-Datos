The Earth-observation mission operator urgently requests a capture of high-resolution near-infrared imagery for the city of Santa Sofía, Colombia. Given the critical importance of this task, it is classified as an absolute institutional priority, entailing a binding contract requirement with a mandatory directive to collect a thermal mapping dataset. The operator mandates that this "overnight" mission be executed on "the day after tomorrow", which represents the strict deadline for data product delivery without any flexibility for time extension.

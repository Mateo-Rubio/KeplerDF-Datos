The Earth-observation mission operator is tasked with capturing a thermal mapping dataset for the region encompassing the 'Norte' city in 'Italia', which is to be acquired on the precise day that is three days ahead, in the evening. This is an absolute institutional priority due to its critical payload capture nature; hence, it must be executed as an urgent operational mission without any possibility of preemption or rescheduling. The operator will deploy the thermal imaging sensor (TIR) to gather detailed surface temperature profiles and detect heat anomalies across the urban landscape of 'Norte', capturing the essence of both human-made and natural thermal signatures before sunset. This high-resolution optical snapshot is essential for critical decision-making processes concerning urban planning, environmental monitoring, and public safety initiatives.

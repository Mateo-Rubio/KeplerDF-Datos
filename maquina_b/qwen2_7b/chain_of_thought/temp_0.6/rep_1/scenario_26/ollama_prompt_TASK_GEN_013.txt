Given the operational requirements, our Earth observation mission for today involves capturing a night-time snapshot of Betulia, Colombia using the VNIR sensor. This task is designated as a low priority importance request with a flexible schedule for an opportunistic capture. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking and a non-essential background filler task, we aim to document the nighttime scene in Betulia without altering the deadline, which is set for four days from now. This "overnight" observation will contribute to our ongoing efforts in monitoring and understanding the region's environmental conditions through the assessment of chlorophyll absorption levels or calculating the vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance.

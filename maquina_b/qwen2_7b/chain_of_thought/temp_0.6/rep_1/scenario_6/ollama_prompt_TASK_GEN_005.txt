This will allow for cloud-penetrating capture, ensuring that vital ground geometry is captured under any weather conditions. The focus will be on the Italian region, specifically the city of Sizzano, where detailed imagery is required to serve as a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status.

This mission allocation carries a medium importance level and adheres to our baseline pipeline tracking requirements without compromising the quality or timeliness of our services. The data collected will be essential for understanding surface conditions, supporting decision-making processes and monitoring activities related to environmental protection and urban planning in the region.

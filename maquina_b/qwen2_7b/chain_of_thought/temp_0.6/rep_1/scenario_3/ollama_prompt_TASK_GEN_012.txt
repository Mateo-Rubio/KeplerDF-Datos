In three days, during the evening, an urgent institutional priority requires us to capture a critical payload using near-infrared sensors for an essential task. Our mission is to obtain thermal mapping data of the city of Malinau, Indonesia, focusing on identifying heat signatures and surface temperature profiles. The high-resolution optical snapshot will provide invaluable insights into the thermal dynamics of the region, essential for ongoing environmental monitoring and management efforts. The deadline is strict and un-preemptable, mandating that this mission be executed without delay to ensure timely delivery of our data products.

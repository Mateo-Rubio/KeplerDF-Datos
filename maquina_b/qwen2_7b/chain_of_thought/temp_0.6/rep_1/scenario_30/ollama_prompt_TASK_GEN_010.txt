In three days, during the overnight period, we aim to capture thermal mapping data for the region of Tuluá, Colombia. Our mission focuses on capturing a heat signature and surface temperature profile to assist in thermal anomaly detection and mapping of heat emissions across the area. This operational tasking requires a flexible schedule, positioning it as a non-essential background filler task, with the understanding that it can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience if necessary. The primary sensor utilized for this mission is the TIR sensor, designed to provide critical insights into thermal patterns and emission profiles without the interference of atmospheric conditions.

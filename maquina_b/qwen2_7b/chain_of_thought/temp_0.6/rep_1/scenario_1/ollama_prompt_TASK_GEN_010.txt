In three days, during the afternoon, an opportunistic background survey task is requested for the location in Colombia, specifically El Castillo. The mission involves conducting a near-infrared reflection analysis to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels, capturing critical data to support environmental monitoring and agricultural assessment activities. The deadline for this operational tasking is firmly set to ensure timely delivery of these essential background survey results, allowing for informed decision-making without compromising the integrity or urgency of the mission's objectives.

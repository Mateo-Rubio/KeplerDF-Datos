Logging a standard operational tasking for Pantanillo in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over the area to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance, providing valuable insights on the health and productivity of local vegetation. This is considered routine monitoring at normal operational status, and we need the products by the target day, which falls in three days, in the morning.

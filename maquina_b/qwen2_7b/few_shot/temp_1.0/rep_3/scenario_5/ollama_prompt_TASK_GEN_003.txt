As a part of our routine monitoring and as a standard operational tasking with fixed parameters, we have set up this commercial tasking for the area around Ecuatorial in Indonesia. We're using active radar scanning powered by microwave surface imaging to obtain an image that will pierce through any cloud cover, giving us a clear view of the land geometry regardless of weather conditions. This low priority importance task is a background survey or testbed tracking task with no rush on deadlines other than the set target day of four days ahead in the overnight period. We aim to capture this area during this period to provide valuable information for our baseline pipeline tracking and normal operational status. The products are due by the deadline set for four days from now, specifically in the overnight timeframe.

Logging a standard operational tasking over San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. Our target is to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels, essential for agricultural planning and water resource management. An overnight operation, using near-infrared reflection, is required to distinguish surface gradients accurately under the cover of darkness. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and the delivery deadline is set for four days hence when we need these products by then overnight.

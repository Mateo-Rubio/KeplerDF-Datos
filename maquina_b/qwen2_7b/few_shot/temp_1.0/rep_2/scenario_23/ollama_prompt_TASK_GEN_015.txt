Logging a standard operational tasking for Kepulauan Meranti in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We're aiming for a VNIR multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index along with surface reflectance for areas within the archipelago. This is considered routine monitoring at normal operational status, and we will require the products to be ready for ingestion in four days overnight at the latest.

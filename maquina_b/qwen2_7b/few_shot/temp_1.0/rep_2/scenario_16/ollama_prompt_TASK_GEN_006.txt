Logging a standard operational tasking in Indonesia to capture the scene at Mulya Bakti, focusing on a near-infrared reflection for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. We are looking for clear images that distinguish gradients with high resolution in three days overnight, as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Cloud cover has been heavy in the region, so this task aims to provide the necessary information through near-infrared analysis for water bodies and soil moisture content without being subject to weather conditions.

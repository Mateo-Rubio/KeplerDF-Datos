Logging a standard operational tasking over Santander de Quilichao in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We want to assess chlorophyll absorption level and work out an NDVI index along with surface reflectance for this area, aiming for an opportunistic capture that will give us valuable insights into the local ecosystem dynamics. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and we aim to deliver the products in three days' time in the evening, ensuring a comprehensive view of Santander de Quilichao's land cover characteristics.

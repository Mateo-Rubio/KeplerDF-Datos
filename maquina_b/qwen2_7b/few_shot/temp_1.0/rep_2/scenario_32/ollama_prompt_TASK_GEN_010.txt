Logging a low priority importance tasking operation for the future capture of San Vicente del Caguán in Colombia, part of our flexible schedule background survey tasks. Our objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis that evaluates chlorophyll absorption levels and calculates the NDVI index along with surface reflectance. This serves as an opportunistic tracking task to assess land usage without imposing strict operational constraints. The products are needed by the scheduled deadline, which falls in four days during the overnight period.

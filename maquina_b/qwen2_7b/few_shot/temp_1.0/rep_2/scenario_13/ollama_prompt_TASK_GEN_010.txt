Logging a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Puerto Rico in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance to provide comprehensive insight into the vegetation health across the region. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, with the deadline set for three days hence in the morning. The delivery should incorporate detailed data reflecting the spectral properties of the vegetation and aid in agricultural planning and land management practices.

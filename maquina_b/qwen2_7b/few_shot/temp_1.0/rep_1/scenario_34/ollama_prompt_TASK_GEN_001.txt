Placing a standard operational tasking over Vista Hermosa in Colombia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We're looking to capture near-infrared reflection for a water body boundary mapping to delineate the limits of the coastal lagoons and assess soil moisture conditions against heavy cloud cover. This is routine monitoring at normal operational status, and we require the products by the day after tomorrow overnight. The aim is to discern soil and water reflectance gradients that could influence agricultural development and coastal management plans.

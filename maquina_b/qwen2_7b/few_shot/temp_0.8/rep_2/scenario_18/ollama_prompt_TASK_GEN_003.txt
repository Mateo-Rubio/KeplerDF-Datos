Logging a low priority importance task over Singkep in Indonesia, to be treated as a flexible and opportunistic background filler. For the four days ahead, at the dawn of the morning window, we aim to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis with our VNIR sensor. We want to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance to understand the health and composition of the vegetation in this part of Sumatra. The mission is to capture these details and provide the products by four days from now during the morning hours, allowing for a drop or rescheduling at convenience if needed.

Logging a standard operational tasking over Rokan Hilir in Indonesia as part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters. We aim for a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption level and calculate an NDVI index along with surface reflectance to capture the health of agricultural lands. This is a routine monitoring activity at normal operational status, not requiring any urgency. The products are due for ingestion into our system in three days during the morning hours.

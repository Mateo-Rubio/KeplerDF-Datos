On the horizon of Colombia's picturesque landscapes, we aim to capture a true-color visual documentation with high-resolution daylight photography from our automated satellite constellation. This low priority importance request requires us to conduct a 'background survey' for opportunistic capture within a flexible schedule. Targeting the charming city of Murindó in three days and in the evening, we will unveil the beauty of this region through clear optical imagery as it illuminates with golden hour hues. Our mission involves no rush; thus, we consider this task as a 'background filler task' that doesn't compromise our operational efficiency but enriches the vast catalog of environmental studies and testbed tracking tasks at hand.

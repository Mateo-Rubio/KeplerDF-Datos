In response to an operational directive of low priority importance, we are tasked with capturing a flexible schedule, opportunistic survey of Indonesia's ecuatorial region on the target day, in three days around mid-day. This mission involves the use of a VNIR sensor for multispectral vegetation analysis, aiming to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index to understand surface reflectance. The request is framed as a non-essential background filler task, allowing for scheduling adjustments at convenience while ensuring that this background survey contributes to our understanding of the region's ecological dynamics.

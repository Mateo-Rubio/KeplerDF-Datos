Our mission involves the use of a near-infrared (NIR) sensor for an active radar scan, aiming to provide us with cloud-penetrating capture capabilities that will allow us to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels. This observational task is treated as a non-essential background filler task, where we can drop or reschedule it at our convenience if needed. The goal is to gather data that will help us discriminate gradients in soil moisture conditions without the need for strict deadlines or immediate processing times.

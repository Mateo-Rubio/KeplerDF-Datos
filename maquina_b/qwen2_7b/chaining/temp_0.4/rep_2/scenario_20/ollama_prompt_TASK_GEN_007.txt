In anticipation of capturing a comprehensive view of Puerto Libertador, Colombia, on our target day in four days in the morning, we are initiating a standard operational tasking request. Our primary sensor, the SAR system, will execute an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging to provide cloud-penetrating capture and ground geometry details. This mission is classified as low importance, allowing for flexible scheduling and opportunistic capture. The data collected will serve as a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We aim for high-resolution optical snapshots as daylight photography, clear optical imagery, or true-color visual documentation to ensure the integrity of our dataset. The deadline for this task has been set, and we are committed to adhering to it without any reevaluation or adjustment.

In the heart of Indonesia's equatorial region, where lush green landscapes meet the horizon, our mission is to capture a near-infrared reflection snapshot during the day after tomorrow's afternoon. This low priority importance task aims to assess soil moisture and water body boundaries in an opportunistic manner. As part of our flexible schedule, this background survey will serve as a testbed tracking task for understanding the unique environmental conditions of this tropical area. The data collected will provide insights into soil health and water management without imposing any strict deadlines or contractual requirements, allowing us to prioritize other missions with higher importance levels.

In three days around mid-day, we're tasked with capturing a low priority importance, flexible schedule background survey of Indonesia's Ecuatorial region using near-infrared (NIR) sensors. Our mission aims to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels for this operational tasking, part of a baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. This "non-essential background filler task" is treated as a testbed tracking task, with an explicit note to drop or reschedule at convenience if needed. The goal is to gather data for a daylight photography-like high-resolution optical snapshot, focusing on clear optical imagery and true-color visual documentation of the region's landscape under these conditions.

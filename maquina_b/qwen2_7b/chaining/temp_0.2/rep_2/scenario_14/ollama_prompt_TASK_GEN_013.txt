In preparation for an upcoming opportunity to capture critical data on the environmental dynamics of Colombia's Suratá region, we are initiating a standard operational tasking for our automated satellite constellation planner. The mission involves conducting an active radar scan using microwave surface imaging technology, aimed at cloud-penetrating capture and ground geometry assessment. This request is framed as a low priority importance task with flexible scheduling considerations, serving as a non-essential background filler task that can be dropped or rescheduled at convenience. The primary objective is to gather detailed information on the region's topography and surface characteristics under real-world conditions, providing invaluable insights for our baseline pipeline tracking setup.

The operational context is set for three days ahead, with the target day scheduled around mid-day. This timeframe ensures optimal lighting conditions for our SAR sensor to capture high-quality imagery without interference from atmospheric conditions or solar zenith angle issues that could affect data accuracy and utility. The task will contribute to our ongoing efforts in monitoring environmental changes, supporting decision-making processes, and enhancing our understanding of the region's natural resources.

As part of this mission, we are requesting a thermal mapping capability to capture heat signatures and surface temperature profiles, which will aid in detecting thermal anomalies and mapping heat emissions across the region. This aspect complements our multispectral vegetation analysis objectives using VNIR sensors, aiming to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance. Additionally, we are including near-infrared reflection capabilities to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels, providing a comprehensive view of environmental conditions.

In summary, this task request is designed to gather high-resolution optical snapshots for daylight photography, true-color visual documentation, and thermal mapping data, all within the specified timeframe and operational guidelines. The focus remains on delivering valuable insights that support our ongoing research and monitoring efforts without compromising the integrity or quality of the data collected.

In response to a medium-importance operational tasking, we aim to capture a high-resolution multispectral vegetation analysis using our VNIR sensor. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves assessing the chlorophyll absorption level and calculating the NDVI index for a region in Colombia, specifically focusing on the city of Sabana de Torres. The objective is to gather detailed information about vegetation health and land use patterns using near-infrared reflection capabilities.

The capture will occur overnight, three days from now, adhering to the deadline set by an upstream module. This task is essential for understanding environmental conditions and supporting decision-making processes without disrupting regular operations. We will ensure that the data collected meets the required standards for accurate analysis and will be available for review upon completion of the mission.

In three days during the morning, an opportunistic background survey is requested for the region of Uribe, Colombia. The low priority importance task involves capturing high-resolution radar imagery using an active microwave surface imaging sensor. This mission aims to serve as a testbed tracking task that will be conducted in a flexible schedule without an immediate need for urgent execution. Expecting results that may help in cloud-penetrating capture and ground geometry analysis, this observation will contribute to the operational status and workflow updates of our baseline pipeline tracking system.

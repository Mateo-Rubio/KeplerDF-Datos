We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over San Gil, Colombia. The task involves capturing high-resolution optical imagery during daylight hours. We need these data products delivered with a strict deadline set for the afternoon of the day after tomorrow, ensuring they are ingested into the pipeline before that time.

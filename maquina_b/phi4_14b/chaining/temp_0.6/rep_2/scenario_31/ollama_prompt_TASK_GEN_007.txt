We are initiating a standard operational tasking for a thermal mapping mission over Mapiripán, Colombia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking, involving fixed parameters that align with our normal operational status and workflow update. The objective is to capture a detailed heat signature and surface temperature profile using thermal infrared imaging. This will help in identifying and documenting any thermal anomalies within the region. The data capture needs to be completed in three days during the afternoon, ensuring timely data availability for subsequent analysis and reporting.

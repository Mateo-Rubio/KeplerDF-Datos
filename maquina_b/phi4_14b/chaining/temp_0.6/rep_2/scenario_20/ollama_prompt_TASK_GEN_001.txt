We have a standard operational tasking to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over the region of San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and involves capturing chlorophyll absorption levels to calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. It's important that this mission is included in our normal operational status workflow update with fixed parameters, ensuring it aligns seamlessly with our scheduled tasks. The data delivery deadline is set for "in three days overnight," providing us with ample time to process and validate the results effectively.

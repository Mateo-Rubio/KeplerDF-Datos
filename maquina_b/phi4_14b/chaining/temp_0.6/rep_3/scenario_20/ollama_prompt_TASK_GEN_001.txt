We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over San Carlos de Guaroa in Colombia. This routine monitoring forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Our goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance using VNIR technology. Please ensure that this task is scheduled as a workflow update within the commercial tasking framework. The data must be delivered in three days overnight.

We are initiating a standard operational tasking to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis over the region of Norte, Italy. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for this area, providing us with valuable insights into the vegetation health and surface reflectance. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup and involves fixed parameters representing normal operational status within our routine monitoring workflow. We expect the data delivery by in three days during the afternoon.

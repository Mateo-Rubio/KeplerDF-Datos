We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner. The mission involves capturing near-infrared reflection data over the Kepulauan Meranti region in Indonesia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, focusing on water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment to discriminate gradients. We aim to complete this routine monitoring task with fixed parameters representing normal operational status, ensuring a workflow update. The data must be delivered by "in four days in the morning" to meet our operational requirements.

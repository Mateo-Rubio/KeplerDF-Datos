We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Pavullo nel Frignano, Italy. This mission involves capturing near-infrared reflection data to assist in water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, we require this task to be executed as a normal operational status update within the workflow. The delivery deadline for this task is in three days around mid-day, ensuring timely data availability for further analysis.

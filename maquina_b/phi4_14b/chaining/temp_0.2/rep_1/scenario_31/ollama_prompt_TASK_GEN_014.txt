We have an absolute institutional priority to conduct a thermal mapping mission over the northern region of Italy. This mission is a binding contract requirement involving a critical payload capture using thermal infrared sensors. The objective is to obtain a comprehensive surface temperature profile and detect any thermal anomalies in this area. Given the high importance of this task, it is imperative that we execute this operation urgently and without preemption. The data must be available no later than "in three days in the evening" to ensure timely analysis and decision-making.

We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Arsiero, Italy. This involves a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index in the region. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, this task represents a normal operational status update within our workflow. The data capture is scheduled for in four days in the morning, ensuring timely integration into our ongoing environmental monitoring efforts.

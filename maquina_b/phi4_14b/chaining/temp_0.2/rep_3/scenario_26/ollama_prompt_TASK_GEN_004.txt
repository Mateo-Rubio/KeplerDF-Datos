We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Lebrija, Colombia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The objective is to conduct a near-infrared reflection analysis for water body boundary mapping and soil moisture assessment. We aim to capture detailed gradients in these areas using the satellite's capabilities. Please ensure that this task is scheduled for tomorrow in the evening, as this is the upper limit for data availability.

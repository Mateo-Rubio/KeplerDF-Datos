We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner. The mission involves conducting a multispectral vegetation analysis over the region of Arsiero in Italy. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking and involves capturing data to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index using VNIR sensors. As this is a routine monitoring activity, it falls under our commercial tasking with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The data capture needs to be completed in four days in the morning to ensure timely integration into our workflow update.

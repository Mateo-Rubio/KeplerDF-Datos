We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Santander de Quilichao in Colombia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status and a standard workflow update. The mission involves capturing a high-resolution optical snapshot using the VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. Please ensure that this task is scheduled for execution in three days in the evening.

We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Banyuasin, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves capturing a detailed NDVI index and assessing surface reflectance using VNIR technology to evaluate the chlorophyll absorption levels in the region. We require this data to be delivered in three days around mid-day, ensuring it aligns with our workflow update schedule.

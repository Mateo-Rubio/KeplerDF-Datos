We have a standard operational tasking in place for a commercial mission over Indonesia, specifically focusing on the city of Ecuatorial. This routine monitoring forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Our objective is to conduct thermal mapping to capture a heat signature and surface temperature profile, which will be crucial for thermal anomaly detection and mapping heat emissions in this region. The data delivery deadline is set for "in four days in the morning," ensuring timely access to the necessary information for our ongoing analysis.

We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Puerto Rico, Colombia. This task is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup, with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and a workflow update. Our objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. We aim to capture high-quality surface reflectance data for this region. The mission needs to be completed in three days in the morning, ensuring timely data availability for further analysis.

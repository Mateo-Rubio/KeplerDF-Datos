We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner involving Venaco, France. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The mission involves a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index and surface reflectance in the area. Please ensure that this task is scheduled for data capture in three days in the morning, as this is the upper limit for data availability.

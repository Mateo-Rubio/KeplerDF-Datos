We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial mission involving baseline pipeline tracking in the region of Italy, specifically targeting the city of Norte. The objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using the VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. This task is part of our routine monitoring efforts and adheres to fixed parameters representing normal operational status. Please ensure that data capture is completed in three days during the afternoon, as this is the upper limit for data availability.

We have a standard operational tasking for a thermal mapping mission over the region of Norte in France. This routine monitoring activity is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The objective is to capture a heat signature and surface temperature profile using thermal imaging technology. We are aiming for a comprehensive thermal anomaly detection and mapping of heat emissions across the area. The data collection should be completed in three days during the afternoon, ensuring timely delivery as part of our workflow update.

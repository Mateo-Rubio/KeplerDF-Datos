We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis over Trujillo, Colombia. This mission involves capturing data to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for surface reflectance, forming part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. The imagery is essential for routine monitoring and updating our workflow. We require this task to be completed by the day after tomorrow overnight, ensuring timely data availability for subsequent analysis.

We have a standard operational tasking for a routine monitoring mission over Saas-Almagell, Switzerland. This is part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters, representing normal operational status within our workflow update. The objective is to utilize the near-infrared reflection capabilities of our sensors to map water body boundaries and assess soil moisture levels in this region. We need this data for a comprehensive analysis of environmental conditions. Please ensure that the task is scheduled to be completed by the day after tomorrow in the evening, as this is the upper limit for data availability.

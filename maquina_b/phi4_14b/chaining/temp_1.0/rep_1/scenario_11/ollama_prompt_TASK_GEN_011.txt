We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner that involves monitoring the region around Tagliole, Italy. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters, we request a routine multispectral vegetation analysis in this area using the primary sensor designated for this mission. The goal is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the vegetation NDVI index, which is crucial for understanding surface reflectance dynamics in this region. This task is part of our commercial tasking and represents normal operational status as a workflow update. The data must be collected and delivered in three days overnight to meet our scheduling requirements.

We are requesting a standard operational tasking for routine monitoring in the Rokan Hilir area of Indonesia. This mission is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status and involves a commercial tasking component. Our objective is to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR sensors to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. We aim for a clear collection of multispectral data that will support our ongoing monitoring efforts. The task must be completed by in three days in the morning, aligning with our planned workflow update.

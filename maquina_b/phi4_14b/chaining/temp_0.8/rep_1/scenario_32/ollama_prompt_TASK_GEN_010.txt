We have a low-priority task available for monitoring vegetation health in the San Vicente del Caguán area of Colombia. This is an opportunistic capture using the VNIR sensor, aiming to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index for multispectral vegetation analysis. Given its lower urgency, this task can be flexibly scheduled or rescheduled as needed, and operators are encouraged to view it as a background survey or testbed tracking task.

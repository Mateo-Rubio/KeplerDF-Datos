This is an absolute institutional priority request for a critical payload capture involving a thermal mapping mission over the region of Bolívar, Colombia. Our objective is to achieve a comprehensive heat signature and surface temperature profile analysis, essential for detecting thermal anomalies. The task requires the utilization of the thermal infrared sensor to map heat emissions accurately. Given the high importance of this mission allocation, it is framed as a binding contract requirement with an urgent operational execution mandate. This operation must be locked in without preemption and completed in three days in the evening, ensuring data availability within the specified time frame.

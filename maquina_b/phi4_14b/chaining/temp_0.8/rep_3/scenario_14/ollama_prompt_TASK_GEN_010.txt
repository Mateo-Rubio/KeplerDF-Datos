We have an absolute institutional priority to capture critical payload data over Andina, Colombia. This mission is a binding contract requirement involving a high-priority multispectral vegetation analysis using VNIR to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index. It's essential for understanding local vegetation dynamics with true color visual documentation of surface reflectance. Given the nature of this task as a standard operational tasking, the satellite constellation must adhere to an un-preemptable mission lock ensuring that data is acquired by tomorrow overnight, with no possibility of rescheduling.

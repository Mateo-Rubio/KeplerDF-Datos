We have a standard operational tasking for our satellite constellation planner to focus on Prati di Tivo in Italy. This routine monitoring forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking with fixed parameters to maintain normal operational status and provide a workflow update. The mission involves utilizing the VNIR sensor to conduct a multispectral vegetation analysis. Our objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate an NDVI index, which will help us understand the health and reflectance of the vegetation in that area. This task must be executed with precision, and the data needs to be delivered in four days in the morning, as per our agreed schedule.

We have a standard operational tasking for a commercial mission focusing on Santander de Quilichao, Colombia. This involves a multispectral vegetation analysis using a VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate the NDVI index over the area. As part of our baseline pipeline tracking, this is a routine monitoring activity with fixed parameters indicating normal operational status. The data capture must be completed by "in three days in the evening" to ensure it fits within our workflow update schedule.

We have a standard operational tasking for the Tagliole region in Italy, focusing on a multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels. This involves capturing data using the VNIR sensor for accurate vegetation NDVI index calculations and surface reflectance. The observation is scheduled to take place overnight, with a deadline to deliver the collected data products in three days. This task forms part of our baseline pipeline tracking and represents a routine monitoring activity with fixed parameters reflecting normal operational status.

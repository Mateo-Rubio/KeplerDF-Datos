We have a task centered around Pradis di Sopra in Italy. It's quite an open request, with flexibility as our guiding principle, so please feel free to manage it with convenience in mind. Our aim is to take advantage of the next available opportunity to get a high-resolution optical snapshot over this area. Think of it as capturing clear and vibrant daytime photography of the landscape. While we'd love for this to happen sooner rather than later, it's scheduled for "the day after tomorrow" during the "overnight". If there are more pressing missions at hand or constraints on the satellite constellation, we completely understand if this task needs to be postponed or dropped altogether. It's a non-essential background filler task, and while we hope to see the fruits of such imagery, it's not critical for our operations at this time.

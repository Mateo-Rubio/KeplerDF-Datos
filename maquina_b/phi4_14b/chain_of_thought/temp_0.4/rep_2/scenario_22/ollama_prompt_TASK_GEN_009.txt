We have a standard operational tasking for a multispectral vegetation analysis in Banyuasin, Indonesia. This routine monitoring is part of our baseline pipeline tracking setup with fixed parameters representing normal operational status. We require a high-resolution optical snapshot using the VNIR sensor to assess chlorophyll absorption levels and calculate a vegetation NDVI index. The data products must be delivered in three days, specifically around mid-day, as this timing is crucial for capturing accurate surface reflectance under optimal lighting conditions.

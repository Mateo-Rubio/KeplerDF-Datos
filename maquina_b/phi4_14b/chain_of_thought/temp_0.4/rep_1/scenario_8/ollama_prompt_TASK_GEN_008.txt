We have an absolute institutional priority mission to Greve in Chianti, Italy. This mission involves a critical payload capture using a multispectral vegetation analysis sensor. The objective is to assess the chlorophyll absorption level and calculate the NDVI index for this region. This task is part of a binding contract requirement and must be executed with an urgent operational execution. The data products are required in four days, specifically during the overnight period. This mission lock is un-preemptable, ensuring that all resources are allocated to meet this high-importance directive.

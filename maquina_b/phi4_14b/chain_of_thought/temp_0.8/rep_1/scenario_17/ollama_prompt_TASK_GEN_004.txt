We have a non-essential background filler task for a flexible schedule that involves an opportunistic capture of vegetation health in Caramanta, Colombia. This task is designed as a low priority importance activity, allowing for flexibility to drop or reschedule at convenience. The objective is to perform multispectral vegetation analysis to assess chlorophyll absorption levels using near-infrared reflection, which will help calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. We aim to achieve this in three days, specifically targeting data acquisition in the evening.

This mission is an absolute institutional priority involving a critical payload capture of a multispectral vegetation analysis in the region of Chivatá, Colombia. The objective is to assess chlorophyll absorption levels using a VNIR sensor to calculate a vegetation NDVI index and surface reflectance. This task must be conducted during the afternoon, with data delivery strictly required in three days. The operation is a binding contract requirement that demands an urgent operational execution with an un-preemptable mission lock.
